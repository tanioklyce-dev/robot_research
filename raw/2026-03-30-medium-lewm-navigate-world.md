# LeWorldModel — Navigate the World from Raw Pixels

Short (6 min) blog post. Partially paywalled — body cuts off after the "Simple Intelligence" section header.

## Accessible content
- Frames LeWM as "the first stable, end-to-end framework that learns how the world moves and reacts using video frames and actions"
- Research team: NYU, Mila, and other institutions, including Yann LeCun
- Emphasis on learning physics from raw pixels without hand-coded rules
- LeWM "makes it faster and easier for robots to imagine their way to a goal"
