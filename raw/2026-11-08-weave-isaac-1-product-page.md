Frequently asked questions
What is Isaac 1?
Isaac 1 is our mobile robot designed from the ground up for the home. It handles tedious household tasks so you don't have to.
What can it do?
Isaac 1's capabilities are grouped by feature area:
Laundry Flow — Isaac 1 handles much of the laundry cycle for you:
Finds and picks up dirty clothes
Handles loaded hampers
Folds and puts clothes away
Daily Reset — Isaac 1 resets your rooms so you come back to spaces ready to be lived in:
Makes beds
Fixes pillows and blankets
Puts kids' and pets' toys, shoes, and clutter back where they belong
Depending on the home in question, Isaac 1 may be able to help with even more within each feature area (such as loading and unloading clothes from washer/dryer machines). No matter what, Isaac 1 grows more capable over time as we roll out improvements over the air.
When does Isaac 1 ship?
First shipments begin in fall of 2026. Deliveries to California come first, with broader U.S. availability following through 2027.
How much does it cost?
Isaac 1 has two payment options for home customers: $449/month on our subscription plan, or $7,999 up-front. You can order yours now with a fully refundable $250 deposit .
Please reach out to us for commercial use cases at [email protected] .
What happens after I pre-order?
Expect a confirmation and thank-you email from us shortly after your pre-order.
Then, as your delivery date approaches, we'll reach out to you again to coordinate which of two ways you'll have the option of seeing Isaac 1 in action before taking delivery: an in-person visit and demo at a Weave location or viewing one remotely over video call! We'll also use this time to understand your top priorities for Isaac in your home.
How do I pre-order?
Place a fully refundable $250 deposit on our website. The deposit reserves your place in line and is refundable any time until your Isaac 1 ships.
Is Isaac 1 teleoperated?
Isaac 1 is autonomous for Laundry Flow and Daily Reset by default, with teleoperation assistance when needed to guarantee we complete tasks.
How do I control Isaac 1?
You control Isaac 1 through our smartphone companion app. You can request a task to be completed on-demand or at a scheduled time, whether you're at home or away.

Frequently asked questions
What is Isaac 0?
Isaac 0 is a stationary laundry folding robot. It's an early-release prototype that Weave is shipping in limited quantities across California now.
What can it do?
Isaac 0 folds your household laundry. It can fold nearly everything you give it:
T-shirts, long-sleeves
Hoodies, sweaters
Pants, shorts
Undergarments
Towels
Pillow cases
Isaac 0 can group socks and inside-out most clothes. Isaac 0 does not yet fold large blankets or bed sheets.
We're continuously expanding coverage to more garment types and edge cases as the system learns.
How can I order Isaac 0?
If you're in California, you can place an order today on our website.
How much does it cost?
Customers who want an Isaac 0 for their home will have two payment options as their unit nears production, at which point the Weave team will reach out to confirm their preference. They can choose our subscription path for $249/month, or pay $3,999 up-front and $49/month for priority delivery and a lower monthly rate.
Please reach out to us for commercial use cases at [email protected] .
Why are you shipping only to California?
We are a young company in San Francisco. Focusing first on California will help us ensure our first customers have the best experience possible before we deliver to more regions.
If I order an Isaac 0 now, can I upgrade to Isaac 1 when it's launched?
Yes, and Isaac 0 customers will receive preferred pricing for their upgrade. The details of the upgrade program will be available when Isaac is launched in 2026.
How do I use Isaac 0?
You'll control your Isaac 0 through our iOS companion app.
Is Isaac 0's companion app available on Android?
No, our companion app is currently only available on iOS. We're working on Android support and will announce when it's available.
Where does Isaac 0 go in my home?
Isaac 0 only needs about a 6' x 5' space (this includes a table to work on) and a stable internet connection to start working for you.
You can place your Isaac 0 anywhere you're comfortable leaving it to work unattended for you for hours.
Do I need to do anything special to "train" it?
No. Your Isaac 0 will fold for you on day one. It will improve over time through normal use and software updates.
Is Isaac 0 teleoperated?
Isaac 0 blends autonomy with teleoperation (remote assistance by a Weave specialist) to guarantee that we complete every fold. The exact percentage of each depends on the kind of clothing and how much of it we've folded before. T-shirts, shorts, and long-sleeves we often fold almost entirely autonomously; other clothes require more assistance.
Isaac 0 will only get better at folding on its own over time, and for more clothes.
How often does it need help?
It depends on the load and the mix of garments. The goal is that most loads run start-to-finish without intervention, and that the need for assists drops over time as Isaac 0 learns from real-world use.
What information is available to the Weave specialist?
Our specialists are only able to see what is needed to complete the sub-in: feeds from the head and wrist along with diagnostic information. No audio data is collected.
How long does Isaac 0 take to fold a load of laundry?
This depends on the mixture of garments and size of the laundry load. Isaac 0 will generally complete an average laundry load in 30 to 90 minutes.
How does Isaac 0 sort and stack laundry?
Isaac 0 currently sorts laundry according to each of the following garment categories:
Long sleeves and t-shirts
Shorts and pants
Hand towels
Body towels
Pillow sheets
Bed sheets
Can I cancel my order after I place it?
Yes, you can cancel your order at any time prior to delivery.
Does Isaac 0 come with a warranty?
Warranty for all ongoing support is included with every Isaac 0 order.
Can I give feedback on my Isaac 0?
We hope you do! We're excited to hear from you and continually improve your product experience as one of our first customers.
