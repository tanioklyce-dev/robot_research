# TurtleBot 4 Product Page

"The next-generation of the world's most popular open source robotics platform for education and research."

## Variants
- TurtleBot 4 Standard: OLED screen, OAK-D-PRO camera, mounting plate with accessible ports. Weight: 3.9 kg.
- TurtleBot 4 Lite: OAK-D-LITE camera, no display or mounting plate. Weight: 3.3 kg.

## Specs
- Dimensions: 341 × 339 × 351mm (Standard); 341 × 339 × 192mm (Lite)
- Max speed: 0.31 m/s
- Payload: 9 kg default; 15 kg custom
- Runtime: 2.5–4.0 hours
- Computer: Raspberry Pi 4B (4GB)

## Sensors
- OAK-D stereo camera (spatial AI)
- 2D LiDAR (RPLIDAR-A1)
- IMU, wheel encoders, infrared, cliff/bump/slip detection

## Software
- ROS 2 pre-installed and configured
- Ignition Gazebo simulation model included
- Open source (GitHub)
- micro-ROS support

## Base
iRobot Create 3 mobile base.

## Use cases
Education, research, autonomous navigation, mapping, payload integration.
