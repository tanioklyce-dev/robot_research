## Privacy Policy
Source: https://flourish-robots.com/privacy-policy

Privacy Policy
A robot with a camera moves around your home. You should know exactly what that means.
The robot does not record unless you say yes. Every recording session asks first.
Recordings you approve are sent to us and used to improve how your robot performs that task.
Live operation is not stored. When the robot is working, video passes between the robot and our servers to make decisions and is discarded. It is not kept.
We do not sell your data. Not to anyone, for any price.
The rest of this policy is the detail behind those four lines.
Flourish Robots Corporation 1111b St Governors Ave, Suite 54999 Dover, DE 19904, United States

1. Who we are
Flourish Robots Corporation is the controller of the personal information described here. Our address and contact details are at the end of this policy.
2. What we collect
Account and order information. Name, email address, shipping and billing address, phone number, and order history. Card details are handled by our payment processor and are never stored by us.
Recordings you approve. When you teach the robot a task, or when you approve a recording session, we receive camera footage, depth and sensor data, robot movement data, and the workflow steps associated with that task. This footage is captured inside your home and may show your rooms, your possessions, and any person or animal present.
Live operational data. While the robot is carrying out a task, sensor and camera data is transmitted between the robot and our servers so the system can decide what to do next. This data is processed in transit and is not stored.
Robot and app data. Device identifiers, firmware version, battery and motor telemetry, error and crash logs, task success and failure records, connection quality, and app usage.
Voice input. If you speak to the robot, we process what you say in order to respond and act.
Communications. Messages you send us, including through any community channel we operate.
Website data. IP address, browser and device type, pages viewed, and referral source.

The robot does not begin a recording session on its own. It asks, and you approve, each time.
Approval covers a session, not a permanent state. You can end a session at any time, and you can delete an approved recording afterwards from the app.
Other people in your home. Only you can give this consent, but the camera may capture your family, your housemates, your guests, and anyone else present. You are responsible for telling them that the robot has a camera and for obtaining their agreement where your local law requires it. Some U.S. states and many countries require the consent of every person recorded. Please take this seriously — it is your household, and we cannot manage it for you.

Approved recordings are used to fine-tune the performance of the task they relate to. This is how a workflow that works most of the time becomes one that works nearly always.

We do not use your recordings for advertising, we do not sell them, and we do not share them with other robot owners.
If you would rather not contribute recordings at all, decline the recording prompt. Teaching a task requires a recording session, so declining means that task cannot be taught — but the robot will continue to run every task it already knows.

Deleted recordings are removed from active systems immediately and from backups within 14 days. Data already incorporated into a trained model cannot be individually extracted; deletion stops any further use.
