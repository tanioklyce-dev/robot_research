# Assistive Robots Transforming Human Lives

Seven assistive technologies showcased at 2023 AI for Good Global Summit:

1. RELab Tenoexo (ETH Zurich) — lightweight hand orthosis for therapy and assistance
2. ReHandyBot — table-top robotic device for hand function recovery post-neurological injury
3. iReCHeCk (EPFL CHILI Lab) — robot teaching handwriting to children via interactive learning
4. Social Robot Haru (Honda Research Institute) — expressive robot for emotional connection and community engagement
5. I.R.I.S. / QT Robot — platform supporting older adults' sense of purpose and social connection
6. Buddy (Blue Frog Robotics) — emotional companion for education and elderly care; thousands deployed in French schools
7. Furhat (Furhat Robotics) — humanoid robot with advanced conversational AI; customizable via SDK

## Organizations
- ETH Zurich RELab: hand rehabilitation for spinal cord injury
- EPFL CHILI Lab: educational robot for handwriting
- Honda Research Institute: social interaction
- Indiana University Bloomington: elder care and social engagement
- Blue Frog Robotics: educational deployment
- Furhat Robotics: conversational AI platform

## SDG alignment
- SDG 3 (Health & Wellbeing): accelerates recovery, reduces healthcare burden
- SDG 4 (Quality Education): enhances interactive learning
- SDG 10 (Reduced Inequalities): promotes inclusive participation
