# Assistive Robotics Lab — Virginia Tech

## PI
Prof. Alan Asbeck (director)

## Mission
"Our goal is to help people to regain capabilities they have lost, or enable people to perform feats that were not previously possible."

## Target populations
Disabled individuals, rehabilitation patients, and physically fit users.

## Research focus
1. Designing human interfaces for mechanical and electrical systems
2. High-efficiency actuators and transmissions
3. Human biomechanics and body control strategies
4. Sensors and control systems for assistive device integration

## Research domains
- Exoskeletons
- Human motion sensing
- Haptic feedback
- Soft robotics
