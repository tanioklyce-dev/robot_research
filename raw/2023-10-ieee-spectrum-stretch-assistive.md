# This Robot Could Be the Key to Empowering People With Disabilities

## Key facts about Stretch
- $20,000 price (vs. PR2's $400,000)
- ~2 kg lift capacity
- Lightweight, movable by one person
- Limited DOF: vertical movement, extend/retract, wrist joint
- Inexpensive gripper from Amazon assistive tools
- Basic obstacle avoidance + depth camera
- "Assistive autonomy" — user-directed operations via customizable GUI

## Case study: Henry Evans (quadriplegic)
- Scratches itches (needed multiple times per hour)
- Moves blankets, retrieves items, transports laundry
- Plays cards, bowling, basketball with granddaughter
- Feeding, meal prep, delivering recipes
- Delivered flowers independently
- Restored agency, reduced caregiver dependency (wife Jane Evans)

## Limitations
- Cannot manipulate user's body/limbs
- No stair navigation
- Requires significant technical intervention
- Emotional support not replaceable
