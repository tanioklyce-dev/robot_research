FAQ
What can they do?
It’s very similar to a regular human cleaning. They’ll clean bathrooms, kitchens, bedrooms, living rooms, hallways, offices: dusting, wiping surfaces, cleaning toilets, vacuuming, mopping etc.
How do they come to my apartment? Can they walk up stairs, and what about elevators?
They are dropped off with a supervisor, and yes, they can walk up stairs and use elevators.
Do you bring your own equipment?
Yes, we do. We use Mrs. Meyers multi-purpose cleaner for surfaces, unscented Bona for mopping floors. Happy to use any of your special cleaners if you have preferences.
Are they good around pets?
In our experience, yes! Dogs are mostly curious, sometimes a bit anxious first, but they get comfortable very quickly. Some of them even offer their toys to play with them. Cats usually peek around the corner, don’t bother at all or just hide if they don’t want to be around, typical cat behavior :)
How many robots are there per cleaning?
We usually send 2 robots per cleaning.
Do I have to clean up beforehand?
No, not more than you usually do for any other cleaning staff. And during your booking you can let us know if there are any areas you don’t want us to clean.
Can I talk to the humanoids and can they respond?
Yes, you can talk to them and they will answer. Special requests, areas to avoid that you did not state during your booking, we got you covered.
Do I have to be present for the cleaning?
No, we only need access to the property. You’re welcome to stay of course. If children who require supervision will be home during the visit, you or another authorized adult must stay and supervise them for the entire visit.
Are the robots fully autonomous?
No, robots are operated by humans and AI. So the humanoids are supervised by a human that can decide when to let the robot do tasks autonomously and when to take over and steer them themselves.

Service Privacy Policy
Tau Robotics, Inc. · Last updated October 24, 2026. This policy covers bookings and cleaning visits; our website has its own Privacy Policy .
The short version
A Tau cleaning is performed by a humanoid robot that is operated in real time by a trained
human being in another location. That person sees your home through the robot’s cameras
while they work.
We record what those cameras see. We use that footage to run the cleaning and to train the
AI models that we hope will eventually do the job without a human operator. This is not
optional. It is how the service works, and it is why the service is priced the way it is.
The robot’s microphones are disabled. We do not record or listen to audio in your home.
Ever. The robot can speak to you through a speaker, but audio only travels one way: your
operator can talk, and cannot hear you.
The rest of this page explains exactly what we collect, who sees it, how long we keep it,
and what control you have.
1. Who we are
Tau Robotics, Inc. (“Tau,” “we,” “us”) is a Delaware
corporation operating a humanoid cleaning service in San Francisco, California.
Contact: bookings@tau-robotics.com
This policy covers our booking platform and cleaning visits performed in your home or
office. Our website is governed by a separate privacy policy .
2. What we collect during a cleaning
Video. Continuous video from cameras mounted on the robot’s head and
wrists, for the duration of the visit. This is a first-person view of your space, including
rooms, surfaces, objects, and any people present.
No audio. The robot’s microphones are disabled. We do not capture,
transmit, record, store, or listen to sound in your home, in real time or otherwise. The
robot does have a speaker: your operator can speak to you through it. Audio travels one way
only. They can talk; they cannot hear you.
Robot telemetry. Joint positions, forces, balance state, battery, and other
sensor readings from the robot itself.
Operator commands. The motion inputs our operator sends to the robot, and
the timing of those inputs.
Spatial data. Depth and geometry derived from the robot’s cameras,
effectively a three-dimensional record of the layout of the rooms it works in, and the
position of objects within them.
Service records. What was cleaned, how long it took, what went wrong, and
any notes the operator makes.
3. What we collect outside a cleaning
Booking and account information. Name, service address, email, phone,
access instructions, scheduling preferences, restricted-area designations. If you share
your WiFi network name and password so the robot can connect during visits, we store
those too.
Payment information. Payments are processed by Stripe. Your card details go
directly to Stripe and are handled under their privacy policy. We never see or store your

About indefinite retention. We want to be direct about this, because it is
the part of our service most likely to matter to you.
The video our robot records is not a byproduct of the cleaning. It is the training data.
There is no separate, sanitized copy. The footage from your home, as recorded and including
unblurred faces, is what our models learn from.
Training a model is also not a one-time event. Models are retrained, architectures change,
and data collected today gets used again years later against methods that do not exist yet.
Data that expires is data we cannot learn from twice.
So we keep it. We are not putting an expiry date on it and we are not going to imply one.
You can end this at any time by requesting deletion under §9, and we will honor that.
Absent a request from you, we retain this footage for as long as we operate.
9. Deleting your data, including an honest limitation
You can ask us to delete data from your visits at any time at bookings@tau-robotics.com. When
you do:
We delete the raw video and telemetry from our systems.
We remove the data from our training sets, so it will not be used in any future model.
What we cannot do: if footage from your home was already used to train a
model, that training has already influenced the model’s parameters. Those parameters
are not a database and individual visits cannot be extracted from them or subtracted out. We
will not use your data again, but we cannot un-train a model that has already been trained.

California treats some categories as sensitive, and two are relevant here:
Precise geolocation. Your service address is your home address.
Biometric and image data. Our cameras will capture the faces of people in

around them. This means images of you and anyone else present are retained as part of our
training data, subject to the retention periods in §8 and the deletion rights in
§9.

station inside our facility, for the duration of the visit it belongs to, and is not

text and consented to SMS/MMS, an operator may deliberately export an individual still frame
and send it to that contact, as described in section 3.

bound to use it solely for us. Their access is view-only; they cannot download or export
footage.
