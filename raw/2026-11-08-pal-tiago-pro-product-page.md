PRODUCT HIGHLIGHTS
Co-design Principles for Spatial Flexibility
TIAGo Pro’s hardware, based on co-design principles, provides a total workspace of around 7m³ and 23 DoF, enabling high coverage with minimal repositioning. With a vertical arm reach of 92 cm and 35 cm torso lift, it can access both floor-level and elevated surfaces.
-->
PRODUCT HIGHLIGHTS
Platform for Embodied AI
From integrating large language models, to leveraging perception, teleoperation and data analysis for adaptive behavior, TIAGo Pro enables researchers to explore how AI systems learn, act, and collaborate within human environments.
-->
PRODUCT HIGHLIGHTS
Safety and Security Features
TIAGo Pro is designed to operate safely in human environments. Its arms absorb energy upon impact, reducing the risk of damage during collisions. Integrated safety brakes ensure controlled, responsive manipulation.
