 Big Scenes. No Big Deal. 
Build worlds, not workarounds. Up to 128 GB of unified memory handles 3D scenes up to 90 GB, and RTX-exclusive features like OptiX and DLSS keep the ray-traced viewport moving as fast as you do.
 Full Quality, Zero Detours
Drop 4:2:2 footage straight from the camera into your timeline and start cutting in full quality. Hardware encode and decode for HEVC, H.264, and AV1 keeps editing smooth and exports fast.
 Every Take is Free
Generate images and video right on your device, as often as you like. No subscription. No per-prompt fees. No waiting in line. Just the take that finally looks right.
 Look Sharp, Sound Sharper
Watch, call, or go live—RTX Video sharpens what you're watching, and NVIDIA Broadcast cleans up your camera and mic in real time. Everyone hears you. Nobody hears the dog.

 Build It Here, Run It Anywhere

 One Stack to Run Them All
Build on CUDA, TensorRT, and NVFP4, the same stack the world's AI runs on. What you build here runs unchanged on NVIDIA GPUs anywhere.
 Model Behavior
Run models up to 120B parameters with 1M context. Up to 128 GB of unified memory fits the whole model, and a petaflop of FP4 performance keeps inference fast.
 All Local, All Yours
Run it locally and get the good stuff: instant iteration, no API bills, and data that stays on your machine. Need more scale? Offload to the cloud, and your code runs there unchanged.
