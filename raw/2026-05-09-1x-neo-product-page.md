# 1X NEO Product Page

Home robot for household chores and domestic companion role.

## Specs
- Height: 5'6" (~168 cm)
- Weight: 66 lbs (~30 kg)
- Lift capacity: 154 lbs (~70 kg)
- Carry capacity: 55 lbs (~25 kg)
- Battery: 842 Wh, 4-hour runtime
- Speed: 1.4 m/s walking; up to 6.2 m/s running
- DOF: 22 hand joints per side; 7 arm joints per side; spine + neck articulation
- Noise level: 22 dB (quieter than modern refrigerators)
- Safety: HIC <250, no pinch points

## Capabilities
- Autonomous household tasks: walk to people, put away dishes, clean bathrooms
- Voice interaction; mobile app control
- Remote piloting via VR
- Self-charging
- Operates as Bluetooth speaker
- "Visual and spatial awareness with memory" for personalized interactions

## AI
- Runs Redwood AI (1X's proprietary VLM)
- Built-in LLM
- "Expert Mode": 1X staff remotely supervise to teach new capabilities
- Early owners receive foundational autonomy that expands over time

## Pricing / availability
- Initial deposit: $200
- No mandatory subscription

1X Technologies (Norway/US). NEO Beta released 2024; NEO Gamma announced 2025.
