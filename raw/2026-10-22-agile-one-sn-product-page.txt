       Discover the features
            Head camera     The RGB-D camera on the head provides an overview of the robot’s surroundings, giving it visual and depth information about the wider scene.
    Onboard AI compute     NVIDIA Jetson Thor processes sensor data and runs robot inference in real time, directly on the robot. An Intel i3 N305 base computing module complements it. 
    Dexterous hands     Dual Wuji tactile five-finger hands for delicate manipulation.
    Whole-body control     Whole-body control for coordinated, human-like movement of the entire body.
    Wrist cameras     The wrist cameras capture close-up views during manipulation, providing visual information as the robot works with objects at close range. 
    Waist 3D LiDAR     The 3D LiDAR at the waist, together with RGB-D cameras at the waist and back, provides additional sensor coverage around the robot.

             Preconfigured with NVIDIA Isaac GR00T
 Agile ONE SN is preconfigured with NVIDIA Isaac™ GR00T software, including GR00T 1.7 and whole-body control. 
 The GR00T reference workflow covers the full development cycle, and URDF and USD assets bring the robot straight into NVIDIA Isaac Sim™ and Isaac Lab.
