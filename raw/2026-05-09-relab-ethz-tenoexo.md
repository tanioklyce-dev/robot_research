# RELab tenoexo — Robotic Hand Orthosis

Wearable robotic hand orthosis for individuals with sensorimotor hand impairment. Supports grasping during therapy and daily activities (eating, grooming).

## Specs
- Hand module weight: <150g
- Back module weight: 720g (motors, power, controllers; worn as backpack or wheelchair mount)
- Fingertip force: 5N per finger maximum
- Capability: lift everyday objects up to 0.5-liter water bottles
- Fabrication: 3D-printed + laser cutting
- Actuation: remote system with 3-layered compliant sliding spring mechanism
- Thumb module: actuated, enables both opposition and lateral grasping

## Research directions
- Unsupervised home-based therapy via mobile apps
- Intention detection strategies for user control
- Pediatric optimization (PEXO variant)
- Clinical evaluation across populations

## Results
"Immediate functional benefit in adults after spinal cord injury" and children with severe hand impairment.
