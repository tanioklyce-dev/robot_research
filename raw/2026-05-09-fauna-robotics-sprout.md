# Fauna Robotics — Sprout Creator Edition

"A modern platform for robotics development." Humanoid robot for developers building robotics applications.

## Specs
- Height: 107 cm
- Weight: 22.7 kg
- DOF: 29 total (6 DOF arms, 5 DOF legs, 2 DOF neck)
- Processor: NVIDIA Jetson AGX Orin 64GB
- Storage: 1 TB SSD
- Battery: 3–3.5 hours runtime, swappable
- Vision: ZED 2i stereoscopic cameras + 4 time-of-flight sensors

## Features
- Compliant locomotion with capable transitions and balanced movement
- Whole-body teleoperation; floor-to-countertop reach; integrated grippers
- Modular autonomy: navigation, mapping, human awareness
- Social engagement: interactive behaviors, conversation-driven interactions, dynamic facial expressions
- Safety: soft materials, minimized pinch points, compliant motor control
