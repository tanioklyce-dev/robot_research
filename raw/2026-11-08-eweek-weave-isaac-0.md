Weave’s $8K Laundry Robot Still Needs Human Help

If you were hoping for a home robot that could finally handle laundry for you, Weave Robotics has an answer — sort of.
The company’s new Isaac 0 folding robot is now shipping in the Bay Area, but it can’t do the job alone.
The roughly $8K device folds common garments in 30 to 90 minutes and improves through weekly AI updates. When it gets stuck, remote human specialists step in to correct mistakes in real time. Isaac 0 offers a candid look at where home robotics will stand in 2026: useful, but still dependent on humans behind the scenes.
Built to fold, backed by people
Weave announced Isaac 0 on Feb. 11 as the company’s first laundry robot.
“It’s already in our first customer’s homes less than a year and a half after Weave’s founding,” the company stated.
“Laundry is a universal time sink that’s taken for granted to be human-only work simply because an alternative hasn’t existed,” Weave emphasized. The company described folding as a bounded task with measurable outcomes, making it an ideal testing ground for robotics in the home.
Isaac 0 is a stationary robot that plugs into a standard wall outlet. Users drop in a load of clothes and return to neatly folded stacks. The system handles T-shirts, long sleeves, sweaters, pants, towels, and similar garments. It doesn’t fold large blankets or bed sheets, according to TechBuzz .
Weave acknowledged the robot’s limitations : “Because it’s an early, first-of-its-kind product, Isaac 0 won’t be perfect all the time,” the company explained.

Weave added that when the robot encounters a difficult garment or makes a mistake it can’t fix, a Weave specialist can remotely substitute in for a 5- to 10-second correction and hand it back off to Isaac.
The Register noted that teleoperators can access camera feeds from the robot’s head and wrists to guide those corrections, giving human operators visibility into the folding process.
According to Weave’s official website , Isaac 0 carries a list price of $7,999 with a $250 refundable reservation deposit. This comes with a two-year warranty and secures priority delivery. There’s also a $450-per-month subscription plan for early adopters looking for a pay-as-you-go option.

A hybrid model for home robotics
That human-in-the-loop approach reflects a broader pattern for the robotics industry . Companies are increasingly combining automation with remote human oversight to bridge capability gaps as AI models mature.
Folding laundry remains technically difficult as soft fabrics wrinkle, overlap, and crease easily. Computer vision systems struggle to identify garment edges in cluttered piles. By designing Isaac 0 as a stationary system, Weave narrowed the scope of the challenge but didn’t eliminate it.
Weave said Isaac 0 began as an internal prototype of its broader mobile robot program. Earlier versions operated in commercial environments for months, folding thousands of pounds of laundry before expanding into homes.
For now, Isaac 0 operates as a blend of automation and human oversight. The robot completes most folds independently, while remote specialists intervene briefly when needed.
Whether consumers accept that balance remains unclear, as Isaac 0 isn’t a fully autonomous home robot but a connected system dependent on people to deliver consistent results.

Frequently asked questions
What is Isaac 0?
Isaac 0 is a stationary laundry folding robot. It's an early-release prototype that Weave is shipping in limited quantities across California now.
What can it do?
Isaac 0 folds your household laundry. It can fold nearly everything you give it:
T-shirts, long-sleeves
Hoodies, sweaters
Pants, shorts
Undergarments
Towels
Pillow cases
Isaac 0 can group socks and inside-out most clothes. Isaac 0 does not yet fold large blankets or bed sheets.
We're continuously expanding coverage to more garment types and edge cases as the system learns.
How can I order Isaac 0?
If you're in California, you can place an order today on our website.
How much does it cost?
Customers who want an Isaac 0 for their home will have two payment options as their unit nears production, at which point the Weave team will reach out to confirm their preference. They can choose our subscription path for $249/month, or pay $3,999 up-front and $49/month for priority delivery and a lower monthly rate.
Please reach out to us for commercial use cases at [email protected] .
Why are you shipping only to California?
We are a young company in San Francisco. Focusing first on California will help us ensure our first customers have the best experience possible before we deliver to more regions.
If I order an Isaac 0 now, can I upgrade to Isaac 1 when it's launched?
Yes, and Isaac 0 customers will receive preferred pricing for their upgrade. The details of the upgrade program will be available when Isaac is launched in 2026.
How do I use Isaac 0?
You'll control your Isaac 0 through our iOS companion app.
Is Isaac 0's companion app available on Android?
No, our companion app is currently only available on iOS. We're working on Android support and will announce when it's available.
Where does Isaac 0 go in my home?
Isaac 0 only needs about a 6' x 5' space (this includes a table to work on) and a stable internet connection to start working for you.
You can place your Isaac 0 anywhere you're comfortable leaving it to work unattended for you for hours.
Do I need to do anything special to "train" it?
No. Your Isaac 0 will fold for you on day one. It will improve over time through normal use and software updates.
Is Isaac 0 teleoperated?
Isaac 0 blends autonomy with teleoperation (remote assistance by a Weave specialist) to guarantee that we complete every fold. The exact percentage of each depends on the kind of clothing and how much of it we've folded before. T-shirts, shorts, and long-sleeves we often fold almost entirely autonomously; other clothes require more assistance.
Isaac 0 will only get better at folding on its own over time, and for more clothes.
How often does it need help?
It depends on the load and the mix of garments. The goal is that most loads run start-to-finish without intervention, and that the need for assists drops over time as Isaac 0 learns from real-world use.
What information is available to the Weave specialist?
Our specialists are only able to see what is needed to complete the sub-in: feeds from the head and wrist along with diagnostic information. No audio data is collected.
How long does Isaac 0 take to fold a load of laundry?
This depends on the mixture of garments and size of the laundry load. Isaac 0 will generally complete an average laundry load in 30 to 90 minutes.
How does Isaac 0 sort and stack laundry?
Isaac 0 currently sorts laundry according to each of the following garment categories:
Long sleeves and t-shirts
Shorts and pants
Hand towels
Body towels
Pillow sheets
Bed sheets

We hope you do! We're excited to hear from you and continually improve your product experience as one of our first customers.
