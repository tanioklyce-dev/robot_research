**Leslie Pack Kaelbling** — MIT professor; foundational work in reinforcement learning, POMDPs, and **task-and-motion planning** (TAMP) — the tradition that explicitly addresses the gap this wiki keeps hitting: connecting symbolic action specifications to continuous motion. Co-author on **[Generalized Planning in PDDL Domains with Pretrained LLMs](../sources/generalized-planning-pddl-llm-paper.md)**.

_Stub._ **TAMP itself is uncovered in this wiki** — a notable gap given how directly it bears on [symbolic task planning](../concepts/agents/symbolic-task-planning.md)'s "says nothing about motion" limitation.
