**Sam Kriegman** — researcher in **evolutionary computation, artificial life, and AI-designed robots**. Co-first author (with Douglas Blackiston) of both [Xenobots](xenobots.md) papers, done as a PhD student in [Josh Bongard](josh-bongard.md)'s lab at the University of Vermont in collaboration with [Michael Levin](michael-levin.md)'s group at Tufts.

## Role in this wiki
- **Lead author, [Kriegman et al. 2020](../sources/kriegman-2020-reconfigurable-organisms.md)** — the scalable pipeline that evolves living-machine designs in silico and builds them from frog cells.
- **Lead author, [Kriegman et al. 2021](../sources/kriegman-2021-kinematic-self-replication.md)** — kinematic self-replication in those organisms, with an evolutionary algorithm designing the amplifying C-shape.
- His thread is **using evolutionary search to co-design body + behavior** for machines that are then physically (often biologically) realized — a distinctive corner of the wiki's [artificial-life](../concepts/alife/artificial-life-and-self-replication.md) / [evolutionary-computation](../concepts/alife/evolutionary-computation.md) branch.
