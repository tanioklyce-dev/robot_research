# Xue Bin (Jason) Peng

Physics-based character-animation and humanoid-control researcher — **[NVIDIA](nvidia.md) (Canada)** and **Simon Fraser University**. One of the field's central figures in **learned physically-simulated motion** (the AMP/ASE lineage of adversarial motion priors) — the animation-side root of the RL motion-tracking methods this wiki tracks for humanoid [whole-body control](../concepts/robotics/whole-body-control.md). Senior author on **[MotionBricks](../sources/motionbricks-paper.md)** (SIGGRAPH 2026) and on **[SONIC](../sources/sonic-paper.md)** (NVIDIA [GEAR](nvidia-gear.md)) — the two NVIDIA token-latent motion models that bridge character animation and real-world [Unitree G1](unitree-g1.md) control.

## Why he matters in this wiki

- **Animation ↔ robotics bridge** — his work is where physically-simulated character motion (game/animation) meets humanoid robot WBC; MotionBricks makes this literal (one model driving both a UE5 demo and a G1).
- The **AMP/ASE adversarial-imitation lineage** he's known for is the explicit foil in [SONIC](../sources/sonic-paper.md)'s argument that dense motion-tracking supervision scales better than adversarial imitation (which mode-collapses).
