**Roberto Martin-Martin** — Assistant Professor of Computer Science at UT Austin. Works on embodied AI, manipulation, mobile manipulation, and HRI. Collaborator with [Yuke Zhu](yuke-zhu.md) at UT Austin's Robot Perception and Learning (RPL) lineage.

## Papers in this wiki
- **[Designing Accessible Robot Communication for Blind People (Huh et al. 2026)](../sources/huh2026-accessible-robot-comm.md)** — middle author; provides the robotics-systems half of the cross-institutional collaboration.

## Why it matters in this wiki
Martin-Martin is one of two senior UT Austin co-authors (with Zhu) anchoring the robotics side of the Huh et al. 2026 study, providing access to the [Franka Panda](franka-panda.md) and [Tiago](tiago.md) platforms used in the in-person observational study. Useful to track for forthcoming UT-Austin mobile-manipulation work touching accessibility.
