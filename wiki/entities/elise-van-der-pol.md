**Elise van der Pol** — Microsoft Research AI for Science (formerly University of Amsterdam, with Max Welling). The central figure of the **symmetry-in-RL** line: first author of *MDP Homomorphic Networks* (NeurIPS 2020), which builds policy networks equivariant to a known symmetry group of the MDP, and of *Plannable Approximations to MDP Homomorphisms* (PRAE, 2020), the contrastive abstract-MDP method that is the baseline in the one paper of hers this wiki holds. Co-author with Kipf of *Contrastive Learning of Structured World Models* (C-SWM, 2019).

## In this wiki

- [Learning Abstract World Models with a Group-Structured Latent Space](../sources/group-structured-latent-space-paper.md) (2025–26) — co-author. The paper reads as her programme's answer to its own cost problem: equivariant architectures *"come with additional computational overhead,"* so put the symmetry in the latent manifold instead.

## Why she matters to this wiki

The [geometric-priors concept](../concepts/learning/geometric-priors-and-equivariance.md) has three ways to encode a known symmetry — in the architecture (her 2020 work), in the loss, or in the latent topology (the 2025 paper). Her name is on both ends of that axis, which makes her papers the natural primaries for the whole page. **MDP Homomorphic Networks is not ingested** and is the first thing to pull if this thread continues.
