**Demis Hassabis** — co-founder and CEO of [Google DeepMind](google-deepmind.md); Nobel laureate (Chemistry, 2024, for AlphaFold). In this wiki he appears in his own voice once, at the [Davos 2026 "Day After AGI" panel](../sources/wef-davos-2026-the-day-after-agi.md), and that appearance matters for one reason: **he is the second frontier-lab leader, from the opposite camp to [LeCun](yann-lecun.md), to name world models as a precondition and robotics as what follows.**

## Stated positions (Davos, January 2026)

- **AGI timeline:** *"a 50% chance"* of a system exhibiting *"all the cognitive capabilities humans can"* by the end of the decade — restated as *"5 to 10 years."* Unchanged from Paris 2025. *"Maybe one or two missing ingredients"*: generating the hypothesis rather than solving the given problem; learning from few examples; continual learning; long-term memory; planning.
- **Physical AI is inside the definition.** *"I also include, by the way, for AGI, physical AI, robotics working"* — and it is the part where *"hardware in the loop may limit how fast the self-improvement systems can work."* Coding and mathematics close the loop first because they are verifiable; natural science and robotics need experiments.
- **World models and continual learning as the fallback:** *"These are the things that will need to be cracked if self-improvement doesn't deliver the goods on its own. And then I think things like robotics may have its sort of breakout moment."* No date on the robots.
- **Pace:** would prefer *"a slightly slower pace than we're currently predicting, even my timelines,"* and *"international… minimum safety standards for deployment."* Technical safety is *"a very tractable problem if we have the time. Maybe we don't have that."*
- **Labor:** near-term, normal disruption plus new jobs; entry-level effects begin in 2026; post-AGI, *"uncharted territory"* and then *"meaning and purpose."*
- **Great filter:** *"probably multicellular life"*; the Fermi paradox does not argue for AI doom because *"we should be seeing paper clips coming towards us."*

## Why he is on a robotics wiki

The [Gemini Robotics](gemini-robotics.md) line, the [ASIMOV](asimov-benchmark.md) safety benchmarks, and the [GR 2 safety report](../sources/gemini-robotics-2-safety-report.md) are his organisation's; this page records what the person at the top says the robots are waiting on. It is the same answer LeCun gives, and the two agree on almost nothing else about architecture.
