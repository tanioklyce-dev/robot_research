**Sensori Robotics** — a U.S. (Southlake, TX) robotics company selling **[Yuri](yuri.md)**, an integrated dual-arm bimanual-manipulation platform for Physical AI / [VLA](../concepts/learning/vla-models.md) research. Positions itself as delivering *"a complete Physical AI Robot, not a box of parts"* — hardware, software, and support bundled, "Built & Supported in USA" ([company site](../sources/sensori-robotics-yuri.md)).

## What they make

- **[Yuri](yuri.md)** — dual 7-DOF arms ("14-DOF"), in **Desktop** (benchtop) and **Mobile** (wheeled OpenBase) configurations. See the robot page for full specs.
- **Landscape Robotics** — a separate offering referenced at landscape.sensorirobotics.com; no detail on the main site.

## Open hardware

Maintains open repos rather than a fully closed product:
- **OpenArm+** — the extended-reach 7-DOF arm design.
- **OpenBase** — the wheeled mobile platform.
- github.com/SensoriRobotics + docs.openarm.dev.

This puts Sensori in the "commercial integrator on top of open hardware" niche — comparable to how [Hello Robot](hello-robot.md) ships supported Stretch hardware, or how the [LeRobot](lerobot.md) ecosystem vendors ([Seeed](seeed-studio.md), The Robot Studio) sell buildable open designs — but with force-feedback teleoperation and a day-one recording/training stack pre-integrated.

## Business model

- **Quote-only pricing** ("Tell us what you want to build… We'll follow up with details, pricing, and availability"). No public price, team, or funding disclosed.
