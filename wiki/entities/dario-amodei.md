**Dario Amodei** — co-founder and CEO of [Anthropic](anthropic.md). Previously named in this wiki only in the acknowledgements of [Claude's Constitution](../sources/claudes-constitution.md); he speaks in his own voice at the [Davos 2026 "Day After AGI" panel](../sources/wef-davos-2026-the-day-after-agi.md). He is the most aggressive timeline in the wiki — and **the one frontier-lab leader here who does not mention robots at all.**

## Stated positions (Davos, January 2026)

- **Timeline:** *"AI that's better than humans at everything in maybe one to two years, maybe a little longer."* Models doing *"most, maybe all, of what SWEs do end to end"* within *"6 to 12 months."* Nobel-level research across fields by 2026–27: *"I don't think that's going to turn out to be that far off."* The mechanism is the AI-builds-AI loop in code and research; hardware and training time are the parts that do not speed up.
- **Labor:** *"half of entry-level white-collar jobs"* gone within one to five years, reaffirmed; Anthropic itself expects to need fewer junior and intermediate people. The risk is that the exponential *"will overwhelm our ability to adapt."*
- **Chips:** *"not selling chips is one of the biggest things we can do to make sure that we have the time"* — the nuclear-weapons-to-North-Korea analogy against the bind-them-into-US-supply-chains argument.
- **Pace:** *"I prefer Demis' timeline. I wish we had 5 to 10 years."* Cannot slow down because *"we have geopolitical adversaries building the same technology at a similar pace."*
- **Risk:** not a *"doomer"*; Anthropic *"pioneered"* [mechanistic interpretability](../concepts/safety/mechanistic-interpretability.md) and now uses it on documented model misbehavior. A risks essay, the counterpart to *Machines of Loving Grace*, announced as forthcoming — framed on *Contact*'s *"how did you get through this technological adolescence without destroying yourselves?"*
- **Revenue:** 0 → $100M (2023) → $1B (2024) → $10B (2025), offered as the reason an independent lab survives to AGI.

## Why he is on a robotics wiki

As the boundary case. [LeCun](yann-lecun.md) and [Hassabis](demis-hassabis.md) both name world models as what robots wait on; Amodei's account of the next two years runs entirely through code and research and never reaches the physical world. Anthropic's own [robotics evaluations](../sources/anthropic-how-claude-performs-on-robotics-tasks.md) and [Project Fetch](../sources/anthropic-project-fetch-robot-dog.md) are the organisation's answer to that gap; this page records that its CEO's timeline does not include it.
