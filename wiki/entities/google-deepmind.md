Google's AI research lab. Maintains MuJoCo and [[mujoco-playground|MuJoCo Playground]], and co-developed the [[newton-physics-engine|Newton physics engine]] with [[nvidia|NVIDIA]] and [[disney-research|Disney Research]] under the Linux Foundation.

## Robotics simulation contributions
- **MuJoCo** — the open-source physics engine (acquired and open-sourced by DeepMind in 2021).
- **[[mujoco-playground|MuJoCo Playground]]** — robot-learning framework on MJX, presented at RSS 2025.
- **[[newton-physics-engine|Newton physics engine]]** co-development.
