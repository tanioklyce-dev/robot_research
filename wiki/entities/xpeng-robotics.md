**XPENG Robotics** — the robotics arm of Chinese EV maker XPeng; humanoid development plus embodied-AI research. Lead affiliation on **[UniT](unit.md)** ([paper](../sources/unit-paper.md), April 2026), with Tsinghua and the University of Hong Kong.

_Stub._ Real-world validation in that paper runs on the **IRON-R01-1.11** humanoid with a **50-dimensional action space** covering arms, hands, waist, head, and wrist poses — one of the highest-DoF real platforms in this wiki, and a useful contrast with the [SO-101](so-arm101.md)-class arms most VLA work here targets. The wiki has no coverage of XPENG's humanoid hardware line itself.
