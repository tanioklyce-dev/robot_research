**Hypershell X** — consumer dual-motor powered exoskeleton for walking assistance, controlled from a phone over Bluetooth.

## Security

An Alias Robotics assessment reported 12 Critical or High vulnerabilities, including Bluetooth control with no authentication, covering motor commands, and owner data available through unauthenticated APIs. Asked to engage, the company said it "is not pursuing vulnerability disclosure reports or external security research submissions" ([paper](../sources/cai-hacking-consumer-robots-paper.md)). The paper shows that motor commands are exposed but does not demonstrate an injury scenario.
