**Elephant Robotics** — Chinese robotics company making affordable collaborative robots and compound mobile manipulators for education and research. Product lines span tabletop arms (myCobot, myArm, mechArm, myPallitizer, ultraArm), mobile bases ([myAGV](myagv.md)), and dual-arm systems ([myBuddy 280](mybuddy-280.md)).

## Key products in this wiki

### Mobile platforms
- **[myAGV](myagv.md)** (~$1,498–$2,099) — Raspberry Pi 4B autonomous mobile base; ROS + 2D/3D navigation; pairs with arm attachments.
- **Compound kits** — myAGV + arm bundles for manipulation; up to $21,999 for the myCobot Pro 630 + Ranger Mini 2.0 Pro tier.

### Dual-arm robots
- **[myBuddy 280](mybuddy-280.md)** (~$1,619) — 13 DOF dual-arm system; 280mm working radius; Raspberry Pi 4B + ESP32; ROS1.

### Tabletop arms (not independently ingested)
myCobot 280 Pi, myPallitizer 260, mechArm 270, myArm, ultraArm — referenced as compatible with myAGV.

## Software ecosystem
- myBlockly (drag-and-drop visual programming)
- myStudio (cloud firmware / configuration)
- Python SDK (Windows / Linux / macOS)
- Open-source URDF files

## Price tier
Entry-level educational robotics ($1,498–$21,999). Significantly cheaper than research-grade arms (Franka Panda ~$30k+) but less precise and lower payload. Comparable tier to ROSOrin Pro.
