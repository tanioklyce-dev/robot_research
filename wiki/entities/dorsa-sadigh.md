**Dorsa Sadigh** — Stanford professor working on human-robot interaction, interactive imitation learning, and learning from human feedback; senior author on **[RT-H](rt-h.md)** ([paper](../sources/rt-h-paper.md)).

_Stub._ RT-H sits squarely in her line: its contribution is as much about **how a human corrects a policy** (in language, at the motion layer, cheaply learned from) as about the policy architecture. The finding that language-motion corrections beat teleoperated ones by 50 pp is an HRI result wearing a VLA's clothes.
