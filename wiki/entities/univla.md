**UniVLA** — "Learning to Act Anywhere with Task-Centric Latent Actions" (Bu, Yang, Cai et al., RSS 2025). A **7.6 B** generalist [VLA](../concepts/learning/vla-models.md) built around **task-centric latent actions** as a cross-embodiment interface.

_Stub — known here only through the [TurboVLA](turbovla.md) comparison table:_ **LIBERO 95.2 avg** (Spatial 96.5 / Object 96.8 / Goal 95.6 / Long 92.0) at **15.0 GB VRAM and 173.8 ms** on an RTX 4090.

Worth a primary ingest: the latent-action interface is the same idea family as LAPA-style latent-action pretraining and is directly relevant to the wiki's [cross-embodiment](open-x-embodiment.md) thread.
