# Team 254: The Cheesy Poofs

One of the most storied [FIRST Robotics Competition](first-robotics-competition.md) teams. Based at Bellarmine College Preparatory in San Jose, California. Founded in 1998 by NASA Ames Research Center. 2022 FRC World Champions. Known for publishing technical resources that raise the bar across the FRC community.

## History
- **1998**: Founded at Broadway High School by NASA Ames Research Center ([Team 254 Website](../sources/team-254-website.md)).
- **2000**: Relocated to Bellarmine College Preparatory.
- **2022**: Won FRC World Championship with robot "Sideways."
- **2026**: Robot "Overload"; 65-6-0 record; ranked #2 in FIRST California district (364 pts) ([Team 254 Website](../sources/team-254-website.md)).

## Technical contributions

### AI in FRC (2026)
Team 254 delivered the landmark presentation **"The Next Revolution: AI in FRC"** at the 2026 FIRST Championship Conference ([presentation source](../sources/team-254-ai-in-frc-presentation.md)):
- Advocated **Claude Code** for LLM-assisted FRC software development ("vibe-coding").
- Demonstrated **closed-loop AI agent systems**: agents that run WPILib simulations, analyze logs, and iterate code autonomously.
- Introduced **ClaudeScope**: a tool combining "SKILLS and a CLI" enabling LLMs to interact with robots.
- Sparked community discussion on the **wpilib-agent-tools** toolkit ([github.com/edanliahovetsky/wpilib-agent-tools](https://github.com/edanliahovetsky/wpilib-agent-tools)) — experimental Python CLI for evidence-driven agent-simulation workflows.

### Legacy technical resources
- Published code, CAD, and technical handbooks that many FRC teams treat as reference implementations.
- Previous Championship presentations on motion planning, control theory, and integrating computer vision with motion control.
- Hosts **Chezy Champs**, an annual off-season tournament at Bellarmine ([Team 254 Website](../sources/team-254-website.md)).
