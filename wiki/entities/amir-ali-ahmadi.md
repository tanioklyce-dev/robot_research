**Amir Ali Ahmadi** — Princeton University (ORFE). Works on **optimization, polynomial and semidefinite programming, and their applications to control and dynamical systems**. First author of **[Safely Learning Dynamical Systems](../sources/safely-learning-dynamical-systems-paper.md)** (Foundations of Computational Mathematics, 2026).

## Why it matters in this wiki
He anchors the **formal-methods pole** of the robot-safety cluster. Where the rest of the cluster measures safety with benchmarks and predicts it with anomaly detectors, this line asks when safe exploration admits a *certificate* — and answers with LP, SOCP, and SDP representations of the set of safe initial conditions, including the ability to **certify that safe learning is impossible**.

That last capability has no analogue anywhere else in the wiki: every other safety method reports a rate, never an impossibility proof.
