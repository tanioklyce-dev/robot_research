**Kevin Clark** — NLP / deep-learning researcher; Stanford NLP PhD (Christopher Manning's group). In this wiki, the author of the CS224n handout [Computing Neural Network Gradients](../sources/clark-computing-nn-gradients.md), a widely-used vectorized-backprop reference.

## Known work
- **ELECTRA** (Clark, Luong, Le, Manning, 2020) — replaced-token-detection pretraining; a more compute-efficient alternative to BERT's masked-language-modeling. His best-known contribution (outside this wiki's current scope).
- The **CS224n gradient notes** ([source](../sources/clark-computing-nn-gradients.md)) — Jacobian-based vectorized backprop; the practical companion to scalar autograd ([micrograd](../sources/karpathy-micrograd.md)) in [Curriculum Module 1](../syntheses/curriculum/curriculum-01-neural-networks.md).
