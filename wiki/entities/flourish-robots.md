**Flourish Robots** (Flourish Robots Corporation) is a consumer home-robotics startup with a San Francisco dateline, a Delaware incorporation and a Paris workshop. It makes [Flourish 1](flourish-1.md). It emerged from stealth on **2026-09-29**, roughly nine months after it was founded ([press release](../sources/flourish-1-launch-press-release.md), [Robot Report](../sources/therobotreport-flourish-one.md)).

## Company
- **Founded** January 2026 ([Robot Report](../sources/therobotreport-flourish-one.md)). **Flourish Robots Corporation**, a Delaware corporation using a registered-agent address in Dover, under Delaware law ([Terms](../sources/flourish-robots-website.md)).
- **Locations:** described as *"straddling San Francisco and Paris"* ([Robot Report](../sources/therobotreport-flourish-one.md)). The robot is *"designed and assembled in our workshop in Paris, with components coming mostly from Asia"* ([Clubic](../sources/clubic-flourish-1.md)). Data infrastructure is in the US per the [Privacy Policy](../sources/flourish-robots-website.md). Clubic's report of EU hosting conflicts with that.
- **Funding:** pre-seed **led by Families Fund** (founder Kartik Sathappan). No amount disclosed ([press release](../sources/flourish-1-launch-press-release.md)).
- **Team:** described as *"a tiny team"* hand-building the first 50 units ([Robot Report](../sources/therobotreport-flourish-one.md)). The size is not published. The site sells *"a direct line to the founders,"* plural, but names only one.

## People
- **Antoine Marcel**, founder and CEO. According to the [press release](../sources/flourish-1-launch-press-release.md) and [Clubic](../sources/clubic-flourish-1.md), he trained at École 42, has seven years in AI and automation, sold a prior company (**Joyger**, logistics automation), and then founded **Latice.ai** (voice-AI fine-tuning automation) with backing from **Kima Ventures** and The Quest. Self-described thesis: *"I've automated everything in my computer… But when I came home, the most painful stuff in my life was still there"* ([Robot Report](../sources/therobotreport-flourish-one.md)).

> [!note] One origin
> Every biographical claim above traces to the founder, through his press release or his interview. A web search on 2026-10-07 found no independent record of Joyger or Latice.ai. No separate person page has been filed.

## Strategy
- **Per-home fine-tuning over a generalist model.** *"Every home is different… Instead of waiting for a generalist AI model, Flourish fine-tunes per-task, per-home"* ([Robot Report](../sources/therobotreport-flourish-one.md)). This is the opposite bet to [Figure](figure.md)'s [Helix 2.5](../sources/figure-helix-2-5.md), which pretrains on a crowdsourced human-video corpus and reports zero-shot results in 30 unseen homes. Flourish expects the owner to supply the home-specific data and fine-tunes on it.
- **Bill-of-materials discipline.** No legs, no five-fingered hands, no 10 kg arms, a Raspberry Pi instead of a GPU (*"would immediately double the cost"*), and the claim that units are **sold at a margin** ([Robot Report](../sources/therobotreport-flourish-one.md), [CNET](../sources/cnet-flourish-1.md), [Clubic](../sources/clubic-flourish-1.md)).
- **Recurring revenue from compute.** The cloud plan costs $50/month after a 6-month inclusion. Approved owner recordings train fleet models ([site](../sources/flourish-robots-website.md)). An owner-built task store plus SDK adds a second flywheel.
- **Disclosure posture.** The Terms of Sale lead with what the product cannot do and that it will fail. The site revised a "runs on-device" claim within a week to match its Privacy Policy ([edition history](../sources/flourish-robots-website.md#edition-history)). The Privacy Policy still contains an unfilled `[PRIVACY EMAIL]` placeholder. For a company nine months old, the legal and disclosure documents are unusually candid and visibly unfinished.
