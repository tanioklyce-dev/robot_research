**Zipeng Fu** — Stanford CS PhD; **Mobile ALOHA co-lead** with [Tony Z. Zhao](tony-zhao.md) ([source](../sources/mobile-aloha-paper.md)). Stanford Graduate Fellowship recipient. Advised by [Chelsea Finn](chelsea-finn.md).

## Earlier: locomotion at CMU

Before the Stanford manipulation work, Fu co-authored **[RMA](../sources/rma-paper.md)** (RSS 2021) at CMU with Ashish Kumar, [Deepak Pathak](deepak-pathak.md) and Jitendra Malik — the rapid-adaptation quadruped controller that walks a blind [A1](unitree-a1.md) over sand, mud and oily plastic with sub-second adaptation. Worth noting on this page because it makes his trajectory **legged locomotion → mobile manipulation**, which is an unusual span; most researchers in this wiki stay on one side of it.
