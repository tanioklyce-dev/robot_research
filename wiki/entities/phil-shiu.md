**Philip K. Shiu** — Lead author on the [Shiu et al. 2024 *Nature* fly-brain paper](../sources/shiu-fly-brain-paper.md). Built the first **whole-brain leaky-integrate-and-fire dynamical model of an adult animal**, on top of the [FlyWire](flywire.md) connectome. Trained as a postdoc in **Kristin Scott's lab at UC Berkeley**; subsequently moved to **Eon Systems** (per the [Berkeley News writeup](../sources/berkeley-fly-brain-news.md), which interviews him as the lead).

## Contributions captured in this wiki

- **Lead author + maintainer**, [Shiu et al. 2024](../sources/shiu-fly-brain-paper.md) (*Nature* 634:210–219). LIF model on 127,400 FlyWire neurons in **Brian 2**; single free parameter; ~91% of 164 optogenetic predictions held; novel finding that Ir94e GRNs are aversive.
- **Maintainer** of the open-source codebase [philshiu/Drosophila_brain_model](drosophila-brain-model.md) (MIT-licensed). The implementation that makes "fly brain on a laptop" reproducible.
- **Public framing of the work** as a stepping stone toward mouse and eventually human connectomes ([Berkeley News](../sources/berkeley-fly-brain-news.md)): *"This really suggests that getting a mouse connectome, and eventually a human connectome, will be incredibly valuable."* Same article frames the work as *"an alternate way of getting to really good AI."*

## Affiliations

- **UC Berkeley** (Kristin Scott lab, postdoc) — where the work was done.
- **Eon Systems** — current affiliation.

## Why it matters here

- **Anchors the brain-side reproducibility surface.** The wiki's brain↔body integration story (see [Whole-organism agentic AI](../syntheses/agents/whole-organism-agentic-ai.md)) depends on having a runnable brain simulation; Shiu's released code is that artifact, and he is the person to track for follow-on work.
- **AI-bridge framing.** Among the Shiu paper's 27 co-authors, he is the one positioning the work explicitly in AI/ML terms. That framing is what made the [Berkeley News](../sources/berkeley-fly-brain-news.md) coverage relevant to this wiki's "alternate paths to capable agents" thread.
