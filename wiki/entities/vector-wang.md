**Vector Wang** (Gaotian Wang) — creator of [XLeRobot](xlerobot.md), an open-source $660 dual-arm household mobile manipulator that composes [SO-ARM101](so-arm101.md) + [LeKiwi](lekiwi.md) + [LeRobot](lerobot.md). Public-facing handle on X: `@VectorWang2`.

## Projects in this wiki

- **[XLeRobot](xlerobot.md)** (2024–2025–) — creator. Tagline: "Bring Embodied AI to Every Family Around the World." V0.3.0 released August 30, 2025. Apache 2.0.

## Why it matters in this wiki

Vector Wang is a useful exemplar of the **glue-the-existing-ecosystem-together** pattern that is bringing serious dual-arm mobile manipulation under $1k. The XLeRobot project doesn't introduce new motors, new chips, or new framework code — it identifies an existing low-cost arm (SO-ARM101), an existing low-cost base (LeKiwi), an existing IL framework (LeRobot), and engineers a clean integration with 3D-printed brackets, an IKEA cart, and tutorial documentation. The resulting platform is more capable than its constituent parts would suggest. A useful contrast to the integrated-vendor approach of [Hello Robot](hello-robot.md) ([Stretch](stretch.md)) or [Pollen Robotics](pollen-robotics.md) ([Reachy 2](reachy.md)).
