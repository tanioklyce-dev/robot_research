**K-Scale Labs** — YC-backed humanoid robotics startup. Shut down late 2025 after failing to close Series A funding. Notable as one of the few humanoid startups to produce a detailed post-mortem account of its failure ([Robot Report, March 2026](../sources/robot-report-kscale-labs-lessons.md)).

## Background
- Y Combinator backed
- Attempted to build affordable humanoid robots
- Shut down: late 2025
- Cause: failed to close Series A; runway exhausted

## What went wrong (per COO Rui Xu)
Six identified failure modes — see full analysis in [Robot Report source page](../sources/robot-report-kscale-labs-lessons.md):
1. AI overconfidence replacing hardware safety engineering
2. Misleading analogies ("iPhone moment", "hoverboard economics") guiding decisions
3. Supply chain treated as a one-time task, not a sustained capability
4. Hardware falsely treated as a commodity
5. Bad R&D prioritization while fundraising windows closed
6. Impossible timelines causing compounding failures

## Core diagnosis (Xu)
"Overconfidence in AI + underestimation of hardware complexity = systemic optimism bias that accelerated failure."

## Legacy
Some code open-sourced before shutdown. The post-mortem (Xu, March 2026) is among the most candid first-hand accounts of humanoid startup failure available publicly. Listed as a **mentor / partner** at the [Seeed × NVIDIA × HF Embodied AI Hackathon (October 2025)](../sources/seeed-embodied-ai-hackathon-2025-recap.md) — i.e., the company was still active in ecosystem-facing capacity weeks before the shutdown.
