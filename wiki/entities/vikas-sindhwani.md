**Vikas Sindhwani** — [Google DeepMind](google-deepmind.md) (Robotics at Google, New York). **The connective author of this wiki's entire robot-safety cluster**: he appears on all four ingested safety papers, spanning approaches that otherwise share nothing.

## Papers in this wiki
- **[ASIMOV Benchmark](../sources/asimov-benchmark-paper.md)** (CoRL 2025) — semantic-safety benchmark + generated robot constitutions.
- **[Predictive Red Teaming](../sources/predictive-red-teaming-paper.md)** (2025) — predicting policy vulnerabilities without hardware.
- **[Evaluating Gemini Robotics Policies in a Veo World Simulator](../sources/veo-robotics-policy-evaluation-paper.md)** (2025) — generative-world-model evaluation incl. safety probing.
- **[Safely Learning Dynamical Systems](../sources/safely-learning-dynamical-systems-paper.md)** (FoCM 2026) — provable safe exploration via LP/SOCP/SDP.

## Why it matters in this wiki
His authorship is what makes the cluster legible as **one program rather than four papers**: a benchmark for what a robot should judge, a predictor for where a policy will break, a simulator for testing what you cannot safely stage, and a formal theory for exploring with certificates.

It also sharpens the gap. The same researcher works on **conic-optimization safety guarantees for polynomial systems** and on **prompt-loaded constitutions for VLMs** — and no ingested source connects them. The [public safety framing](../sources/deepmind-gemini-robotics-safety-page.md) names the empirical work and omits the formal line entirely.
