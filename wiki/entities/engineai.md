# EngineAI

**EngineAI (众擎)** — a Shenzhen humanoid-robot company founded October 2023, focused on **highly-dynamic bipedal humanoids with full-stack self-developed joints**. Organizer of the [URKL](../sources/urkl-robot-combat-league.md) humanoid combat league and maker of the [T800](engineai-t800.md).

## Profile

- **Founded** October 2023, Shenzhen ([URKL coverage](../sources/urkl-robot-combat-league.md)).
- **Founder**: Zhao Tongyang — previously led Xpeng's humanoid-robotics program; earlier founded the Shenzhen startup Dogotix.
- **Team** ~110; **raised >$160M** (~1 billion RMB) to date.
- **Manufacturing**: launched a Shenzhen Intelligent Manufacturing Base (May 2026); production line in Zhengzhou rated at 5,000 units/yr, targeting 10,000-unit scalable delivery.

## Product lineup

- **SA01** — accessibility-oriented entry model.
- **SE01** — demonstrated one of the world's earliest human-like walking gaits.
- **PM01** — achieved the **world's first humanoid front flip**; initially priced under $15,000.
- **[T800](engineai-t800.md)** — high-dynamic, heavy-duty flagship; the [URKL](../sources/urkl-robot-combat-league.md) combat platform. (Debuted as the "T-8000" prototype at WRC 2025.)

## Notable

- Organizes the **[URKL](../sources/urkl-robot-combat-league.md)** (Ultimate Robot Knockout Legend) — the world's first humanoid-robot free-combat league — providing T800s free to 200+ teams as both spectacle and a real-world engineering testbed.
