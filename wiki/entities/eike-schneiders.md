**Eike Schneiders** — researcher at Aalborg University, Department of Computer Science (Denmark, as of 2021). Works on human–robot and human–smart-home interaction, qualitative HCI methods, and the everyday adoption of automation in private households.

## Papers in this wiki
- **[Domestic Robots and the Dream of Automation (Schneiders et al. 2021)](../sources/schneiders2021-domestic-robots-automation.md)** — first author. CHI 2021. Multi-method qualitative study of 24 Danish households using consumer vacuum / lawnmower / hybrid robots; introduced the **task fragmentation** finding and documented the strict task-division-by-household-member pattern that contradicts earlier Forlizzi work.

## Why it matters in this wiki
Schneiders 2021 is one of the **earliest deployment-reality** anchors in this wiki. Domestic vacuum and lawnmower robots are the only category of consumer robots that has reached genuine household saturation, and their adoption patterns prefigure many of the problems facing mobile manipulators and humanoids: task fragmentation, under-trust, the gap between marketed and actual autonomy, and uneven adoption within multi-person households.
