**Petter Ögren** — robotics researcher (KTH); co-author with [Michele Colledanchise](michele-colledanchise.md) of **[Behavior Trees in Robotics and AI: An Introduction](../sources/behavior-trees-book.md)**. The book's formal contributions — the state-space formulation for safety/robustness proofs, and the stochastic-BT-to-Markov-chain reduction that yields success probabilities and expected completion times — are the part of [behavior trees](../concepts/robotics/behavior-trees.md) most relevant to the wiki's [guardrails](../syntheses/agents/guardrails-for-robot-agents.md) thread.
