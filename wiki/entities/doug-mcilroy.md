**M. Douglas (Doug) McIlroy** — Bell Labs computer scientist, best known for inventing **Unix pipes** and writing core Unix tools (`diff`, `spell`, `join`, etc.); later at Dartmouth. In this wiki he enters as a co-creator of **[Darwin](../sources/darwin-1961-bell-labs-game.md)** (1961), the first self-reproducing-program game.

## Role in this wiki
- **Coded the umpire for [Darwin](../sources/darwin-1961-bell-labs-game.md)** (overnight, August 1961) and wrote the tiny 15-cell "hard-shelled virus" creature; **transcribed** the 1971 letter that is this wiki's primary Darwin source.
- Darwin is the **earliest ancestor** in the wiki's [self-replicating-program lineage](../concepts/alife/artificial-life-and-self-replication.md): Darwin (1961) → [Core War](core-war.md) (1984) → Tierra/Avida → [Computational Life](../sources/computational-life-self-replicating-programs-paper.md).
