**Karol Hausman** — formerly Robotics at Google; co-founder and CEO of [Physical Intelligence](physical-intelligence.md). Corresponding author on [SayCan](saycan.md) and co-author on two more of the lineage's foundational papers.

## Papers in this wiki
- **[SayCan](../sources/saycan-paper.md)** (CoRL 2022) — corresponding author.
- **[Inner Monologue](../sources/inner-monologue-paper.md)** (CoRL 2022) · **[Code as Policies](../sources/code-as-policies-paper.md)** (ICRA 2023) — co-author.

## Why it matters in this wiki
Like [Brian Ichter](brian-ichter.md), Hausman moved from the *LLM-plans-over-skills* program to founding the lab that builds **end-to-end VLAs** ([π0](pi-zero.md), π0.5). Those same VLAs are the models that score 0.00 under perturbation in [CaP-X](../sources/cap-x-paper.md)'s and [ASPIRE](../sources/aspire-paper.md)'s tables — while the approach he helped originate degrades gracefully. The wiki records this without irony: both lines are attempts on the same problem by overlapping people, and the 2026 comparison is a within-community result rather than a rivalry.
