**UniX AI Panther** — full-size wheeled dual-arm robot from Suzhou-based UniX AI, marketed for hotels, retail and homes.

## Specs

34 joints, 8-DoF arms, an 80 cm upper-body lift that reaches ground level, and a four-wheel-steer base. Dual-arm load is 12 kg on the Pro Max Mini ([launch](../sources/unix-ai-panther-launch.md)).

## Status

The vendor says global deliveries have begun and calls Panther the first service humanoid in real household deployment ([launch](../sources/unix-ai-panther-launch.md)). No price, SDK or independent home deployment was found as of 2026-11-08, so that claim stands as the vendor's alone.
