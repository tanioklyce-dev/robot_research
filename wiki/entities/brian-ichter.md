**Brian Ichter** — formerly Robotics at Google / Google DeepMind, later a co-founder of [Physical Intelligence](physical-intelligence.md). **Present on all four Google-lineage papers** in this wiki's [code-as-policy](../concepts/agents/code-as-policy.md) thread, and a corresponding author on SayCan.

## Papers in this wiki
- **[SayCan](../sources/saycan-paper.md)** (CoRL 2022) — corresponding author; affordance-grounded skill selection.
- **[Inner Monologue](../sources/inner-monologue-paper.md)** (CoRL 2022) — senior author; closed-loop feedback.
- **[Code as Policies](../sources/code-as-policies-paper.md)** (ICRA 2023) — co-author.
- **[Language to Rewards](../sources/language-to-rewards-paper.md)** (CoRL 2023) — co-author.

## Why it matters in this wiki
Ichter is the through-line of the 2022–2023 Google robotics-and-language program, and his later move to [Physical Intelligence](physical-intelligence.md) — the [π0](pi-zero.md) lab — puts one person on both sides of the wiki's central architectural argument: **LLM-plans-over-skills** and **end-to-end VLA**. That the same researchers pursued both is a useful corrective to reading the two camps as opposed schools.
