**Anna Choromanska** — NYU (Courant Institute at the time of the 2015 paper; Electrical and Computer Engineering by 2017). In this wiki, the author who carries [LeCun](yann-lecun.md)'s **statistical-physics thread** on the theory side: first author of [The Loss Surfaces of Multilayer Networks](../sources/choromanska2015-loss-surfaces-multilayer-networks.md) (AISTATS 2015), which maps a ReLU network's loss to a spherical spin-glass Hamiltonian, and second author of [Entropy-SGD](../sources/chaudhari2017-entropy-sgd.md) (ICLR 2017), which turns local free entropy into a training objective. Also coauthor of the COLT 2015 open-problem note on the same landscape (not ingested) and of elastic-averaging SGD (Zhang, Choromanska & LeCun, NIPS 2015; cited by Entropy-SGD as a replica-theoretic relative).

## Why she is on a robotics wiki

The two papers are the formal bridge between the spin-glass prehistory on the [energy-based-models page](../concepts/learning/energy-based-models.md) and modern training, and they pose the collapse question the wiki's JEPA thread has not answered — see [loss-landscape geometry](../concepts/learning/loss-landscape-geometry.md).
