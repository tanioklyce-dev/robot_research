**1X NEO** — household humanoid from 1X Technologies (Halodi Robotics, Norway). **NEO Beta** released 2024; **NEO Gamma** announced 2025. Notable for **OpenAI investment** and explicit consumer-household positioning (vs Figure's industrial-first or Atlas's research-only).

## Specs (NEO Beta, 2024)
- ~1.65 m tall, ~30 kg.
- Tendon-driven actuation (different design philosophy than rigid-actuator humanoids like Atlas / H1).
- Soft-touch surfaces for human safety.
- Cameras + microphones; conversational AI integration.

## Position vs other humanoids
- **Household / consumer focus.** Soft, quiet, designed for human-occupied spaces — a different design point from industrial-first humanoids.
- **Tendon drives, not rigid actuators.** Lower payload than Atlas / H1 but lighter, quieter, safer around humans.
- **OpenAI investor.** Strategic positioning around foundation-model-driven household assistance.
