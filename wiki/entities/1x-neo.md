**1X NEO** — consumer household humanoid from 1X Technologies (Halodi Robotics, Norway/US). Explicitly designed for home chores and domestic companionship. **NEO Beta** (2024); **NEO Gamma** (2025). $200 deposit; no mandatory subscription.

## Specs ([1X NEO product page](../sources/1x-neo-product-page.md))
- Height: 5'6" (~168 cm); Weight: 66 lbs (~30 kg)
- Lift: 154 lbs (~70 kg); Carry: 55 lbs (~25 kg)
- Battery: 842 Wh, 4-hour runtime
- Speed: 1.4 m/s walking; up to 6.2 m/s running
- DOF: **22 hand joints per side**; 7 arm joints per side; spine + neck articulation
- Noise: **22 dB** (quieter than a refrigerator)
- Safety: HIC <250, no pinch points

## Capabilities
- Autonomous household tasks (dishes, laundry, cleaning)
- Voice interaction; mobile app control; VR remote piloting
- Self-charging; Bluetooth speaker
- "Visual and spatial awareness with memory" — personalized interactions

## AI stack
- **Redwood AI** — 1X's proprietary vision-language model
- Built-in LLM
- **Expert Mode**: 1X staff remotely supervise to teach new tasks; autonomy expands over time via updates
