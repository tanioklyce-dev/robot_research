**Amazon Astro** — a wheeled, armless home robot for monitoring, check-ins and Alexa, sold by invitation in the US since 2021. It was Amazon's first home robot, and it never left limited release.

## What it does

Astro navigates a home by itself, gives owners a remote live view through a periscope camera, can patrol with a Ring subscription, and follows people with Alexa ([Amazon](../sources/amazon-astro-launch-post.md)). Its cargo bin carries up to 4.4 pounds ([TechCrunch](../sources/techcrunch-astro-for-business-shutdown.md)). It can't pick anything up, so it does no physical chores.

## Status (2026-11-08)

- **Invite-only.** [CNBC](../sources/cnbc-amazon-home-robot-after-astro.md) says it shipped to "just a few thousand customers" and never went beyond invitations.
- **Price.** Launched at $1,449.99, or $999.99 introductory ([Amazon](../sources/amazon-astro-launch-post.md)). A [May 2026 roundup](https://the-gadgeteer.com/2026/05/20/home-robots-available-2026/) reports a $1,599 listing. Amazon's current listing could not be read here.
- **Future.** Amazon has moved much of the team to a new home-robot effort, including [Fauna Robotics](fauna-robotics.md). No end of support for home Astro was found.

## Privacy and reliability

Amazon describes on-device processing, no-go zones, a button that disables cameras, microphones and motion, and indicator lights for cloud streaming ([Amazon](../sources/amazon-astro-launch-post.md)). These are design claims.

> [!warning] Contradiction — leaked developer accounts
> In 2021 [Vice's Motherboard, as summarized by SlashGear](https://www.slashgear.com/amazon-astro-robot-leak-alleges-big-privacy-and-durability-concerns-29693065), published claims by purported developers that Astro was fragile, its person detection unreliable and it might fall down stairs. Amazon called those descriptions "simply inaccurate." The Vice original was not reviewed here, and the claims date from 2021.

No independent technical security assessment of Astro was found.

## Why it matters

- **A warning about vendor dependence.** The business version [stopped working on a set date](../sources/techcrunch-astro-for-business-shutdown.md) seven months after launch.
- **Navigation wasn't the limit.** A former executive says Bezos was impressed by mapping but disappointed with what it could do ([CNBC](../sources/cnbc-amazon-home-robot-after-astro.md)). The missing piece was physical usefulness.
