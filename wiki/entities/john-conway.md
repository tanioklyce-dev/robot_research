**John Horton Conway** (1937–2020) — British mathematician (Cambridge, then Princeton), best known in this wiki for inventing the **[Game of Life](game-of-life.md)** in **1970**: a 2D [cellular automaton](../concepts/alife/cellular-automata.md) whose single neighbor-count rule yields Turing-complete, open-ended complexity. His broader mathematics (surreal numbers, the Conway group, knot theory, the "look-and-say" sequence) is outside the wiki's scope.

## Contribution
- Designed the Game of Life rule to sit at the **edge of order and chaos** — neither dying out nor exploding — which is exactly what makes it [Class 4](../concepts/alife/cellular-automata.md) and computationally universal. With colleagues (incl. [Bill Gosper](bill-gosper.md)) found the early gliders and the first glider gun in 1970–71 ([Wolfram, 2025](../sources/wolfram-2025-game-of-life-engineering.md)).
