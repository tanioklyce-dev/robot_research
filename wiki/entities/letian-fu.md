**Letian Fu** (publishes as **Max Fu**) — UC Berkeley researcher, with an [NVIDIA](nvidia.md) affiliation on CaP-X. **Lead author of [CaP-X](../sources/cap-x-paper.md)** and co-author of **[ASPIRE](../sources/aspire-paper.md)** — the two papers that put measured numbers on [code-as-policy](../concepts/agents/code-as-policy.md).

## Papers in this wiki
- **[CaP-X](../sources/cap-x-paper.md)** (ICML 2026) — first author (equal contribution) and corresponding author. The framework, benchmark, training-free agent, and RL post-training study.
- **[ASPIRE](../sources/aspire-paper.md)** (Jun 2026) — co-author; the continual-learning system built on CaP-X.

Cited as *"Fu et al., 2026"* throughout ASPIRE, where CaP-X is the environment and the baseline.

## Why it matters in this wiki
Fu is the connective tissue of the wiki's code-as-policy thread: the same person authored the measurement apparatus and then helped build the system that beats it. Both of the papers [Waddle](waddle-labs.md) named as its closest prior work ([refs 13 and 14](../sources/waddle-labs-introducing-waddle.md)) carry his name.
