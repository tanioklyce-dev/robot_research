**Karl Pertsch** — Co-lead of [DROID](droid.md) alongside Alexander Khazatsky. Robot-learning / data-collection researcher; affiliations span UC Berkeley + Stanford in the wiki context (DROID's 13-institution distributed structure means he carries collaborator hats at multiple).

## Papers in this wiki
- **[DROID](droid.md)** — co-lead (with Khazatsky). 350 hr / 76k trajectories / 564 scenes of [Franka Panda](franka-panda.md) manipulation across 13 institutions.

## Why it matters in this wiki
Pertsch is the **organizational center of the DROID effort**. The "scene diversity at fixed embodiment" thesis that underpins DROID's pitch — and that V-JEPA 2 / JEPA-WMs both lean on for their real-robot data story — was largely his and Khazatsky's design call. Updated annotations on HuggingFace ship under `KarlP/droid` (per the DROID project page).
