**Blue Collar Robotics** — Utah startup building remotely operated robots that pick online grocery orders inside existing supermarkets.

## Product and stage

Robot 1.0 is a wheeled basket with one suction arm, picking items up to 10 pounds at about 120 items an hour with one remote person per robot ([Utah Business](../sources/utah-business-blue-collar-robotics.md)). The figures come from the company. In August 2026 it said Gen 2 units were in production for paid customer trials in the fourth quarter ([company](../sources/blue-collar-robotics-site.md)). It was a startup-pitch finalist at Groceryshop 2026 ([The Robot Report](../sources/robot-report-groceryshop-2026.md)).

## Bet

Do not wait for full autonomy. Sell remote labor through a robot now, then automate gradually ([company](../sources/blue-collar-robotics-site.md)).
