**Alexander Mordvintsev** — researcher at **Google Research (Zürich)**, known for **DeepDream** (2015) and for originating **[Neural Cellular Automata](../concepts/alife/neural-cellular-automata.md)** ("Growing Neural Cellular Automata," Distill 2020). His work makes **self-organization / morphogenesis learnable** via shared local neural update rules.

## Role in this wiki
- **Originator of the NCA line** and co-author of **["Neural Cellular Automata: From Cells to Pixels" (2025)](../sources/pajouheshgar-2025-nca-cells-to-pixels.md)** — high-resolution, real-time NCA.
- **Co-author of [Computational Life](../sources/computational-life-self-replicating-programs-paper.md)** (Agüera y Arcas et al., 2024) — the self-replicating-program-soup paper.
- **The bridge between two ALife threads** the wiki tracks: **learnable self-organization** ([NCA](../concepts/alife/neural-cellular-automata.md)) and **emergent self-replication** ([artificial life](../concepts/alife/artificial-life-and-self-replication.md) / Computational Life). Both pursue complex global behavior from simple local rules — NCA *learns* the rule; Computational Life lets replicators *emerge*.
