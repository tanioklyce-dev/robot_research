**Noriaki Hirose** — first author of [MBRA / LogoNav](../sources/mbra-paper.md); Berkeley RAIL and **Toyota Motor North America**. The model-based-learning thread in RAIL's navigation work is largely his: GONet (traversability, 2018), deep visual MPC-policy learning (2019), Depth360 (self-supervised monocular depth, 2022), **ExAug** (geometric experience augmentation, 2023 — the forward-model objective MBRA reuses), SACSoN (social navigation), SELFI (RL self-improvement around people), LeLaN (language-conditioned policy from in-the-wild video), and OmniVLA (2025). MBRA is the point where that thread meets crowdsourced data: a differentiable unicycle-plus-depth model used to *relabel* rather than to control.
