**Alexander Keewatin Dewdney** — mathematician, computer scientist, and author of *Scientific American*'s **"Computer Recreations"** column (1984–1991, succeeding Martin Gardner / Douglas Hofstadter's "Metamagical Themas" lineage). In this wiki he enters as the **co-creator and popularizer of [Core War](core-war.md)**.

## Role in this wiki
- **Co-creator of [Core War](core-war.md)** (with David Jones, University of Western Ontario) and author of the **May 1984 [*Scientific American* column](../sources/dewdney-1984-core-war-scientific-american.md)** that introduced it — the founding text of the self-replicating programming-game tradition that the wiki's [artificial-life branch](../concepts/alife/artificial-life-and-self-replication.md) traces toward [Computational Life](../sources/computational-life-self-replicating-programs-paper.md).
- His column repeatedly explored **self-replicating and self-repairing programs**, cellular automata, and recreational ALife — making him an early popular voice for the "complexity from simple rules" theme the wiki tracks.
