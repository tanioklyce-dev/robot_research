# Geniatech

Shenzhen ARM-embedded ODM (self-described 27 years, 100+ R&D engineers, own factory) selling system-on-modules and single-board computers on Rockchip (RK3576 / 3588 / 3568 / 3566), NXP i.MX, Renesas RZ, Qualcomm and MediaTek Genio silicon, plus edge-AI box PCs, M.2 AI accelerator cards and e-paper displays ([site navigation](../sources/geniatech-rk1828-vs-orin-nx-vs-hailo-8.md)).

In this wiki it appears as the author of a **vendor comparison** promoting the [RK1828](rockchip-rk1828.md) M.2 LLM coprocessor it sells, against the [Jetson Orin NX](jetson-orin-nx.md) and [Hailo](hailo.md)-8 — a page whose competitor columns misstate the Orin NX's bandwidth (68 vs 102.4 GB/s) and credit the Hailo-8 with LLM inference. Useful as the first sighting of an on-package-memory LLM accelerator and as a specimen of secondary comparison tables; not a source for any competitor's numbers. Its companion posts (M.2 accelerator chart, RK1828/RK1820 launch, "RKNN Toolkit vs HailoRT vs ONNX Runtime") are not ingested.
