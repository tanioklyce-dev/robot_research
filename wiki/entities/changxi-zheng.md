**Changxi Zheng** — co-founder of **[SceniX](scenix.md)**; professor of computer science at **Columbia University**, specializing in **physical simulation and computer graphics**. The simulation half of SceniX's founding team, alongside [Yunzhu Li](yunzhu-li.md)'s robotics half.

## Why he matters here

[Fei-Fei Li](fei-fei-li.md) describes him as "a world-class technologist in simulation" and "such a senior researcher and technologist in simulation," and identifies **VFX** as his other background — **Weta**, **Tencent**, and prior entrepreneurship ([a16z conversation](../sources/a16z-worldlabs-scenix-conversation.md), ~7:45). Her stated reason this mattered to the acquisition: "what World Labs is doing is very much interfacing the world of simulation."

That pairing is the whole architecture of [R2S2R](../concepts/robotics/real-to-sim-to-real.md) in personnel form. The method's distinguishing claim is a reconstructed world that is faithful in **appearance and dynamics at once** — a graphics/simulation problem welded to a robot-learning problem — and the founding team is one of each.
