**Victor A. Vyssotsky** — Bell Labs computer scientist (later a leader on the **Multics** project and at Bell Labs / DEC research). In this wiki he enters as the **inventor of [Darwin](../sources/darwin-1961-bell-labs-game.md)** (1961), the first self-reproducing-program game.

## Role in this wiki
- **Invented [Darwin](../sources/darwin-1961-bell-labs-game.md)** (proposed it one day in August 1961) and contributed its standard **5-instruction move-and-relocate loop** — the idiom that made organisms able to correctly *relocate* (not merely copy) themselves. Primary author of the original "Darwin: A Game of Survival and (Hopefully) Evolution" flyer.
- The "(Hopefully) Evolution" subtitle frames the earliest entry in the wiki's [self-replicating-program lineage](../concepts/alife/artificial-life-and-self-replication.md).
