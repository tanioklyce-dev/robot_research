**Chris Adami** — physicist turned computational biologist (Caltech → JPL → Michigan State / Keck Graduate Institute), known for **information-theoretic approaches to evolution and complexity** and as co-creator of **[Avida](avida.md)**.

## Role in this wiki
- **Co-creator of [Avida](avida.md)** (with C. Titus Brown) and lead author of **["Evolutionary Learning in the 2D Artificial Life System 'Avida'" (1994)](../sources/adami-brown-1994-avida.md)** — the spatial, tierra-inspired digital-evolution system.
- His thread — **complexity as information accumulated in a genome from its environment** ("stochastic information transfer") — sits squarely in the wiki's [artificial-life / emergence branch](../concepts/alife/artificial-life-and-self-replication.md) and connects to the [Computational Life](../sources/computational-life-self-replicating-programs-paper.md) "high-order entropy" complexity measures.
