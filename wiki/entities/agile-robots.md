# Agile Robots

**Agile Robots SE** — a Munich-based industrial-automation and Physical-AI company, spun out of the **German Aerospace Center (DLR)**, that in late 2025 became one of the few **European** entrants into the humanoid-robot race with [Agile ONE](agile-one.md).

## Profile

- **Founded** 2018 by DLR (German Aerospace Center) experts ([Agile ONE launch](../sources/agile-robots-agile-one-launch.md)).
- **HQ**: Munich, Germany; ~2,000+ employees across 15 global sites.
- **Revenue**: doubled year-over-year to **€200 million** (as of the Nov 2025 announcement).
- **Heritage**: force-sensitive robotic arms and force-torque sensing — the technical lineage behind Agile ONE's sensorized hands.
- **October 2026 self-description**: **3,500+ employees**, up from ~2,000+ in Nov 2025. Subsidiaries are **BÄR Automation, Franka Robotics, idealworks and Krause Automation** ([ONE SN release](../sources/agile-robots-agile-one-sn-launch.md)). NVIDIA's GTC Berlin release lists Franka and idealworks as separate ecosystem partners without noting they are Agile subsidiaries.
- **Two AI stacks**: a [Google DeepMind](google-deepmind.md) partner (Agile ONE, Gemini Robotics 2) *and* an NVIDIA GR00T reference-platform vendor (ONE SN), as of Oct 2026.

## Products

- **[Agile ONE](agile-one.md)** — industrial humanoid (2025 announcement; series production 2026).
- **[Agile ONE SN](agile-one-sn.md)** — compact R&D humanoid (140 cm / 43 kg, 71 DoF incl. WUJI hands), preconfigured with NVIDIA GR00T 1.7 on Jetson Thor; NVIDIA's named **reference humanoid** for Isaac (GTC Berlin, 2026-10-21; available early 2027) ([launch](../sources/agile-robots-agile-one-sn-launch.md)).
- **Agile Hand** — dexterous manipulation module.
- **FR3** — force-sensitive robotic arm.
- **Diana 7** — 7-axis robotic arm.
- **Thor Series** — (Agile Robots' own product name; unrelated to [NVIDIA Jetson Thor](jetson-thor.md) as far as known).
- **AMRs / AGVs** — autonomous mobile robots and automated guided vehicles.
- **AgileCore** — the AI-driven software platform that integrates all of the above into one managed automation stack.

## Strategy

Agile Robots' distinguishing bet is **integration over standalone humanoid**: where competitors ([Figure](figure.md), [1X NEO](1x-neo.md), [Tesla Optimus](tesla-optimus.md)) sell the humanoid as the product, Agile Robots positions [Agile ONE](agile-one.md) as **one component** of a fully integrated offering — humanoid + AMRs + arms + AI vision, all orchestrated by AgileCore. Manufacturing is deliberately **in-house in Bavaria, Germany** ("made in Germany, not China"). AI development is backed by partnerships with **[Google DeepMind](google-deepmind.md)** and **Deutsche Telekom's Industrial AI Cloud**.
