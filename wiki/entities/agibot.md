Shanghai-based embodied-AI and humanoid-robotics company (full name: AGIBOT Innovation (Shanghai) Technology Co., Ltd.). Maintains an open simulation stack including [[agibot-genie-sim|AGIBOT Genie Sim 3.0]] and the world-model simulator [[genie-envisioner|Genie Envisioner]].

## What we know
- **Simulation stack**: launched [[agibot-genie-sim|AGIBOT Genie Sim 3.0]] open source at CES 2026 ([[agibot-genie-sim-3-announcement|AGIBOT Genie Sim 3.0 Announcement]]).
- **World model**: maintains [[genie-envisioner|Genie Envisioner]] / GE-Sim2 ([[agibot-genie-envisioner-2-announcement|AGIBOT Genie Envisioner 2.0 Announcement]], [[genie-envisioner-paper|Genie Envisioner Paper]]).
- **Hardware**: humanoid robot lineup (introduced alongside Genie Sim 3.0 at CES 2026).
- **Open-source posture**: ships datasets and evaluation code on GitHub.

## Why it matters
One of the most active publishers of open embodied-AI infrastructure in 2026 — and a counterweight to the NVIDIA-centric Western stack.
