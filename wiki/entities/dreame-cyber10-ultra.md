**Dreame Cyber 10 Ultra** — robot vacuum with an extendable arm, shown at CES 2026.

## Specs and status

The arm lifts up to 500 grams and extends about 33 cm, and can also use cleaning attachments. Dreame targeted August 2026 at about €1,799. **Whether it shipped is unconfirmed**, and no Dreame primary was found ([Engadget and Tom's Guide](../sources/engadget-dreame-cyber10-ultra.md)). At CES the arm was seen only moving balls.
