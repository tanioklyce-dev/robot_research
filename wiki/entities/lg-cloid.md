**LG CLOiD** — wheeled two-arm home robot that LG demonstrated at CES 2026 as part of its "Zero Labor Home" vision.

## Specs and status

Two 7-DOF arms with five-fingered hands on a tilting torso and wheeled base, integrated with ThinQ appliances. LG has announced no price, date or market ([press release](../sources/lg-cloid-ces-2026.md)).
