**Nick Maselli** — founder of [Vulcan Robotics](vulcan-robotics.md), the US startup behind [Sourccey](sourccey.md), the open-source personal home robot.

## What is known

Very little. The founding attribution comes from press coverage of the August 2026 Sourccey announcement — *"Sourccey is a personal home robot from US startup Vulcan Robotics, founded by Nick Maselli"* ([Interesting Engineering](https://interestingengineering.com/videos/meet-sourccey-the-open-source-home-robot-you-can-3d-print-and-teach-to-fold-your-laundry) / [Open Source For You](https://www.opensourceforu.com/2026/08/sourccey-brings-open-source-robotics-home/), Aug 2026). The [Vulcan site](../sources/vulcan-robotics-sourccey-site.md) itself names no team members. No prior affiliations, publications, or funding history are published.
