**Pollen Robotics** — French robotics company making [Reachy 2](reachy.md), described as "the first open-source humanoid robot specifically designed for the development of embodied AI and real-world applications." Open-source hardware and software philosophy.

## Products
- [Reachy 2](reachy.md) — bimanual mobile manipulator; 7 DOF per arm; ROS 2 Humble; Python SDK; VR teleoperation. Multiple variants.
- Reachy Mini — smaller companion product; has a HuggingFace presence (content not retrievable in this ingest).

## Philosophy
Fully open-source hardware and software. Positioned as a research platform for embodied AI development rather than commercial deployment.
