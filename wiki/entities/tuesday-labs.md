**Tuesday Labs** — New York City home-robot brand of Adagy Robotics, Inc., taking reservations for the legged Voxel Series-1.

## Voxel Series-1

Advertised for tidying, finding and fetching objects, and watering plants. It has 7-DOF arms, 1-DOF grippers and Z-shaped legs. A $200 refundable reservation holds a place, the estimated price is $20,000, and deliveries are "expected to begin in 2028 and may be later." What is shown is "a concept and development prototype," and the vendor expects a substantial hardware redesign before delivery. It does not yet have FCC authorization ([vendor pages](../sources/tuesday-labs-voxel-series-1.md)).
