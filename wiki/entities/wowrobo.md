**WowRobo** — Shenzhen contract manufacturer of open-hardware research robots: the **certified (★★★) manufacturer of [OpenArm](openarm.md)** on Enactic's directory, and, by its own description, *"the official manufacturer of the [SO-ARM101](so-arm101.md)."* It is the vendor behind the OpenArm units the wiki has already met — [UME](ume.md)'s *"OpenArm 1.0 bimanual made by WowRobo"* at $5,200.

## Prices (self-reported on the OpenArm purchase page, 2026-09)

| Item | Price |
|---|---|
| OpenArm V1.1 bimanual | $5,400 |
| **OpenArm 2.0 bimanual** | **$6,500** |
| V1 → V2 upgrade kit | listed, unpriced |
| OpenArm Cell modular kit | $6,200 |
| OpenArm KER leader arm | $2,599 |
| KER encoder unit | $49 |

Lead time 20–40 days; worldwide via DHL/UPS/FedEx; bank transfer, Shopify, PayPal. Its pitch: *"deliver the finest craftsmanship with minimal profit, and grow together with the OpenArm project."*

## Why it matters

The open-arm tier from ~$120 (SO-101) to $6,500 (OpenArm bimanual) now runs through one factory. That is convenient for buyers and a single point of dependence for two "open standards."
