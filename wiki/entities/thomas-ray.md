**Thomas S. Ray** — ecologist/biologist (University of Delaware; later University of Oklahoma) who created **[Tierra](tierra.md)**, the landmark open-ended digital-evolution system. A tropical-ecology background informs his framing of digital organisms as an *ecology* — parasites, immunity, and coevolution rather than mere optimization.

## Role in this wiki
- **Creator of [Tierra](tierra.md)** and author of **["An Approach to the Synthesis of Life" (1991)](../sources/ray-1991-tierra-synthesis-of-life.md)** — the argument that life should be **synthesized** (self-replicating + open-endedly evolving) rather than simulated, and the demonstration that a single hand-written ancestor evolves a whole ecology.
- Anchors the **evolved-digital-replicator** middle of the wiki's [self-replication lineage](../concepts/alife/artificial-life-and-self-replication.md), directly inspiring [Avida](avida.md).
