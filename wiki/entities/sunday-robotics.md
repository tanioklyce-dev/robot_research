**Sunday Robotics** — home-robot company building Memo, a wheeled two-arm robot trained from data recorded with a wearable glove.

## Memo

- **Status.** Not for sale; a beta is "launching late 2026" ([site](../sources/sunday-memo-site.md)).
- **Cost.** About $20,000 per hand-built unit, with a stated goal of cutting that by at least 50% at scale. Retail pricing is not announced ([site](../sources/sunday-memo-site.md)).
- **Shown skills.** Clearing tables, loading and running a dishwasher, espresso, folding socks. These are labelled autonomous, with no published success rates ([site](../sources/sunday-memo-site.md)).
- **Hardware.** 1.7 m, 170 lb, arms 2×7, hands 2×4, 4 h run-time ([site](../sources/sunday-memo-site.md)).

## Why it matters

Memo is the wiki's clearest vendor demonstration of the dishes task, and it is a bet on [imitation learning](../concepts/learning/imitation-learning.md) from human wearers instead of robot teleoperation.
