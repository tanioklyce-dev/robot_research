**Josh Bongard** — Professor of Computer Science at the **University of Vermont**; a leading figure in **evolutionary robotics** and **embodied cognition** (the view that intelligence is shaped by the body, not just the brain). Senior author on both [Xenobots](xenobots.md) papers.

## Role in this wiki
- **Senior author, [Kriegman et al. 2020](../sources/kriegman-2020-reconfigurable-organisms.md) & [2021](../sources/kriegman-2021-kinematic-self-replication.md)** — the evolutionary-design side of the reconfigurable-organisms work came out of his lab.
- His research program — **evolving the morphology *and* control of machines together** — is why the Xenobots pipeline uses evolutionary search rather than gradient learning (only EA naturally co-designs physical structure + behavior). See [evolutionary computation](../concepts/alife/evolutionary-computation.md).
- Embodied-cognition stance is a counterpoint to the wiki's predominantly disembodied learning threads (BC/VLA/world-models) and adjacent to the [artificial-life](../concepts/alife/artificial-life-and-self-replication.md) branch.
