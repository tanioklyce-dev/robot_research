**Fauna Robotics** — New York City-based robotics company making **Sprout Creator Edition**, a humanoid developer platform for researchers building real-world robotics applications. Assembled in America. Has an arXiv technical report.

## Sprout Creator Edition specs ([Fauna Robotics product page](../sources/fauna-robotics-sprout.md))
- Height: 107 cm; Weight: 22.7 kg
- DOF: 29 (6 DOF arms, 5 DOF legs, 2 DOF neck)
- Compute: NVIDIA Jetson AGX Orin 64GB + 1 TB SSD
- Battery: 3–3.5 hours, swappable
- Vision: ZED 2i stereoscopic cameras + 4 ToF sensors

## Amazon

Amazon acquired Fauna in March 2026 for a new Personal Robotics Group, and job posts mention what it feels like "to live with a Fauna robot." CNBC says it is unclear what the group will ship ([CNBC](../sources/cnbc-amazon-home-robot-after-astro.md)).

## Capabilities
- Compliant locomotion; whole-body teleoperation; floor-to-countertop reach
- Modular autonomy (navigation, mapping, human awareness)
- Social behaviors; dynamic facial expressions; conversational interaction
- Safety-first: soft materials, no pinch points, compliant motor control

## Target market
Developers and creators building real-world robotics applications. Primarily developers and researchers. As of 2026-11-08 the site also lists "Consumer & home" among Sprout's uses, as a personal assistant or family companion, with no chore software described.

## Status (rechecked 2026-11-08)

- **Owner.** "Fauna Robotics has officially joined Amazon," and will keep selling Sprout Creator Edition ([Fauna](../sources/fauna-robotics-sprout.md)).
- **Availability.** Shipping as a Creator Edition since the 2026-01-27 launch. Early customers named are Disney, Boston Dynamics, UC San Diego and NYU.
- **Price.** Fauna's pages say only "Request Robot." Press reports put it at about [$50,000](https://humanoid.guide/product/sprout/).

## Pricing
Not listed publicly. "Request Robot" form.

## Position
Fills a niche between research platforms (Stretch, Franka) and large-scale humanoids (Atlas, Figure). Developer-accessible humanoid with Jetson AGX Orin at modest scale (107 cm). Comparable to Reachy 2 in positioning (open embodied-AI platform) but full bipedal humanoid vs. Reachy's torso-on-wheels.
