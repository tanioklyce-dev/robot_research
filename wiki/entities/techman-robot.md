# Techman Robot

**Techman Robot Inc.** — a Taiwan-based **collaborative-robot (cobot)** manufacturer, a subsidiary of contract-manufacturing giant **Quanta**, best known for shipping the **first cobots with a built-in vision system**. The **world's #2 cobot brand** since 2018, behind Universal Robots.

## Profile

- **Founded 2015** (predecessor lab launched inside Quanta in 2012) ([Techman profile](../sources/techman-robot-about.md)).
- **HQ**: Taoyuan, Taiwan (Hwa Ya Technology Park) — the **only cobot manufacturer based in Taiwan**.
- **Parent**: Quanta (established as a subsidiary of Quanta Storage Inc., under Quanta Computer).
- **Overseas branches**: Shanghai, Shenzhen, Chongqing, Busan, Alblasserdam (NL); 100+ distributors across China, Europe, Japan, Korea, SE Asia.

## Products & differentiation

- **TM Robot Series** — cobots with a **built-in vision system** (the original market differentiator); markets itself as "**Native AI Engine + Robotic Arm + Vision System**."
- **TM Operator Series**, **TM Smart Factory Series**, **TM Xplore** — extended product lines.
- First commercial product, the **TM5**, shipped end of 2016; won Red Dot (2018), iF Design (2018), and Taiwan Excellence awards.

## Market & partnerships

- **World's #2 cobot brand by 2018**; ~**10% market share vs. Universal Robots' ~50%** as of 2021.
- **Omron** acquired a **10% stake in 2021** (building on a 2018 MoU to co-promote cobots).

## Context in this wiki

Techman is a **classical industrial-automation cobot vendor** — vision-integrated but not a learned-policy / VLA player. It's a useful contrast case to the LeRobot / [GR00T](nvidia-groot.md) "Physical AI" center of gravity: proof that "AI + vision + robot arm" as an industrial product predates and runs parallel to the imitation-learning wave.
