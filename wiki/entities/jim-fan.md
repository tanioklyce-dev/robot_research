NVIDIA Director of Robotics, Distinguished Scientist; co-founded [NVIDIA GEAR](nvidia-gear.md) lab with [Yuke Zhu](yuke-zhu.md) in February 2024. Co-leads Project [GR00T](nvidia-groot.md). Public-facing voice of NVIDIA's "every machine that moves will be autonomous" thesis.

## Pre-GEAR research lineage
Author / co-author on the open-ended-agents thread that preceded GEAR's founding:
- [MineDojo](https://minedojo.org/) (NeurIPS 2022 outstanding paper) — Internet-scale Minecraft agent benchmark.
- [VIMA](https://vimalabs.github.io/) (ICML 2023 outstanding paper) — multimodal-prompt robot manipulation.
- [Voyager](https://voyager.minedojo.org/) (TMLR 2024 best-paper finalist) — open-ended LLM agent in Minecraft.
- [Eureka](https://eureka-research.github.io/) / DrEureka (ICLR 2024 / RSS 2024) — LLM-as-reward-designer.

## At GEAR (2024–present)
Co-leads the lab's foundation-model program. Public spokesperson for [GR00T N1](https://developer.nvidia.com/isaac/gr00t), the humanoid foundation-model line (SONIC, CHIP, HOVER, ASAP, VIRAL, Doorman), and the Dream* world-model thread (DreamGen → DreamZero → DreamDojo). NitroGen (CVPR 2026 oral) extends the MineDojo line into "open foundation model for gaming agents". Project lead on **[EgoScale](../sources/egoscale-paper.md)** (Feb 2026) — the first published VLA scaling law (20,854 hr human-video pretrain, the corpus that underlies GR00T N1.7). Project lead on **[DreamDojo](../sources/dreamdojo-paper.md)** (ICML 2026 Spotlight) — the destination paper of the Dream* triplet; a 44,711-hr human-video generative-video world model with 14B-param scale and Self-Forcing distillation to 10.81 FPS real-time.
