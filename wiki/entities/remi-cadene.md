**Remi Cadene** — robotics lead at [Hugging Face](hugging-face.md), responsible for the [LeRobot](lerobot.md) framework. Co-organizer of the [LeRobot Worldwide Hackathon 2025](lerobot-worldwide-hackathon-2025.md) (June 14–15, 2025) alongside Marine Caous and Thomas Wolf. HF handle: `cadene`.

## Why it matters in this wiki

Cadene is the most directly relevant person to the wiki's affordable-mobile-manipulator coverage: he runs the [LeRobot](lerobot.md) project at Hugging Face, which is the de-facto open-source IL stack across [LeKiwi](lekiwi.md), [XLeRobot](xlerobot.md), [SO-ARM101](so-arm101.md), and similar platforms. The June 2025 hackathon — 916 team members, ~400 submissions, 30 ranked winners — is the ecosystem-scale signal that his project has hit community velocity.
