**TonyPi / TonyPi Pro** — small bipedal humanoid education kits from [[hiwonder|Hiwonder]]. Same vendor as [[rosorin|ROSOrin]] and [[rosorin-pro|ROSOrin Pro]]. Targets the **educational-tier humanoid** niche at price points well below NAO ($300–$700 vs NAO's $8k+).

## Specs (TonyPi Pro, 2024 generation)
- ~17 DOF (typical biped kit configuration).
- Raspberry Pi 4 / Raspberry Pi 5 controller.
- Cameras + IMU; HX-12H bus servos (same servo line used in [[rosorin-pro|ROSOrin Pro]]).
- Pre-loaded software for walking, vision, and basic AI demos.

## Why it matters in this wiki
- **Cheapest humanoid in the wiki by far.** $300–$700 puts TonyPi into hobbyist + classroom budgets that NAO ($8k+) and even Unitree G1 ($16k) don't fit.
- **Hiwonder lineage signal.** Combined with [[rosorin|ROSOrin]] / [[rosorin-pro|ROSOrin Pro]] / [[openclaw|OpenClaw]], TonyPi shows Hiwonder is building a coherent **educational-tier robotics catalog** spanning mobile robot → mobile manipulator → biped humanoid. Whether they bundle agentic-AI / LLM-agent curriculum on TonyPi the way they do on ROSOrin Pro is unclear.
- **Educational-vs-research gap is sharper here.** TonyPi can teach fundamentals but is far below the capability needed for serious VLA / JEPA-on-humanoid research. The closest "educational + capable" tier is [[unitree-g1|Unitree G1]] at ~50× the price.

## Position vs other humanoids
- **Smallest + cheapest** humanoid in this wiki.
- **Same vendor as [[rosorin-pro|ROSOrin Pro]]** — likely shares HX-12H bus servos, similar Hiwonder software stack, similar educational positioning.
- **Far below research-tier capability** — limited payload, no dexterous manipulation, walking is basic.
