**Tony Z. Zhao** — Stanford CS researcher; **first author on the original ALOHA (RSS 2023) and ACT** ("Action Chunking Transformer"); **co-lead on Mobile ALOHA** with [Zipeng Fu](zipeng-fu.md) ([source](../sources/mobile-aloha-paper.md)). The wiki has ingested only the Mobile ALOHA paper so far; the original ALOHA + ACT references come via that paper's bibliography.

## Why it matters in this wiki
- **Defined the academic-budget bimanual-teleop reference platform** ([ALOHA](aloha.md)).
- **Introduced action chunking** as an IL primitive that's now near-default across BC and VLA literature ([ACT](act.md), [Diffusion Policy](diffusion-policy.md), [LeRobot](lerobot.md)).
