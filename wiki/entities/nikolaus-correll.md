**Nikolaus Correll** — robotics professor at the **University of Colorado Boulder**; works on swarm/modular robotics, materials-integrated robotics, and robotics education. In this wiki, the senior author of [Cutting the Cord (2026)](../sources/cutting-the-cord-untethered-xlerobot.md), the first measured **untethered onboard-compute [XLeRobot](xlerobot.md)** build.

## In this wiki
- Senior author on [Cutting the Cord](../sources/cutting-the-cord-untethered-xlerobot.md) — an untethered bimanual mobile manipulator (<$1,300) with onboard [Jetson Orin Nano](jetson-orin-nano.md), a Tri-Bus power topology, and on-edge VLA/BC benchmarks. Explicitly framed as a low-barrier **research + education** platform (he has published on a one-year undergraduate robotics curriculum).
