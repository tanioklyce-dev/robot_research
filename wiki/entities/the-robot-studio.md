**The Robot Studio** — open-hardware design group responsible for the **[SO-ARM100 / SO-ARM101](so-arm101.md)** arm lineage. Their designs are the dominant low-cost arm in the [LeRobot](lerobot.md) ecosystem — used as the default manipulator across [LeKiwi](lekiwi.md), [XLeRobot](xlerobot.md), and similar sub-$1k mobile-manipulator platforms.

> [!note] Stub entity
> The Robot Studio appears as the origin / design authority for SO-ARM100/101 across multiple ingested sources, but their own website and documentation have not yet been ingested into this wiki. The entity is recorded here primarily as a citation target.

## Why it matters in this wiki

Without SO-ARM100/101's sub-$500 price point and open-source design, the LeRobot-stack story (LeKiwi + SO-ARM101, XLeRobot's $660 dual-arm) doesn't pencil out. The Robot Studio is effectively the upstream-of-the-upstream of the affordable manipulation tier in this wiki.
