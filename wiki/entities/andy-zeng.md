**Andy Zeng** — formerly Robotics at Google / Google DeepMind. Senior author on **[Code as Policies](../sources/code-as-policies-paper.md)** and co-author on the three other Google-lineage papers in this wiki. Also the author of Transporter Nets, the architecture underneath [CLIPort](cliport.md) — the baseline his own later papers show collapsing out-of-distribution.

## Papers in this wiki
- **[Code as Policies](../sources/code-as-policies-paper.md)** (ICRA 2023) — final/senior author.
- **[SayCan](../sources/saycan-paper.md)** (CoRL 2022) · **[Inner Monologue](../sources/inner-monologue-paper.md)** (CoRL 2022) · **[Language to Rewards](../sources/language-to-rewards-paper.md)** (CoRL 2023) — co-author.
