**Apptronik Apollo** — humanoid from Apptronik (Austin, TX; UT Austin spinout). Announced August 2023; commercial deployments at Mercedes-Benz and other industrial pilots in 2024–2025. **NVIDIA collaboration**: Apollo is one of the platforms targeted by [[nvidia-groot|GR00T]] and the broader Physical AI stack.

## Specs (Apollo, 2023–2024 generation)
- ~1.73 m tall, ~73 kg.
- ~25 kg payload (high relative to other humanoids).
- Battery-swap design for ~24-hour effective uptime.
- Cameras + force sensors; designed for industrial workcells.

## Position vs other humanoids
- **Industrial / logistics focus.** Like Figure's BMW partnership, Apollo's first deployments are warehouse + automotive manufacturing.
- **NVIDIA-aligned AI stack.** GR00T was demonstrated on Apollo at GTC 2024 / 2025. Closer to NVIDIA's open-stack position than Tesla / Figure's vertical integration.
- **Higher payload** than most humanoids in this size class — design choice for industrial use.
