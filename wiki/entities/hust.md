**Huazhong University of Science and Technology (HUST)** — Wuhan-based research university; its computer-vision group under [Xiang Bai](xiang-bai.md) is prolific in detection, scene understanding, and (increasingly) embodied AI. The embodied-vision effort publishes as **H-EmbodVis**.

## In this wiki
- **[TurboVLA](turbovla.md)** ([paper](../sources/turbovla-paper.md), 2026-07) — the LLM-free VLA, with [Huawei](huawei.md) as industrial collaborator. Notable for reaching a top-tier [LIBERO](libero.md) result **on four RTX 4090s**, a compute scale unusual among the labs in this wiki.

The group's non-robotics work shows up in TurboVLA's own citations (autonomous-driving VLA *ORION*, the *Hermes* self-driving world model, 3D-prior scene understanding), which explains the paper's architectural instincts: it reaches for a **detection/grounding** solution to vision-language fusion rather than a language-model one.
