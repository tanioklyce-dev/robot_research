**Starship Technologies** — Estonian-American maker of small autonomous sidewalk delivery robots, and by its own numbers the largest operator in that category.

## Scale and focus

The company reports over 10 million deliveries and more than 3,000 robots in eight countries. In June 2026 it said it would leave US campuses and redeploy over 1,200 robots to grocery ([Starship](../sources/starship-grocery-press-releases.md)). Its strongest grocery market is Finland, with one million S Group deliveries. All these are company figures.

## What it isn't

Starship solves the last stretch to the door. It doesn't shop, pick items or carry them inside. See [Retail and grocery robotics](../concepts/robotics/retail-and-grocery-robotics.md).
