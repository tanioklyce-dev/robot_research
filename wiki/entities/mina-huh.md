**Mina Huh** — researcher at the University of California, Berkeley. Works at the intersection of accessibility, HCI, and HRI; focused on how blind and low-vision users perceive and interact with autonomous systems.

## Papers in this wiki
- **[Designing Accessible Robot Communication for Blind People (Huh et al. 2026)](../sources/huh2026-accessible-robot-comm.md)** — first author. Two-study CHI 2026 workshop paper on robot narration for blind users; collaboration with [Amy Pavel](amy-pavel.md) (Berkeley) plus the UT Austin robot-learning group (Martin-Martin, Zhu, H. Liu) and [Maya Cakmak](maya-cakmak.md) (UW).
