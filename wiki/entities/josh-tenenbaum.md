**Joshua B. Tenenbaum** — MIT professor of computational cognitive science; known for Bayesian models of cognition, intuitive physics and psychology, and program induction as a model of concept learning. Co-author on **[Generalized Planning in PDDL Domains with Pretrained LLMs](../sources/generalized-planning-pddl-llm-paper.md)**.

_Stub._ The program-synthesis framing of that paper — learn a *program* that generalizes, not a solution to one instance — is his long-running line applied to LLMs.
