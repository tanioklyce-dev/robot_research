**Pierre Sermanet** — [Google DeepMind](google-deepmind.md). **First author of the [ASIMOV benchmark](asimov-benchmark.md)** and the person most associated with **[semantic safety](../concepts/safety/semantic-safety.md)** as a named research area in this wiki.

## Papers in this wiki
- **[ASIMOV Benchmark](../sources/asimov-benchmark-paper.md)** (CoRL 2025) — first author.
- **[Predictive Red Teaming](../sources/predictive-red-teaming-paper.md)** (2025) — co-author.
- **[Inner Monologue](../sources/inner-monologue-paper.md)** (CoRL 2022) — co-author, from the earlier Google robotics-and-language cohort.

## Why it matters in this wiki
His arc runs from the [code-as-policy](../concepts/agents/code-as-policy.md) lineage's closed-loop-feedback branch into robot safety — the same trajectory as the wider Google Robotics group, but toward *what the robot should refuse* rather than *what it can do*. He is also credited with **SciFi-Benchmark**, named on the [safety page](../sources/deepmind-gemini-robotics-safety-page.md) and still un-ingested.
