**YOR** — open-source bimanual mobile manipulator from an NYU-led team, built for about $9,250 in parts.

## Specs

Omnidirectional swerve base, telescopic lift with 63.5 cm stroke, two 6-DoF AgileX PiPER arms at 1.5 kg payload each, 27 kg total, 4–5 hours of battery ([paper](../sources/yor-paper.md)).

## Evidence

One proof-of-concept learned box pick, lift and move, 9/10 ([paper](../sources/yor-paper.md)). No household results yet.
