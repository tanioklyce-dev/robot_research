**Human-Centered Robotics Lab (HCR Lab)** — robotics research lab at the University of Washington Paul G. Allen School of Computer Science & Engineering. Director: [Maya Cakmak](maya-cakmak.md). Focus: assistive robots and end-user robot programming; grounded in participatory design with users with disabilities.

## Research focus

Two primary tracks:
1. **Physically assistive robots** — mobile manipulators for people with motor impairments; feeding, grooming, teleoperation interfaces, handovers.
2. **End-user robot programming (EUP)** — tools for non-programmers to customize robot behavior; FLEX-SDK; tools transferred to [Hello Robot Stretch SE2](stretch.md).

Primary research platform: [Hello Robot Stretch](stretch.md).

Notable collaborators: Siddhartha Srinivasa (UW Personal Robotics Lab, now CMU), Dieter Fox (UW/NVIDIA), Bilge Mutlu (UW), Elin Björling (UW).

## Notable outputs
- HRI 2025 Best Systems Paper Finalist — out-of-lab feeding robot deployment
- HRI 2024 Best Short Contribution — 3D mice for robot control
- ICRA 2021 Best HRI Paper Award — reactive handovers
- HRI 2023 Best Design Paper Award — feeding in social contexts
- Multiple HRI best paper finalists

## Significance

The lab that most directly addresses the "accessible HRI for low-motor users" gap identified in this wiki's assistive robotics synthesis. Their long-term deployments with quadriplegic user Henry Evans (Stretch, summers 2021–2023) are the best-documented real-world assistive Stretch deployments.
