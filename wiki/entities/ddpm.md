**DDPM — Denoising Diffusion Probabilistic Models.** Foundational generative-model class introduced by Ho, Jain, Abbeel (UC Berkeley, NeurIPS 2020, [arxiv 2006.11239](https://arxiv.org/abs/2006.11239)). A network is trained to predict the Gaussian noise added at each step of a forward Markov noising process; samples are generated by iteratively denoising from `N(0, I)` back to data. Substrate for almost all subsequent diffusion-model work, including image generation, video generation, **and robot action diffusion** ([Diffusion Policy](diffusion-policy.md)).

## Mechanics

- **Forward process**: gradually add Gaussian noise over `K` steps until pure noise. Defined by a fixed noise schedule `β_k`.
- **Reverse process**: a learned neural network `ε_θ(x_k, k)` predicts the noise that was added; the reverse step subtracts that prediction (with appropriate scaling) and adds fresh small Gaussian noise.
- **Training loss**: `L = MSE(ε_k, ε_θ(x_0 + ε_k, k))` — predict the noise from a noisy sample at step `k`. Simple, stable, no adversarial training needed.
- **Sampling**: `x_K ~ N(0, I)` → iterate `K` denoising steps → `x_0`.

## Key theoretical contribution

DDPM established the link between diffusion probabilistic models and **denoising score matching with Langevin dynamics** sampling — unifying two previously separate generative-modeling threads. The simple weighted variational bound it derived made diffusion models practically trainable at scale.

## Headline results (image generation)

From [DDPM Paper](../sources/ddpm-paper.md) abstract:
- **CIFAR-10**: Inception score 9.46, FID 3.17 (state-of-the-art at publication).
- **256×256 LSUN**: comparable to ProgressiveGAN.

## Downstream lineage relevant to this wiki

- **DDIM** (Song, Meng, Ermon, ICLR 2021) — non-Markovian sampling that decouples training-step count from inference-step count. Diffusion Policy uses DDIM with **10 inference steps for a 100-step-trained model**, hitting 0.1s real-time control latency.
- **iDDPM** (Nichol & Dhariwal, ICML 2021) — improved square-cosine noise schedule. Diffusion Policy uses this schedule.
- **Score-based generative modeling** (Song & Ermon) — alternative formulation of the same mathematics.
- **Latent-space diffusion / Stable Diffusion** (Rombach et al., CVPR 2022) — diffusion in a learned latent space.
- **Diffusion-as-policy in robotics** — [Diffusion Policy](diffusion-policy.md) (Chi et al., RSS 2023); Diffuser/trajectory-diffusion (Janner et al., ICML 2022); IDQL (Hansen-Estruch et al., 2023).
- **Generative-video world models** — [NVIDIA Cosmos](nvidia-cosmos.md), [Genie Envisioner](genie-envisioner.md), Sora — all build on diffusion-model foundations established here.

## How [Diffusion Policy](diffusion-policy.md) adapts DDPM

Two modifications:
1. Output `x` is a **robot action sequence**, not an image.
2. Denoising is **conditioned on observations** `O_t` (model `p(A_t | O_t)`, not the joint).

Otherwise the training/sampling structure is identical, including the iDDPM noise schedule.

## Why it matters in this wiki

- **Substrate of [Diffusion Policy](diffusion-policy.md)** — without DDPM, Diffusion Policy is not a research direction.
- **Substrate of generative-video world models** — [NVIDIA Cosmos](nvidia-cosmos.md), [Genie Envisioner](genie-envisioner.md) trace to DDPM lineage.
- **Implicit foil for the JEPA argument** — [JEPA](../concepts/world-models/jepa.md)'s pitch ("predict in latent space, not pixels") is positioned against the diffusion-pixel-prediction approach DDPM established. DDPM is the *thing JEPA argues against* in [V-JEPA 2](v-jepa-2.md), [LeWM](leworldmodel.md), and [JEPA-WMs](jepa-wms.md).
