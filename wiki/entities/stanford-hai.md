**Stanford HAI — the Stanford Institute for Human-Centered Artificial Intelligence.** Applies research and analysis to AI policy questions; a stated pillar is informing policymakers, industry, and civil society by disseminating scholarship to a wide audience. Nonpartisan. Contact for policy work: `HAI-Policy@stanford.edu`.

HAI has been a load-bearing source in this wiki since before it had a page: the **AI Index** supplies the canonical sim-to-real gap figures and the industry-concentration numbers cited across a dozen pages. The 2026 world-model brief is its first *prescriptive* publication here — the AI Index measures, the Issue Brief argues.

## In this wiki

- **[AI Index Report 2026](../sources/stanford-hai-ai-index-2026.md)** (ninth edition, 2026) — annual measurement of the global AI field. Source of the 89.4% RLBench vs **12.4% [BEHAVIOR-1K](behavior-benchmark.md)** contrast that anchors [sim-to-real transfer](../concepts/learning/sim-to-real-transfer.md), plus the >90%-of-notable-models-from-industry figure. Referenced by ~19 wiki pages.
- **[The World Model and Spatial Intelligence Era: Governing AI Beyond Language](../sources/hai-world-model-spatial-intelligence-brief.md)** (Issue Brief, July 2026) — nine authors spanning HAI staff, law, political science, CS, psychiatry, and the Hoover Institution. Establishes the wiki's [world-model governance](../concepts/safety/world-model-governance.md), [spatial intelligence](../concepts/world-models/spatial-intelligence.md), [functional taxonomy](../concepts/world-models/world-model-functional-taxonomy.md), and [world-model evaluation](../concepts/world-models/world-model-evaluation.md) pages.

The two are continuous in thesis. The AI Index's central framing is that **AI is scaling faster than the governance and evaluation systems built around it**; the world-model brief is that argument narrowed to one capability and turned into recommendations.

## Funding disclosure

> [!warning] Declared in the brief itself
> HAI "has received financial support from several companies referenced in this brief, including **[NVIDIA](nvidia.md) and Google**," and Fei-Fei Li is on partial leave as CEO of **[World Labs](world-labs.md)**, whose product is cited as an exemplar. HAI states that consistent with its fundraising and research policies, no outside entity sets the institute's research agenda or determines research outcomes, and the authors retained full authority over analysis, findings, recommendations, and editorial decisions.
>
> Recorded here not to discount the brief — the disclosure is unusually complete — but because its central recommendation (public investment in shared simulation infrastructure and action-labeled data) has an obvious beneficiary in a spatial-AI startup without a deployed robot fleet.
