**TidyBot++** — open-source holonomic mobile base with phone teleoperation, from Princeton and Stanford researchers, built for $5–6k before adding an arm.

## Evidence

Diffusion policies on six tasks in a real apartment scored between 6/10 and 10/10, including 7/10 for loading a dishwasher. On one comparison task, holonomic motion scored 9/10 against 4/10 for differential drive ([paper](../sources/tidybot-plus-plus-paper.md)).

## Use elsewhere

It is the base under the Franka FR3 in [Robot-Powered Data Flywheels](../sources/robot-powered-data-flywheels-paper.md).
