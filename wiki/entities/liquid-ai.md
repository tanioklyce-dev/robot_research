**Liquid AI** is a foundation-model company. In this wiki it appears as the maker of **[d1](liquid-d1.md)**, which it calls a **decision model**: a model that returns answer probabilities in one forward pass instead of generating text ([d1 blog](../sources/liquid-ai-d1-decision-model-blog.md)).

## Products in the wiki
- **[d1](liquid-d1.md)** (2026-10-05): text + image decision model. API at $0.04 / M input tokens, also on Vercel and OpenRouter. Open weights for later models promised "soon."

> [!note] Thin page
> The company's background and its other model lines are not covered by any ingested source. Liquid is widely known for efficient "Liquid Foundation Models" (LFM), but the d1 post does not say whether d1 is built on them, so the wiki makes no claim either way.
