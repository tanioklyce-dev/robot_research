**Galbot** — Chinese robotics company whose wheeled dual-arm G1 is sold into retail, instant-retail warehouses, industry and care.

## Galbot G1

Wheeled, omnidirectional, 1730 mm tall and 92.5 kg, with two 7-DoF arms carrying 5 kg each, a 2-DoF lifting leg section, and swappable hands, suction or grippers. It has NVIDIA AGX Orin compute and 8 hours of working time ([product page](../sources/galbot-g1-product-page.md)). The vendor publishes no price.

## Deployment claims

Galbot claims more than 170 "Galaxy Space Capsule" sites in over 40 Chinese cities, and instant-retail warehouses running around the clock for over a year and a half ([product page](../sources/galbot-g1-product-page.md)). This wiki has no independent confirmation.
