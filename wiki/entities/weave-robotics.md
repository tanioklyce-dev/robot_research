**Weave Robotics** — San Francisco home-robot company selling Isaac 0, a stationary laundry folder, and pre-ordering Isaac 1, a wheeled two-arm robot for laundry and tidying.

## Products

- **Isaac 1.** $7,999 or $449 a month, with a $250 refundable deposit. First shipments are planned for fall 2026, California first ([product pages](../sources/weave-isaac-1-product-page.md)). DOF: arms 2×6, hands 2×1, torso 2, base 3. Reach is 80" vertical and 38" horizontal.
- **Isaac 0.** A stationary folder and "early-release prototype," shipping in limited quantities in California ([product pages](../sources/weave-isaac-1-product-page.md)).

- **Isaac 0 price and speed.** $249 a month, or $3,999 up front plus $49 a month ([Weave page, via eWeek source](../sources/eweek-weave-isaac-0.md)). eWeek reports 30 to 90 minutes per load, with remote corrections.

## Autonomy

Weave says Isaac 1 is autonomous by default "with teleoperation assistance when needed to guarantee we complete tasks" ([product pages](../sources/weave-isaac-1-product-page.md)). No intervention rate is published. [CNET](../sources/cnet-flourish-1.md) groups it with NEO as a robot that uses teleoperators.
