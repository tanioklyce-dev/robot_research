# Zongqing Lu

Robot-learning researcher — **Peking University** faculty and lead of **[BeingBeyond](beingbeyond.md)**; **corresponding author of [BumbleBee](../sources/bumblebee-experts-to-generalist-wbc.md)** ("From Experts to a Generalist: Toward General Whole-Body Control for Humanoid Robots," 2025-09; lu@beingbeyond.com). Works on **humanoid [whole-body control](../concepts/robotics/whole-body-control.md)** via clustered expert-to-generalist RL distillation with sim-to-real delta-action modeling.
