**Lerrel Pinto** — Assistant Professor at NYU CS. Robot-learning + manipulation + foundation-models researcher. In this wiki, **co-senior on [[dino-wm-paper|DINO-WM]]** (with [[yann-lecun|LeCun]]) and a co-author on [[robot-utility-models-website|Robot Utility Models]] — both NYU-anchored projects with [[meta-fair|Meta FAIR]] collaborators.

## Papers in this wiki
- **[[dino-wm-paper|DINO-WM]]** (Zhou, Pan, LeCun, Pinto — Nov 2024) — co-senior with LeCun. NYU side of the collaboration.
- **[[robot-utility-models-website|Robot Utility Models]]** (Etukuru, Shafiullah, …, Pinto — Sep 2024) — co-author. The "5 utility models, ~90% zero-shot success" project from NYU with Meta researchers.

## Why it matters in this wiki
Pinto sits at the intersection of two things this wiki tracks closely:
1. **JEPA-adjacent world modeling** (DINO-WM uses frozen DINOv2 features + learned predictor; lightweight benches; one of the comparison baselines for [[leworldmodel|LeWM]] and [[jepa-wms|JEPA-WMs]]).
2. **Real-robot generalist policies** (RUM's Stretch-only training corpus + zero-shot xArm 7 transfer is the canonical "data philosophy" alternative to the [[droid|DROID]]-anchored line).

If a future ingest brings in a Pinto-led paper that bridges those two — e.g. a NYU-line world model trained on RUM-style data — it would be load-bearing for this wiki.
