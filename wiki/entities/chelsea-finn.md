**Chelsea Finn** — Assistant Professor at Stanford CS. Meta-learning + robot-learning researcher; introduced MAML (Model-Agnostic Meta-Learning) in 2017. In this wiki, **co-senior on [[metaworld|Metaworld]]** and **senior on [[droid|DROID]]**.

## Papers in this wiki
- **[[metaworld|Metaworld]]** (Yu, Quillen, Levine, Finn — CoRL 2019) — co-senior on the 50-task meta-RL benchmark; the meta-learning framing is largely Finn's research lineage applied to robot manipulation.
- **[[droid|DROID]]** (Khazatsky, Pertsch, …, Finn, Levine — Apr 2024) — senior author on the 13-institution real-robot teleoperation dataset.

## Why it matters in this wiki
Two infrastructure papers, both shared with [[sergey-levine|Levine]]: **Metaworld** (sim, meta-RL framing) and **DROID** (real, scene-diversity framing). The Stanford-Berkeley-multi-institution axis these papers represent is the closest thing to a "standard reference setup" across the JEPA-for-robotics literature ingested here.

Finn-affiliated work not yet ingested but commonly referenced: ALOHA / ACT (low-cost bimanual teleoperation), Octo (generalist policy), various meta-learning + few-shot imitation work.
