**Amy Pavel** — researcher at the University of California, Berkeley, working on accessibility, HCI, and AI-mediated description for blind and low-vision users. Senior author on the Berkeley-side of the cross-institutional Huh et al. 2026 work.

## Papers in this wiki
- **[Designing Accessible Robot Communication for Blind People (Huh et al. 2026)](../sources/huh2026-accessible-robot-comm.md)** — senior author. The paper extends Pavel's broader line of work on non-visual description and AI-mediated accessibility into the domain of robot task communication.

## Why it matters in this wiki
Pavel's group represents the **accessibility / HCI side** of the new wave of robot-communication research, complementing the more robot-system-focused contributions from UT Austin (Zhu, Martin-Martin) and UW (Cakmak). The Huh et al. paper is the only fully cross-disciplinary HCI ↔ robotics collaboration we've ingested addressing the output-interface gap flagged in the [Nanavati/Cakmak 2024 review](../sources/nanavati2024-physically-assistive-robots-review.md) §6.1.3.
