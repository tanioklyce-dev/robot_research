**Wenlong Huang** — Stanford (PhD under [Fei-Fei Li](fei-fei-li.md)), previously at Robotics at Google. **The most connective author in the pre-2026 [code-as-policy](../concepts/agents/code-as-policy.md) lineage**, appearing on three of its four foundational papers.

## Papers in this wiki
- **[Code as Policies](../sources/code-as-policies-paper.md)** (ICRA 2023) — co-author on the seminal code-writing result.
- **[Inner Monologue](../sources/inner-monologue-paper.md)** (CoRL 2022) — co-first author; closed-loop textual feedback.
- **[VoxPoser](../sources/voxposer-paper.md)** (CoRL 2023) — **first author**; 3D value maps as the LLM's output.

## Why it matters in this wiki
His three papers trace the lineage's central argument end to end: first that LLMs can *select* skills with feedback (Inner Monologue), then that they can *write* the policy (Code as Policies), then that writing a **spatial objective** beats writing a call sequence because the primitive set is the binding constraint (VoxPoser). The third paper is a critique of the second, by an author of the second — which is why the wiki treats the primitive-scope ceiling as an *internally recognized* limitation rather than something [CaP-X](cap-x.md) discovered from outside.
