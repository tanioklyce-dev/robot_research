**Xiang Bai** — professor at [HUST](hust.md); long-standing computer-vision researcher (scene text, detection, shape analysis) whose group has moved into embodied AI and autonomous driving. Senior author on **[TurboVLA](turbovla.md)** ([paper](../sources/turbovla-paper.md)).

_Stub._ Listed here because the group's CV lineage explains TurboVLA's design choice — it solves vision-language fusion with a **detection/grounding** module ([Grounding DINO](grounding-dino.md)-derived) rather than a language-model backbone.
