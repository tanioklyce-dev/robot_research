**myAGV** — [Elephant Robotics](elephant-robotics.md)' autonomous mobile base. Raspberry Pi 4B compute, ROS, 2D/3D navigation. Designed to pair with Elephant Robotics arm attachments (myCobot 280 Pi, mechArm 270, etc.) to form compound mobile manipulators for education and research.

## Specs ([Elephant Robotics myAGV & Compound Collection](../sources/elephant-robotics-myagv-compound.md))
- Compute: Raspberry Pi 4B
- OS: Ubuntu Mate 20.04 (customized)
- Motors: brushless DC planetary
- Navigation: 2D + 3D mapping, autonomous pathfinding
- Programming: myBlockly (drag-and-drop) + Python 2/3
- Pre-installed: ROS, OpenCV, QT, VSCode, VNC, SSH
- Firmware: myStudio cloud platform

## Compatible arms
myCobot 280 Pi, myPallitizer 260, mechArm 270, myArm, ultraArm.
