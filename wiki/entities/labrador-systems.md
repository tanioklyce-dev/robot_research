**Labrador Systems** — California company building self-driving carts that bring items within reach of people with pain, injury or limited mobility. Its robots have no arm.

## The robots

Both models carry up to 25 pounds on two decks and travel between pre-mapped stops on one level. The Caddie has a fixed height. The Retriever adjusts its height and can fetch special trays, up to 10 pounds each, from pallets placed on counters and shelves ([Labrador](../sources/labrador-systems-site.md)). These are stated as target specifications.

**The design idea is the interesting part.** Rather than solving general grasping, the Retriever adds simple infrastructure to the home, at the price of only fetching what's on a tray ([IEEE Spectrum](../sources/ieee-spectrum-labrador-retriever.md)). It's a useful counterpoint to general-purpose [home humanoids](../syntheses/platforms/household-robots-comparison.md).

## Status (2026-11-08)

**Unconfirmed, and not orderable by consumers as far as found.** Reservations closed. The site says a care-provider Retriever Pro comes first, with a production target of Q2 2024 that has passed and not been updated ([Labrador](../sources/labrador-systems-site.md)). The newest dated press item is January 2024. No report of closure was found, and none of production volume.

> [!warning] Contradiction — "deployed" versus reserved
> Coverage says Pro robots have been deployed, but the [company's 2022 release](../sources/labrador-first-business-customers-release.md) announces reservations by organizations "set to receive" them. Don't treat that list as evidence of active use.

Pilot robots did go into homes, with testimonials from named users on the company site, but no measured outcomes were found.

## Price

The closed early-reservation terms were $1,499 upfront plus $99 a month for the Caddie, or $149 a month for the Retriever, for 36 months ([Labrador](../sources/labrador-systems-site.md)). [IEEE Spectrum](../sources/ieee-spectrum-labrador-retriever.md) rounds these to $1,500, $100 and $150. A [Freethink report](https://www.freethink.com/hard-tech/personal-robot) lists a $5,000 Caddie, which matches the total of those Caddie payments. Retriever Pro pricing was not found.

## Privacy

Autonomy runs on the robot, and once trained it can work over local Bluetooth with no Wi-Fi. A technician needs a video connection to train the map ([Labrador](../sources/labrador-systems-site.md)). More detail was promised before production and wasn't found. No security assessment was found.
