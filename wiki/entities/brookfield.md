**Brookfield** — global alternative asset manager (NYSE/TSX: BN, BAM; **>$1 trillion AUM**), and one of the world's largest real-estate owners. Enters this wiki not as a robotics company but as **[Figure AI](figure.md)'s human-video data supplier**: from September 2025, Figure collects egocentric human video inside Brookfield properties to pretrain [Helix](helix.md) under [Project Go-Big](../sources/figure-project-go-big.md).

## The asset base, read as a dataset

| | |
|---|---|
| Residential units | **100,000+** |
| Commercial office | **500M sq ft** |
| Logistics space | **160M sq ft** |

What Figure is buying is **environment diversity** — a distribution over real kitchens, hallways, offices and warehouses that no lab can stage. Collection had already begun at announcement ([partnership post](../sources/figure-brookfield-partnership.md)).

## The three strands

1. **Data** — human video capture across all three property types, feeding "the world's largest and most diverse real-world humanoid pretraining dataset."
2. **Infrastructure** — stated intent to explore **next-generation GPU data centers** and real estate for **robotic training environments**. Never mentioned again in any subsequent Figure post.
3. **Deployment** — humanoids into Brookfield's portfolio "over time." No deployment has been announced as of 2026-08-28.

Plus a fourth, financial: **Brookfield invested in Figure's Series C** (>$1B at $39B post-money, closed 2025-09-16).

## Why it matters here

> [!note] Property portfolios as a robotics data moat
> This is the wiki's first instance of **real estate monetised as training data**. Every other data strategy tracked here buys *labour* — [Index](figure-index.md) pays the public per clip, teleoperation pays operators, [DROID](droid.md) and [RoboMIND](robomind.md) pay lab staff. Brookfield sells **access to space**. Whoever owns a large, varied portfolio of human-occupied interiors owns something a robotics company needs and cannot manufacture; most such owners do not yet know it.

> [!warning] Supplier, investor and prospective customer at once
> Brookfield occupies all three roles simultaneously. Nothing improper follows, but **no strand is priced independently**, so the partnership should not be read as arm's-length market validation of Figure.

> [!warning] Consent inside occupied dwellings is unaddressed
> [Go-Big](../sources/figure-project-go-big.md) describes video *"collected passively as people do behaviors in real Brookfield homes."* Neither post says whether units are occupied or vacant, who is filmed, or on what terms. **The landlord's consent is not the tenant's.** Same gap the wiki flagged on [Index](figure-index.md) — here 11 months earlier, and inside dwellings.
