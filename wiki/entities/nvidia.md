GPU vendor and the dominant force in the agentic-robotics simulation stack as of 2026. Owns or co-owns most of the major substrate components: simulator ([[nvidia-isaac-sim|NVIDIA Isaac Sim]]), learning framework ([[nvidia-isaac-lab|NVIDIA Isaac Lab]]), physics engine ([[newton-physics-engine|Newton physics engine]], co-developed), world model ([[nvidia-cosmos|NVIDIA Cosmos]]), and a flagship open VLA ([[nvidia-groot|NVIDIA GR00T]]).

## What we know
- **Physical AI stack** announced/expanded at GTC 2026, with Newton 1.0 GA, Isaac Lab 3.0, Isaac Sim 6.0, GR00T N1.6 GA / N1.7 EA, and Omniverse NuRec ([[nvidia-newton-contact-rich-manipulation-blog|NVIDIA Newton Contact-Rich Manipulation Blog]], [[nvidia-newton-physics-engine-developer-page|NVIDIA Newton Physics Engine Developer Page]]).
- **Linux Foundation collaboration**: contributed Newton to the Linux Foundation under joint stewardship with [[google-deepmind|Google DeepMind]] and [[disney-research|Disney Research]].
- **Ecosystem partnerships**: powers [[agibot-genie-sim|AGIBOT Genie Sim 3.0]] (built on Isaac Sim) and [[genie-envisioner|GE-Sim2]] (built on Cosmos-Predict2).
- **Research arm**: NVIDIA Research is co-authored on the [[robocasa|RoboCasa365]] paper through Yuke Zhu's dual UT Austin / NVIDIA appointment ([[robocasa365-paper|RoboCasa365 Paper]]).
