**Abhinav Gupta** — **Professor at Carnegie Mellon's Robotics Institute**, and **co-founder and president of [Skild AI](skild-ai.md)**. Previously a research leader at **Meta AI Research (FAIR) Pittsburgh**, working on self-supervised techniques and large-scale robot learning; returned to full-time CMU duties in **May 2022** `[live-web]` [CMU Robotics Institute](https://www.ri.cmu.edu/ri-faculty/abhinav-gupta/).

His research centers on **scaling learning by building self-supervised, lifelong and interactive systems**, and on representation and reasoning for scene understanding grounded in the physical, functional and causal structure of the 3D world `[live-web]`.

> [!warning] Not the same person as Abhishek Gupta
> This wiki already cites an **Abhishek Gupta** (University of Washington) on [SERL](../sources/serl-paper.md), [SAC applications](../sources/sac-applications-paper.md), [RoboArena](../sources/roboarena-paper.md) and [Murray et al. on grasping in clutter](../sources/murray2024-grasping-clutter-ivfp.md). **Different researcher, different institution.** Neither should be merged into the other, and a bare "Gupta" in a robot-learning author list is ambiguous between them — check the given name.

## Background (live-web; not ingested)

- IIT for undergraduate computer science; PhD at the **University of Maryland**; joined the **CMU Robotics Institute in 2009** `[live-web]` [Contrary Research](https://research.contrary.com/company/skild-ai).
- Joined **FAIR in 2018** as a founding member of its Pittsburgh lab and research leader; concurrent with CMU until returning full-time in 2022.
- Reported at **75K+ citations**, across robotic manipulation, locomotion, navigation and visual scene understanding `[live-web]`.

## Why it matters in this wiki

- **The self-supervised / lifelong-learning thesis is Skild's data strategy.** [S1](../sources/skild-s1-blog.md) rests on learning from human video at scale — *"Our model learns by watching human videos. This is a scalable solution for the robotics data problem"* — which is a direct commercial expression of the research program described above. The founder split reads as complementary rather than duplicative: [Pathak](deepak-pathak.md)'s line is adaptation and intrinsic motivation, Gupta's is self-supervised representation at scale.
- **Two CMU Robotics Institute professors founded the company**, and both retain their appointments. Together with [Pathak](deepak-pathak.md) this makes Skild the clearest instance in this wiki of a lab-to-company transfer that did not empty the lab — the opposite of the [TRI](tri.md) → [Walden Robotics](walden-robotics.md) spin-out, where the whole LBM leadership left.
