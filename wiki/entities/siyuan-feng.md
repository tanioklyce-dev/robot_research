# Siyuan Feng

Full-stack roboticist — **co-founder & Principal Architect of [Walden Robotics](walden-robotics.md)** (2026). Specializes in full-stack robot software, from bipedal locomotion through dexterous manipulation. One of the recurring [TRI](tri.md) names in the wiki's manipulation line: a co-author on **[Diffusion Policy](diffusion-policy.md)** and the **[UMI](umi.md)** data-collection work, and a co-lead of TRI's **[Large Behavior Model](../concepts/learning/large-behavior-models.md)** effort.

## Career

- **PhD** Robotics, **Carnegie Mellon** ([Walden launch](../sources/walden-robotics-launch.md)).
- **DARPA Robotics Challenge** — bipedal locomotion (the same challenge [Russ Tedrake](russ-tedrake.md) led Team MIT in).
- **[Toyota Research Institute](tri.md)** — dexterous manipulation; **[Diffusion Policy](diffusion-policy.md)** and **[UMI](umi.md)** co-author; **co-led the LBM effort** ([TRI LBM paper](../sources/tri-lbm-paper.md)).
- **[Walden Robotics](walden-robotics.md)** — co-founder & **Principal Architect** (2026).
