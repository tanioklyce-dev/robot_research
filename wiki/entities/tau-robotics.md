**Tau Robotics** — San Francisco company selling home cleaning by remotely piloted humanoid robots, using each visit to collect training data.

## Service

Invite-only, serving the SF Bay Area and Los Angeles, with New York on the waitlist. Two robots for one hour cost $100 once, or $60–$90 per recurring visit ([Tau](../sources/tau-robotics-service-site.md)). Bathrooms and bedrooms are excluded unless you opt in.

## Autonomy

Tau's terms say "The robot is not autonomous. A person is controlling it" ([Tau](../sources/tau-robotics-service-site.md)). Its co-founder has said vacuuming and mopping can run without help ([MIT Technology Review](../sources/mit-technology-review-tau-cleaning.md)). The CEO estimates phasing out technicians around 2028 or 2029 ([CBS](../sources/cbs-news-tau-robotics.md)).

## Observed performance

One reporter's two visits: stair trouble, an early end to the first visit, then a real kitchen and toilet clean with spills and a toppled air purifier. The robots were "thorough but slow" ([MIT Technology Review](../sources/mit-technology-review-tau-cleaning.md)).

## Hardware

About 4.5 feet tall, with 3D-printed head and claw hands ([MIT Technology Review](../sources/mit-technology-review-tau-cleaning.md)). CBS gives a cost of $50,000 per robot. An [aggregator citing MarketWatch](https://humanoid.guide/tau-robotics-humanoids-clean-homes-for-30-an-hour-with-help/) describes modified Unitree G1 bodies at about $55,000. Tau's own pages reviewed here do not name the base robot.

## Privacy

Visits are continuously recorded, footage trains Tau's models, and there is no option to decline ([Tau](../sources/tau-robotics-service-site.md)).
