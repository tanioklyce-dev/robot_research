# Booster T1

**Booster T1** — a bipedal humanoid robot (from Booster Robotics) used as a **second validation platform** alongside the [Unitree G1](unitree-g1.md) in NVIDIA's **[WBC-AGILE](../sources/wbc-agile-github.md)** whole-body-control engine. In AGILE it appears in **stand-up and velocity-tracking** demos with validated sim-to-real transfer.
