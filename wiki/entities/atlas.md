**Atlas** — Boston Dynamics' flagship research humanoid. Originally announced 2013 as a hydraulic platform; **fully-electric Atlas** announced April 2024, retiring the hydraulic version. Owned by Hyundai (acquired Boston Dynamics 2021). Not commercially sold; used internally and in announced industrial pilots (Hyundai factory, Magna automotive).

## Specs (electric Atlas, 2024 generation)
- ~1.5 m tall, ~89 kg.
- 28 DOF; novel actuator design (electric, high-torque hip / shoulder joints with non-anthropomorphic range of motion).
- Multi-finger end-effector for dexterous manipulation.
- Cameras + IMU + LiDAR.

## Position vs other humanoids
- **Highest-profile capability demos** in the field — parkour, dance, dexterous manipulation, gymnastics. The capability bar against which other humanoids get measured.
- **Closed development.** Boston Dynamics doesn't sell Atlas hardware; academic access is essentially zero. Capability claims are vendor-released, not independently reproduced.
- **Industrial pilot strategy.** Atlas is being deployed inside Hyundai factories and via Magna; the strategy is automotive-manufacturing first, not consumer / lab.
