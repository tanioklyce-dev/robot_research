**Hope Jr Arm** — robot arm awarded as the **premium-tier hardware prize** at the [LeRobot Worldwide Hackathon 2025](lerobot-worldwide-hackathon-2025.md). One Hope Jr Arm per team was given to 1st–3rd place (alongside a [LeKiwi](lekiwi.md)) and to 4th–5th place teams. The platform sits above [SO-ARM101](so-arm101.md) in the prize hierarchy, suggesting it is a higher-cost / higher-capability arm within the [LeRobot](lerobot.md) ecosystem.

> [!note] Stub entity
> Hope Jr Arm appears as prize hardware on the hackathon winners page, but its specs, vendor, design lineage, and price haven't been ingested into this wiki. Recorded here only so that source pages have a citation target.
