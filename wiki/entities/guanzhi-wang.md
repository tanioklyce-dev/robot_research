**Guanzhi Wang** — [NVIDIA GEAR](nvidia-gear.md) / Caltech. **The single most direct bridge between the 2023 and 2026 halves of this wiki's [code-as-policy](../concepts/agents/code-as-policy.md) thread**: first author of [Voyager](voyager.md), then co-author of both [CaP-X](cap-x.md) and project co-lead on [ASPIRE](aspire.md).

## Papers in this wiki
- **[Voyager](../sources/voyager-paper.md)** (TMLR 2024) — first author. The ever-growing executable-code skill library in Minecraft.
- **[CaP-X](../sources/cap-x-paper.md)** (ICML 2026) — co-author.
- **[ASPIRE](../sources/aspire-paper.md)** (Jun 2026) — project co-lead. Voyager's architecture ported to real manipulation.

## Why it matters in this wiki
When the wiki says "ASPIRE is GEAR porting its own Voyager pattern to robotics," Wang is the evidence: automatic curriculum → evolutionary search, code skill library → repair-knowledge library, self-verification → per-primitive execution traces, carried across three years and one domain by the same author. The claim is authorship, not resemblance.
