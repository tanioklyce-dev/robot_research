**Dhruv Shah** — the lead author of Berkeley RAIL's open visual-navigation line (GNM, ViNT, NoMaD, ViKiNG, RECON) under [Sergey Levine](sergey-levine.md); now faculty at Princeton (per the [MBRA](../sources/mbra-paper.md) affiliation footnote), where he is senior author on MBRA / LogoNav. Co-organizer of the [Earth Rover Challenge](../sources/earth-rover-challenge-frodobots-2k.md), the multi-city AI-versus-human navigation competition run on [FrodoBots](frodobots.md) rovers — which makes him a bridge between the crowdsourced-data network and the academic policies trained on it.

## In this wiki

- [MBRA / LogoNav](../sources/mbra-paper.md) — senior author; relabeling 700 h of crowdsourced sidewalk data with a model-based expert.
- [Earth Rover Challenge](../sources/earth-rover-challenge-frodobots-2k.md) — co-organizer, alongside DeepMind's Wenhao Yu and Tingnan Zhang.
- [GNM](../sources/gnm-paper.md) (co-lead), [ViNT](../sources/vint-paper.md) (lead), [NoMaD](../sources/nomad-paper.md) (second author), [OmniVLA](../sources/omnivla-paper.md) — the full line is ingested; see [visual navigation policies](../concepts/robotics/visual-navigation-policies.md).
