# Agile ONE

**Agile ONE** — the first humanoid robot from Munich's [Agile Robots](agile-robots.md), announced November 2025 for **industrial** deployment, with series production from **early 2026** at a new **Bavarian** facility.

## Specifications

| Attribute | Value |
|---|---|
| Height | 174 cm |
| Weight | 69 kg |
| Payload | 20 kg |
| Walking speed | 2 m/s (7.2 km/h) |
| Hand DOF | 21 per hand |
| Sensing | Fingertip sensors + force-torque sensors in every joint |

Source: [Agile ONE launch](../sources/agile-robots-agile-one-launch.md) (specs triangulated with heise online / DirectIndustry).

## Capabilities & design

- **"World-leading dexterous hands"** — 21 DOF approaching human-hand dexterity, with fingertip and per-joint force-torque sensing inherited from [Agile Robots](agile-robots.md)' arm heritage.
- **Layered AI architecture** with specialized cognition levels; trained on "one of Europe's largest real-world industrial datasets."
- **Human-centric design**: responsive eyes and proximity sensors for safe co-working alongside people.
- **Target tasks**: material gathering/transport, pick-and-place, machine tending, tool use, fine manipulation.

## Positioning

Unlike standalone-humanoid competitors, Agile ONE ships as **one node in [Agile Robots](agile-robots.md)' integrated stack** (humanoid + AMRs + arms + vision under the AgileCore platform). AI is developed with **[Google DeepMind](google-deepmind.md)** and Deutsche Telekom's Industrial AI Cloud. No price has been announced — the integrated-solution framing suggests it may not be sold as a bare unit.
