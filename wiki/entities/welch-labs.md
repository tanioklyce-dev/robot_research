**Welch Labs** — independent AI-pedagogy operation founded by **[Stephen Welch](stephen-welch.md)**. Operates a YouTube channel (running since ~2014, restarted in 2024–2025) and as of Feb 2026 publishes a **376-page illustrated textbook**, [*The Welch Labs Illustrated Guide to AI, Volume I*](../sources/welchlabs-illustrated-guide-to-ai.md). Based in Winston-Salem, NC.

## What Welch Labs publishes
- **YouTube videos** — chapter-aligned with the book. Each book chapter has a corresponding Welch Labs video. The wiki has filed two: [Welch Labs Perceptron (Ch 1 companion)](../sources/welchlabs-perceptron.md) and [Welch Labs — Yann LeCun's $1B Bet Against LLMs](../sources/welchlabs-lecun-1b-bet-against-llms.md).
- **The Illustrated Guide to AI book** — Vol I (Feb 2026, 376 pp); Vol II teased in the preface. 9 chapters: perceptron → gradient descent → backprop → deep learning → AlexNet → scaling laws → mechanistic interpretability → attention → diffusion.
- **Supporting code** at [github.com/stephencwelch/ai_book](https://github.com/stephencwelch/ai_book).
- **Exercise solutions** at the back of the book; a printable PDF of exercises only at [welchlabs.com/ai_book](https://welchlabs.com/ai_book).

## Pedagogical position
- **Depth over breadth** is Welch's explicit stance (preface). Vol I covers 9 topics across 376 pages instead of trying to survey the field.
- **Hands-on with code** — every chapter has Python code in the book itself + the GitHub repo. Welch's framing: *"depending on your background and learning style, it can be really nice to just see the code."*
- **Annotation-friendly** — wide margins designed for reader writing. Welch quotes the Fermat's-last-theorem margin note as inspiration.

## Why it matters in this wiki
- One of the wiki's three canonical pedagogy publishers: **[Sutton & Barto](../sources/sutton-barto-rl-textbook.md)** (RL theory), **[3Blue1Brown / Grant Sanderson](../sources/3blue1brown-mlp-in-llms.md)** (MLP geometry / visual intuition), and **Welch Labs** (the full neural-net → attention → diffusion lineage). Future "what should I read?" queries route cleanly through these three.
- Grant Sanderson (3Blue1Brown) is in the book's acknowledgments — the two pedagogical projects are loosely connected.
- The Welch Labs book is **the only ingested primary source** in the wiki that walks through LLM-side scaling laws (Ch 6, Kaplan 2020), mechanistic interpretability (Ch 7, Templeton 2024 + Olah), and DeepSeek's Multi-Head Latent Attention (Ch 8, Liu 2024). For each of those threads it's currently the wiki's accessible-pedagogy entry point.
