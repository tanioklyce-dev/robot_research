**Katherine Driggs-Campbell** — Associate Professor, Department of Electrical and Computer Engineering, University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign (UIUC). Research focus: human–robot interaction in safety-critical systems, autonomous navigation in human-centered environments, dialogue-based and language-grounded robot control.

## Papers in this wiki
- **[DRAGON — Dialogue-Based Robot for Assistive Navigation (Liu et al. 2024)](../sources/dragon-assistive-nav-2024.md)** — IEEE RA-L 2024. Corresponding-author / senior PI; built a TurtleBot 2i guide robot using CLIP-based landmark grounding + dialogue for persons with visual impairments. Collaboration with Wendy A. Rogers (UIUC Applied Health Sciences).

## Why it matters in this wiki
DRAGON is one of the few **assistive-navigation** papers in the wiki that pairs a real robot with end-to-end visual-language grounding and a dialogue system designed for blind users. It complements the [HCR Lab](hcrlab.md) cluster (which centers on manipulation + feeding) by addressing the navigation domain — one of the three "spikes" of PAR research per [Nanavati et al. 2024](../sources/nanavati2024-physically-assistive-robots-review.md). UIUC's pairing of an HRI lab with a partner in Applied Health Sciences (Rogers) is a useful structural pattern.
