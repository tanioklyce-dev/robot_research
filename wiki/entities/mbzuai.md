**MBZUAI** — graduate research university in Abu Dhabi, president [Eric Xing](eric-xing.md). Its **Institute of Foundation Models** is the home of [Critique of World Model](../sources/critique-of-world-model-paper.md) and the [PAN](pan-world-model.md) world model, and therefore of the **Generative Latent Prediction** position — the most explicit institutional counter-programme to the [LeCun](yann-lecun.md) / [AMI Labs](ami-labs.md) latent-prediction line that this wiki tracks.

## In this wiki

- [Critique of World Model](../sources/critique-of-world-model-paper.md) (2025–26) — the position paper; three propositions, a blueprint, no experiments.
- PAN (arXiv 2511.09057) — the results paper, **not ingested**.
