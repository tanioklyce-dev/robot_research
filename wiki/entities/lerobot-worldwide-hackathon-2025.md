**LeRobot Worldwide Hackathon 2025** — global hybrid (online + local) hackathon held **June 14–15, 2025**, organized by [Hugging Face](hugging-face.md) around the [LeRobot](lerobot.md) framework. Theme: *"The ChatGPT moment of robotics."* 916 registered team members; ~400 total submissions; 30 ranked winners published at the [all-winners HF Space](../sources/lerobot-worldwide-hackathon-2025-winners.md).

## Key facts

- **Dates**: 2025-06-14 to 2025-06-15.
- **Format**: hybrid — online plus local satellite hackathons worldwide.
- **Organizers**: Marine Caous (`maringetxway`), Thomas Wolf (`thomwolf`), [Remi Cadene](remi-cadene.md) (`cadene`) — Hugging Face.
- **Sponsors visible on the winners page**: Hugging Face, [Seeed Studio](seeed-studio.md), plus additional sponsors (aggregate image).
- **Outputs created during the event** (per the [LeRobot-worldwide-hackathon HF org page](https://huggingface.co/LeRobot-worldwide-hackathon)): 189 datasets, 12 models, 11 Spaces.

## Prize structure

| Rank | Per-team hardware |
|---|---|
| 🥇 1st–3rd | [Hope Jr Arm](hope-jr-arm.md) + [LeKiwi](lekiwi.md) |
| 🥈 4th–5th | [Hope Jr Arm](hope-jr-arm.md) |
| 🥉 6th–24th | [LeKiwi](lekiwi.md) |
| 🥉 25th–30th | [SO-ARM101 (SO-101)](so-arm101.md) |

[LeKiwi](lekiwi.md) was prize hardware in **22 of 30** ranked positions — the clearest signal of its centrality in the LeRobot ecosystem.

## Why it matters in this wiki

This event is the **ecosystem-scale evidence** for the LeRobot stack we ingested separately ([XLeRobot](xlerobot.md), [LeKiwi](lekiwi.md), [SO-ARM101](so-arm101.md)). 916 team members + ~400 submissions + 189 datasets is not a niche community — it's a robotics analog of what large open-source ML hackathons were doing 5–10 years earlier. The result implies the **buy → assemble → teleop → train → deploy** loop is being closed in practice by hundreds of small teams worldwide, not just by the well-resourced labs that produce wiki-cited papers.
