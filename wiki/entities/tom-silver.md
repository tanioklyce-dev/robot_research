**Tom Silver** — researcher in symbolic planning, abstraction learning, and task-and-motion planning; first author of **[Generalized Planning in PDDL Domains with Pretrained LLMs](../sources/generalized-planning-pddl-llm-paper.md)** (AAAI 2024) with [Tenenbaum](josh-tenenbaum.md), [Kaelbling](leslie-kaelbling.md), and IBM Research collaborators.

_Stub._ That paper is the wiki's entry point to [symbolic task planning](../concepts/agents/symbolic-task-planning.md), and the source of the **No Names** ablation — the sharpest of three independent findings that semantic names beat opaque labels over identical structure.
