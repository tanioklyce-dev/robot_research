**Robert Morris** (Sr.) — Bell Labs cryptographer and Unix contributor (the `crypt` utility, password hashing), later **chief scientist at the NSA's National Computer Security Center**. (Father of **Robert Tappan Morris**, author of the 1988 **Morris worm** — a fitting self-replication footnote.) In this wiki he enters as the author of the **species that "won" [Darwin](../sources/darwin-1961-bell-labs-game.md)**.

## Role in this wiki
- **Wrote the lethal adaptive species** that ended the original [Darwin](../sources/darwin-1961-bell-labs-game.md) runs: a 44-cell organism with an **adaptive search** that tuned its "poking increment" against each opponent's protection pattern and passed successful increments to its offspring — a within-round adaptation that "cleaned up."
- A landmark in the wiki's [self-replicating-program lineage](../concepts/alife/artificial-life-and-self-replication.md): the first time a *self-tuning* competitor dominated a digital-organism arena.
