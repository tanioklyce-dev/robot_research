**Adithyavairavan Murali** — research scientist at [NVIDIA](nvidia.md), working on 6-DOF robotic grasping and manipulation. Senior author on [GraspGen-X](graspgen.md) ([Han et al., CVPR 2026](../sources/graspgenx-paper.md)) and first author of the **GraspGen** framework (2025) it extends, as well as of the earlier target-driven grasping-in-clutter line (Murali et al., 2020) that introduced the pose-retargeting heuristic GraspGen-X later measures and beats.

The through-line across that work is **grasping as a reusable module** — a ranked set of SE(3) poses handed to a planner — rather than as behaviour absorbed into an end-to-end policy. See [6-DOF grasp generation](../concepts/robotics/six-dof-grasp-generation.md).
