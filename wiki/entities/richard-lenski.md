**Richard E. Lenski** — evolutionary biologist at **Michigan State University**, best known for the **Long-Term Evolution Experiment (LTEE)** with *E. coli* (running since 1988). He brought experimental-evolution rigor to **digital organisms** via [Avida](avida.md).

## Role in this wiki
- **Lead author of ["The evolutionary origin of complex features" (2003)](../sources/lenski-2003-evolutionary-origin-complex-features.md)** — the Avida study showing the complex EQU function evolves only when simpler functions are also rewarded (0/50 vs 23/50), a fully-traceable demonstration that complex features arise by Darwinian increments.
- Connects **wet-lab experimental evolution** (LTEE) to the wiki's **digital-evolution** line ([artificial life and the emergence of self-replication](../concepts/alife/artificial-life-and-self-replication.md)); digital organisms let him observe complete genealogies impossible in organic systems.
