**Stephen Welch** — founder of [Welch Labs](welch-labs.md); independent AI educator and creator of *[The Welch Labs Illustrated Guide to AI](../sources/welchlabs-illustrated-guide-to-ai.md)*. Based in Winston-Salem, NC.

## Backstory
Per the Vol I preface: in **summer 2014**, Welch needed to learn neural networks for a startup. After working through a Stanford course and writing his own Python NN library, he made his first YouTube series. That work eventually became [Welch Labs](welch-labs.md), which restarted in earnest in 2024–2025 alongside the book project.

## What he's published in this wiki
- **[The Welch Labs Illustrated Guide to AI, Vol I](../sources/welchlabs-illustrated-guide-to-ai.md)** (Feb 2026, 376 pp). Single-author book covering perceptron → diffusion. Code at [github.com/stephencwelch/ai_book](https://github.com/stephencwelch/ai_book).
- **[Welch Labs Perceptron (video)](../sources/welchlabs-perceptron.md)** — the Ch 1 companion.
- **[Welch Labs — Yann LeCun's $1B Bet Against LLMs (video)](../sources/welchlabs-lecun-1b-bet-against-llms.md)** — LeCun's JEPA pitch, popular explainer.

## Pedagogical style
- **Depth-over-breadth**: Welch's explicit stance — "knowing a few important things really well gets me further than knowing a little about a lot."
- **Hands-on with Python code in the book itself** + GitHub.
- **Encourages reader annotation** of the printed book (wide margins, "annoying colored Flair pens").
- **One YouTube video per chapter**, designed to stand alone or complement the book.

## Why he matters in this wiki
The author of one of the wiki's three canonical pedagogy primary-sources (the other two being [Sutton & Barto](../sources/sutton-barto-rl-textbook.md) and [Grant Sanderson](https://www.youtube.com/@3blue1brown) of 3Blue1Brown). Welch's book is the wiki's **only** ingested primary source covering LLM-side scaling laws ([Ch 6](../sources/welchlabs-illustrated-guide-to-ai.md)), mechanistic interpretability ([Ch 7](../sources/welchlabs-illustrated-guide-to-ai.md)), and DeepSeek's Multi-Head Latent Attention ([Ch 8](../sources/welchlabs-illustrated-guide-to-ai.md)) at pedagogy depth.
