**UnitedMasters** — a music distribution and licensing company that Jordan uses as the real-world instantiation of a **three-way collectivist-AI market** ([Jordan 2025](../sources/jordan-collectivist-economic-ai.md), §4.1, Fig 2). [Michael I. Jordan](michael-i-jordan.md) is a board member and consulted on the market design.

## The three-way market design
Three vertices linked by trained ML recommendation models ([Jordan 2025](../sources/jordan-collectivist-economic-ai.md)):
1. **Musicians** — supply songs.
2. **Listeners** — linked to musicians by a classical ML recommender.
3. **Brands** — the critical third vertex. Brands need music fitting their image and demographic; an ML model supplies a brand with an artist's track and **the artist is paid in that moment**. Audience reaction is measured and visible to other brands, who are then incentivized to reach out and partner with the artist.

The point of the design is that **incentives are built in** — unlike classic streaming, where money is made at the platform (subscriptions/ads) with a weak incentive to route revenue back to musicians, and where the platform even has an incentive to replace musicians with generative AI.

## Scale (per Jordan 2025)
- **>1.5 million musicians** signed to date.
- Music used by brands including the **NBA, Bose, and State Farm**.
- Cited as "an instance of a collectivist AI system that has created jobs."
