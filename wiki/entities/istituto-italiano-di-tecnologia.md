# Istituto Italiano di Tecnologia (IIT)

Italian national research foundation headquartered in **Genova**, and one of Europe's larger robotics research concentrations. The wiki's exposure to it so far is through two of its labs, both authors on the [contact-rich safe-learning survey](../sources/safe-learning-contact-rich-survey.md):

- **HRII — Human-Robot Interfaces and Interaction**, led by [Arash Ajoudani](arash-ajoudani.md). Physical human-robot interaction, interaction control, variable impedance, ergonomics, and safe learning for contact-rich tasks.
- **HHCM — Humanoids and Human Centered Mechatronics**, led by **Nikos Tsagarakis**. Humanoid and legged platform design and control.

Doctoral training runs partly through **DRIM** (the Italian national PhD programme in Robotics and Intelligent Machines) jointly with the **Università di Genova**.

> [!note] Deliberately thin
> This page records only what an ingested source establishes: the two labs, their leads, the Genova location, and the DRIM/UniGe doctoral arrangement. IIT's broader programme — its humanoid platforms, its other research lines, its funding and scale — is **not** asserted here because no ingested source covers it. Worth expanding when one does; IIT's humanoid work in particular is a real gap in this wiki's European coverage.
