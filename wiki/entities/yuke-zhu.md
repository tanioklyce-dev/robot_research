**Yuke Zhu** — Assistant Professor at UT Austin CS, with a dual appointment at [[nvidia|NVIDIA]] Research. Embodied-AI / manipulation researcher. In this wiki, **senior on [[robocasa365-paper|RoboCasa365]]** — the 365-task household-manipulation benchmark that appears across both the VLA cohort and (now) the JEPA cohort via [[jepa-wms|JEPA-WMs]].

## Papers in this wiki
- **[[robocasa365-paper|RoboCasa365 Paper]]** (Nasiriany, Nasiriany, Maddukuri, Zhu — ICLR 2026) — senior author. 365 tasks across 60 kitchen activities, 2,500 scenes, 3,200+ objects, 2,200+ hours of teleop + synthetic data, 500K+ trajectories.

## Why it matters in this wiki
- **Bridge between academic and NVIDIA-Research lineages.** Zhu's dual appointment is the connection that puts NVIDIA Research as a co-author on RoboCasa365 — explaining how RoboCasa fits into NVIDIA's broader Physical AI stack (Isaac Lab + GR00T + RoboCasa eval).
- **Center of gravity for [[robocasa|RoboCasa]] line.** The Nasiriany / Maddukuri / Zhu group has produced both the original RoboCasa (2024) and RoboCasa365 (ICLR 2026); future iterations likely come from the same group.

Adjacent Zhu-affiliated work not ingested: robosuite (the robosuite framework that underlies many manipulation benchmarks including LIBERO), MimicGen (the synthetic-demo expansion tool RoboCasa365 uses), various humanoid-policy work.
