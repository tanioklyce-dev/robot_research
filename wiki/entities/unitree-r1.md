**Unitree R1** — small, low-cost biped from [Unitree](unitree.md): 1,230 mm, 27–29 kg, 20–26 joints, about 2 kg arm load ([Unitree](../sources/unitree-h2-r1-product-pages.md)).

## Pricing and access

R1 Air $4,900 and R1 $5,900, neither with secondary development. R1 EDU is $10,500 in the store. The **R1-A / R1-A-D** dual-arm variants list from $4,290 to $7,690 and were unavailable on 2026-11-08 ([Unitree](../sources/unitree-h2-r1-product-pages.md)).
