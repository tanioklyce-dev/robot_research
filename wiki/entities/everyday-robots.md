**Everyday Robots** — the Alphabet/X robotics company whose **mobile manipulator** (7-DoF arm, two-finger gripper, wheeled base) was the hardware platform for Google's entire 2022–2023 LLM-robotics line. Wound down as an independent effort in 2023, with work absorbed into Google DeepMind.

## Why it appears in this wiki
It is the robot underneath [SayCan](saycan.md), [Inner Monologue](inner-monologue.md), and [Code as Policies](../sources/code-as-policies-paper.md)' kitchen domain — i.e. essentially every real-world number in the pre-2026 half of the [code-as-policy](../concepts/agents/code-as-policy.md) lineage was collected on this one platform, in one or two office kitchens with 15 objects and 5 locations.

> [!note] A generalization caveat worth remembering
> When comparing 2022-era results to 2026 ones, note that the earlier real-world evidence is **single-platform and single-environment**. The [SayCan](../sources/saycan-paper.md) mock-vs-real kitchen gap (74% → 60% execution) is the only environment-shift measurement in that cohort.
