**Craig Reynolds** — computer graphics researcher who created the **[boids](../concepts/alife/flocking-and-boids.md)** model of flocking in **1986** and published it as "Flocks, Herds, and Schools: a Distributed Behavioral Model" at **SIGGRAPH '87**. His work founded **agent-based flocking** and is one of the most-cited demonstrations of emergence — complex group motion from simple local steering rules.

## Contributions in this wiki
- **Boids (1986).** Three local steering rules — separation, alignment, cohesion — produce realistic emergent flocking with no central control ([Reynolds page](../sources/reynolds-boids-page.md)). See [Flocking and boids](../concepts/alife/flocking-and-boids.md).
- **First boids animation:** *Stanley and Stella in: Breaking the Ice* (SIGGRAPH '87 Electronic Theater), with Symbolics Graphics Division + Whitney/Demos Productions ([Reynolds page](../sources/reynolds-boids-page.md)).
- His model was first used in a feature film for ***Batman Returns* (1992)** (bat/penguin flocks) ([Reynolds page](../sources/reynolds-boids-page.md)).
- Frames boids as **emergence** at the "edge of chaos" (citing Chris Langton) ([Reynolds page](../sources/reynolds-boids-page.md)).
