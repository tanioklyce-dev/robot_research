**Jean Ponce** — senior computer-vision researcher; joint appointment at **École normale supérieure / PSL** and **NYU**. Recurring senior author on FAIR's JEPA-line papers.

## What we know
- **JEPA-WMs co-author** (Terver, Yang, **Ponce**, Bardes, LeCun; [paper](../sources/jepa-wms-paper.md), TMLR 05/2026). The recurring "Inria/ENS-side" senior author next to LeCun and Bardes on FAIR JEPA work.
- **VICReg co-author** (Bardes, **Ponce**, LeCun; [VICReg paper](../sources/vicreg-paper.md), ICLR 2022) — the variance-invariance-covariance regularizer that LeCun's 2022 AMI position paper endorses as JEPA's canonical anti-collapse method.
