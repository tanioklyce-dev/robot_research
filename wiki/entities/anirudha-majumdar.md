**Anirudha Majumdar** — Princeton University, with [Google DeepMind](google-deepmind.md) affiliation. **First author of [Predictive Red Teaming](roboart.md)** ("work done while on sabbatical at Google DeepMind") and co-author on ASIMOV and the Veo evaluation report.

## Papers in this wiki
- **[Predictive Red Teaming](../sources/predictive-red-teaming-paper.md)** (2025) — first author; the [RoboART](roboart.md) pipeline.
- **[ASIMOV Benchmark](../sources/asimov-benchmark-paper.md)** (CoRL 2025) — co-author.
- **[Evaluating Gemini Robotics Policies in a Veo World Simulator](../sources/veo-robotics-policy-evaluation-paper.md)** (2025) — co-author.

## Why it matters in this wiki
He is the bridge between the **statistical-guarantees** tradition (conformal prediction, which is the calibration mechanism inside RoboART) and empirical robot safety. RoboART's use of conformal thresholds is the wiki's first ingested instance of distribution-free uncertainty quantification applied to a robot policy — a middle path between the [formal](../concepts/learning/formal-verification.md) and [benchmark](../concepts/safety/semantic-safety.md) poles of this cluster.
