**myBuddy 280** — [Elephant Robotics](elephant-robotics.md)' dual-arm collaborative robot. 13 DOF (6 per arm + 1 torso), 280mm working radius per arm, 250g payload per arm. Raspberry Pi 4B compute with ROS1, MoveIt, and a 7-inch touchscreen. ~$1,619.

## Specs ([myBuddy 280 product page](../sources/elephant-robotics-mybuddy-280.md))
- DOF: 13 (6+6+1 torso)
- Working radius: 280mm per arm
- Payload: 250g per arm
- Accuracy: ±0.5mm
- Weight: 2.75 kg
- Joint speed: 160°/s
- Compute: Raspberry Pi 4B + ESP32 auxiliary
- Display: 7-inch touchscreen
- Cameras: dual 2MP HD

## Software
Python, ROS1 + RViz + MoveIt, myBlockly, myStudio, open-source URDF.

## Position
Entry-level desktop dual-arm research robot. Not a humanoid — a tabletop system. The closest affordable dual-arm alternative to the Franka / UR research tier. Useful for dual-arm manipulation research without a mobile base.
