**HIW-500** — **500+ hours / 23,743 episodes** of whole-body teleoperation of a [Unitree G1](unitree-g1.md) doing household tasks in **12 real homes in Southeast Asia**, released June 2026 by [BitRobot](bitrobot.md) with [Unitree](unitree.md) and Hugging Face under **CC BY 4.0** ([dataset page](../sources/bitrobot-hiw-500-dataset-page.md)). The largest permissively licensed humanoid dataset in this wiki, and a named ingredient of [UnifoLM-WLA-1.0](unifolm.md)'s ≈2,500 h training mix ([WLA page](../sources/unifolm-wla-1-project-page.md)).

## What it is

- **11 tasks**, from 30-second table setting (5,526 episodes) to 8.7-minute children's-table assembly (760 episodes, 110 h — the BitRobot IKEA-assembly challenge task). 161 subtask labels, 148K+ timed annotations.
- **Navigation-dominant:** the top subtask labels are *move to table / bed / trash can / sink / fridge / countertop* — a mobile-manipulation corpus with short grasp-and-place primitives between walks.
- **Sensors:** head stereo camera (1280×480 side-by-side) + two wrist RealSense D405 (RGB + stereo IR), 480p @ 30 fps; 29-DoF joint state, IMU, odometry.
- **Action = the whole-body-controller command (23-D):** base velocity/pose/height (7) + left and right end-effector 6-DoF pose + gripper trigger/squeeze. It records what the WBC was told, not joint targets — see [whole-body control](../concepts/robotics/whole-body-control.md).
- Formats: raw ROS bag / MCAP (~10 TB) and **LeRobot v3.0** (AV1 video; `language_persistent` + `language_events`).

> [!warning] Three hour figures
> Headline 500+ h; per-task chart 485 h; LeRobot frame count ÷ 30 fps = **378 h**. Quote ~380 h for the LeRobot repo.

## Why it matters in this wiki

- **The first humanoid dataset here with a CC BY license** — [DROID](droid.md) and [OXE](open-x-embodiment.md) are arm datasets; [EgoDex](egodex.md) is human video; Unitree's own G1 sets are CC BY-NC-SA.
- **In-the-wild homes, not a lab** — the environment diversity the [crowdsourcing](../concepts/learning/crowdsourced-robot-training-data.md) page argues cannot be staged, obtained here by sending operators into 12 homes rather than by filming the public.
- **A WBC-interface action space** is the concrete precedent for what a humanoid VLA's "lower-body" outputs look like in practice.

## Open questions

- Collection protocol (operators, rig, consent) is unpublished; the public set is a sample of a commercially licensed larger corpus.
- **License vs the network's own rule.** BitRobot's [whitepaper](../sources/bitrobot-network-whitepaper.md) §4.4 says emission-funded subnet outputs default to open-source *non-commercial*; HIW-500 is CC BY 4.0. Either it was a Foundation or private-subnet output, or the default changed. The "public sample + commercial licensing" structure is the whitepaper's second economic loop.
- Whether any published policy result trains on it — UnifoLM-WLA-1.0 names it but reports no numbers.
