**Keen Technologies** — the research company behind [Physical Atari](physical-atari.md). The paper's authors are Khurram Javed, Joseph Modayil, Gloria Kennickell, Richard S. Sutton and John Carmack ([paper](../sources/physical-atari-paper.md)). Sutton is also affiliated with the University of Alberta, and Modayil with Openmind Research Institute.

Its [research page](https://keenagi.com/research/physical-atari/) lists Physical Atari as active work. No ingested source covers the company's founding, funding or wider programme.

## Why it matters in this wiki

Sutton co-wrote the [standard RL textbook](../sources/sutton-barto-rl-textbook.md), and this paper applies the line of work to physical hardware: average-reward evaluation, learning between actions and experiments that run for days without a reset by a person.
