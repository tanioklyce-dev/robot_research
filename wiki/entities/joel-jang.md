**Joel Jang** — research scientist at [NVIDIA GEAR](nvidia-gear.md). Project lead on **[DreamDojo](../sources/dreamdojo-paper.md)** (ICML 2026 Spotlight) alongside [Yuke Zhu](yuke-zhu.md) and [Jim Fan](jim-fan.md) — the foundation generative-video world model trained on 44,711 hr of egocentric human video. Cited as the third name in the project-lead triad for the **DreamGen → DreamZero → DreamDojo** world-model program at GEAR.

## Why it matters in this wiki
The third co-lead of the GEAR world-model program. While Jim Fan is the public-facing voice and Yuke Zhu is the academic-bridge co-lead, Joel Jang appears to be the *operational* co-lead on the WM line — the third name on each Dream* paper.
