**Michael I. Jordan** — machine-learning and statistics researcher at **Inria Paris** and the **University of California, Berkeley**; one of the most-cited figures in ML. In the late 1990s–2010s his lab anchored the *statistical* and *graphical-models* tradition of ML (variational inference, Bayesian nonparametrics, latent Dirichlet allocation with Blei & Ng, spectral clustering). Since ~2015 his agenda has shifted toward the **intersection of machine learning and microeconomics** — mechanism design, incentives, and markets as first-class objects in ML system design. Not to be confused with the basketball player of the same name.

> [!note] Distinct person
> Unrelated to [Jim Fan / Linxi Fan](jim-fan.md), [Michael Freedman](michael-freedman.md), or any robotics figure elsewhere in this wiki. Jordan's connection to the wiki is conceptual (economics-of-ML / AI-society), not robotics.

## The "ML-meets-microeconomics" agenda

Jordan's recent program argues that AI should be understood **collectivist-economically** rather than as individual cognition. The capstone statement is [A Collectivist, Economic Perspective on AI](../sources/jordan-collectivist-economic-ai.md) (2025), which proposes a **tripartite blend** of [computational, inferential, and economic thinking](../concepts/economics/three-thinking-styles.md) for designing real-world ML systems.

Technical lines feeding that essay (co-authored, cited within it):
- **Prediction-powered inference** — Angelopoulos, Bates, Fannjiang, Jordan, Zrnic, *Science* 2023. See [PPI](../concepts/economics/prediction-powered-inference.md).
- **Statistical contract theory / principal-agent hypothesis testing** — Bates, Jordan, Sklar, Soloff, 2024. See [Mechanism design](../concepts/economics/mechanism-design.md).
- **Three-layer data markets** — Fallah, Jordan, Makhdoumi, Malekian, 2024. See [Collectivist AI](../concepts/economics/collectivist-ai.md).
- **Privacy-aware learning** — Duchi, Jordan, Wainwright, *J. ACM* 2014 (privacy/inference tradeoffs).
