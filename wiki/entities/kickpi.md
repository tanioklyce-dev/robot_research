# KickPi

Rockchip development-board vendor and ODM ("open source hardware and software, providing one-stop customized services"), offering RK3566 / RK3568 custom mainboards, Linux adaptation and AI deployment. In the wiki as the author of a [Microduck "case study"](../sources/kickpi-rk3566-microduck-case-study.md) written from Pollen's public docs to advertise RK3566 robotics mainboards — accurate on the headline facts, wrong on the runtime's process split (it invents a separate servo daemon), and silent on every measured number. Also publishes similar ODM case studies (RV1126 face-recognition access control, RK3566 smart-home panels), none ingested.
