**Bill Gosper** (R. William Gosper) — pioneering MIT-tradition hacker and mathematician; in this wiki, the figure most associated with the early engineering of the **[Game of Life](game-of-life.md)**.

## Contribution
- Discovered the first **glider gun** (period-30) in **1970**, settling Conway's open challenge to find an indefinitely-growing pattern — a foundational result that opened up Game-of-Life "construction" ([Wolfram, 2025](../sources/wolfram-2025-game-of-life-engineering.md)).
- Later created **Hashlife**, the memoized quadtree algorithm that simulates Life patterns over astronomically many steps (background; not in the ingested source).
