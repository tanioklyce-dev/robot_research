# AndyMark

Major [FIRST Robotics Competition](first-robotics-competition.md) vendor and field-element manufacturer. Co-founded by Andy Baker (Woodie Flowers Award recipient, teams 3940 and 45). Based in Kokomo, Indiana.

## FRC role

- **Field elements**: Manufactures one of two official FRC field variants (the other is the "Welded FIELD"); AndyMark fields used at many District and international events ([FRC 2026 Game Manual](../sources/frc-2026-game-manual.md), §5.1, Table 5-1/5-2).
- **FUEL**: Sole source for the custom 5.91in foam-ball SCORING ELEMENT (part am-5801) ([FRC 2026 Game Manual](../sources/frc-2026-game-manual.md), §5.10.1).
- **AM14U6 chassis**: The drivetrain underlying the [FRC KitBot](frc-kitbot.md); included in the Kit of Parts. A multi-season FRC staple ([FRC KitBot 2026](../sources/frc-kitbot-2026.md)).
- **Motors**: Distributes the AndyMark 9015, NeveRest, PG, RedLine, and Snow Blower motors — all on the FRC approved motor list ([FRC 2026 Game Manual](../sources/frc-2026-game-manual.md), Table 8-1).
- **Wheels, gearboxes, hardware**: Broad catalog of FRC-specific mechanical components.

## Field variants (2026)

AndyMark fields are deployed at events where the welded steel field is not practical. The two field types have the same critical dimensions and expected user experience, with minor structural differences in guardrails and driver stations ([FRC 2026 Game Manual](../sources/frc-2026-game-manual.md), §5.1).

| Region | Field Type |
|--------|-----------|
| US (most) | Welded |
| Brazil, China, Mexico, Türkiye | AndyMark |
| FIRST in Texas, Indiana, Wisconsin, NC | AndyMark |
| NE FIRST | AndyMark |
| All FIRST Championship fields | Welded |
