**Dingkang Liang** — researcher at [HUST](hust.md); **project lead** on **[TurboVLA](turbovla.md)** ([paper](../sources/turbovla-paper.md)). Also an author on the group's adjacent lines cited by that paper: the *ORION* end-to-end autonomous-driving VLA, the *Hermes* self-driving world model, and *Discrete Diffusion VLA* (DDVLA, ICML 2026) — which appears in TurboVLA's own comparison table as an acceleration-oriented baseline.

_Stub._ Notable that the same author group produced both an LLM-centric acceleration approach (DDVLA, 7.5 B / 60.8 ms) and the LLM-free alternative that supersedes it on their own table — TurboVLA reads partly as a self-critique.
