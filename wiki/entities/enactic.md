**Enactic, Inc.** — Japanese company (enactic.ai; GitHub organization created 2025-05-16, "Japan") that develops and maintains the open-hardware [OpenArm](openarm.md) arm, its Cell and KER accessories, and the surrounding software (ROS 2, Dora nodes, Isaac Lab and MuJoCo assets, dataset tooling). It does not appear to manufacture the arm itself: units come from an **official manufacturing partner (RT Corporation, Tokyo)** and a **certified manufacturer ([WowRobo](wowrobo.md), Shenzhen)** plus a directory of unevaluated vendors, and motors are sold *"through the project."* Enactic also hosts the [Damiao](damiao.md) motor datasheets at damiao.enactic.ai.

## Position

- Model: **copyleft hardware (CERN-OHL-S-2.0) + permissive software (Apache-2.0)**, with the company holding the design, the docs, the manufacturer certification and the community (Discord, GitHub Discussions). Revenue path is not stated in any ingested source.
- Stated aim for 2.0: reproducibility — *"turn isolated experiments into shared progress"* via the OpenArm Cell.
- 51 public repos in the organization; the umbrella repo has 3,038 stars (2026-09-13).
