**Shirley Ryan AbilityLab** (SRAlab) — a rehabilitation hospital in Chicago with research labs built into its clinical floors; until March 2017 it was the Rehabilitation Institute of Chicago ([lab pages](../sources/shirley-ryan-abilitylab-research-labs.md)).

## Why it is in this wiki

It hosts the [argallab](argallab.md), which calls itself the only academic robotics autonomy lab in the United States located inside a rehabilitation hospital. That makes it one of the few places where shared-control and robot-learning research is tested routinely with people who have motor impairments ([lab pages](../sources/shirley-ryan-abilitylab-research-labs.md)).

## Research model

- The hospital reports *"more than 350 studies under way"*.
- Its five "Ability Labs" (Arms + Hands, Legs + Walking, Strength + Endurance, Think + Speak, Pediatric) put clinicians, scientists, engineers and patients in the same space.
- About 30 labs are linked from its research page. Many principal investigators also hold Northwestern University faculty appointments.

All three points are the institution's own description ([lab pages](../sources/shirley-ryan-abilitylab-research-labs.md)).

## Robotics-related groups

| Group | Led by | Focus |
|---|---|---|
| [argallab](argallab.md) (Assistive & Rehabilitation Robotics Lab) | Brenna Argall | Autonomy and shared control for powered wheelchairs and assistive robot arms |
| Regenstein Foundation Center for Bionic Medicine | Levi Hargrove (founded 2006 by Todd Kuiken) | Powered prostheses, exoskeletons, wearable sensing; the open-source bionic leg |
| Neural Engineering for Prosthetics & Orthotics Lab | part of the Center for Bionic Medicine | Control of powered legs and hands from muscle signals |
| Robotics Lab | Sandro Mussa-Ivaldi, James Patton | Therapy robots, error augmentation, body-machine interfaces |
| Max Näder Center | part of the Center for Bionic Medicine | Outcomes research on prosthetics, orthotics, exoskeletons and wearables |
| Slutzky Neuroprosthetics Lab | — | Brain-machine and muscle-signal interfaces for movement and speech |

Source for the table: [lab pages](../sources/shirley-ryan-abilitylab-research-labs.md). The Center for Bionic Medicine reports more than 63 staff, over 200 papers and $25 million in grants, on a page dated 2021.

## Ingested work

- **[Open-source bionic leg](../sources/open-source-bionic-leg-paper.md)** (Nature Biomedical Engineering, 2020): a powered knee and ankle built from drone motors and belt drives at 2–5 times lower gear ratios than comparable prostheses, costing US$10,000–30,000, with open control software. Tested with three people with above-knee amputations in the hospital.
- **[REACT wheelchair shared control](../sources/react-shared-control-wheelchair-paper.md)** (RSS 2026) and **[interface-aware arm teleoperation](../sources/interface-aware-arm-teleoperation-sci-paper.md)** (IJRR 2025), both from the argallab.
- **[Project pages](../sources/shirley-ryan-abilitylab-project-pages.md)**: the COMET center on machines that work alongside therapists, a lightweight powered leg for older users with a variable-transmission knee, and a manual wheelchair that can be propelled while standing.

**A caution the hospital's own history supplies:** after three funding cycles of robot-assisted therapy research, its Robotics Lab reported that robots guiding repetitive motion gave variable gains and slow clinical acceptance, and moved toward simpler devices that keep the therapist involved ([project pages](../sources/shirley-ryan-abilitylab-project-pages.md)).
