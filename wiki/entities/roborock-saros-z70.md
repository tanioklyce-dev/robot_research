**Roborock Saros Z70** — shipping robot vacuum with a retractable 5-axis arm rated to 300 g ([source](../sources/roborock-saros-z70.md)).

## Usefulness

Reviewers rate it a capable vacuum, but the autonomous arm is limited and buggy. Remote-controlled use works better, and TechRadar notes potential for users with mobility issues ([source](../sources/roborock-saros-z70.md)).
