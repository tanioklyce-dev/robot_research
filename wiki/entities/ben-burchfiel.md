# Ben Burchfiel

Robot-learning researcher and engineer — **co-founder & CTO of [Walden Robotics](walden-robotics.md)** (2026). Over a decade translating robot-learning research into production. One of the recurring [TRI](tri.md) names in the wiki's manipulation-foundation-model line: a co-author on **[Diffusion Policy](diffusion-policy.md)** (as Benjamin Burchfiel) and a contributor to **[OpenVLA](openvla.md)** and TRI's **LBM 1.0**.

## Career

- **PhD** Computer Science, **Duke**; postdoctoral research at **Brown** ([Walden launch](../sources/walden-robotics-launch.md)).
- **Aurora Innovation** — developed production ML for autonomous driving.
- **[Toyota Research Institute](tri.md)** — co-led a **60+ person robot-learning team**; worked on **[Diffusion Policy](diffusion-policy.md)**, **[OpenVLA](openvla.md)**, and **LBM 1.0** (the [TRI LBM](../concepts/learning/large-behavior-models.md) program).
- **[Walden Robotics](walden-robotics.md)** — co-founder & **CTO** (2026); part of the TRI robot-learning leadership that spun out with [Russ Tedrake](russ-tedrake.md).
