**Ashish Kumar** — UC Berkeley researcher (with Jitendra Malik) and **first author of [RMA](../sources/rma-paper.md)**, the rapid-adaptation controller that made blind quadrupeds adjust to terrain, payload and friction in under a second. Co-first author of the [egocentric-vision follow-up](../sources/egocentric-vision-locomotion-paper.md) with [Ananye Agarwal](ananye-agarwal.md).

He is the only person appearing in **two of the three papers** of this wiki's [locomotion adaptation lineage](../syntheses/rl/locomotion-adaptation-lineage.md) besides [Deepak Pathak](deepak-pathak.md) — and unlike Pathak and Agarwal, he is not on the [LocoFormer](../sources/locoformer-paper.md) end of it.

## In this wiki

- **[RMA: Rapid Motor Adaptation for Legged Robots](../sources/rma-paper.md)** (RSS 2021) — **first author**. The extrinsics-vector idea is the paper's core: estimate *how behavior should change* rather than the physics that would explain it, and the ablation shows the distinction is worth 17 points (73.5% vs 56.5%).
- **[Legged Locomotion in Challenging Terrains using Egocentric Vision](../sources/egocentric-vision-locomotion-paper.md)** (CoRL 2022) — **co-first author**. The same two-phase recipe applied to geometry rather than physics, with a proved bound on the distillation.

Those two papers are Acts 1 and 2 of the [lineage](../syntheses/rl/locomotion-adaptation-lineage.md) — i.e. **he co-authored both instances of the engineered privileged-teacher approach**, and neither of the papers that abandons it.
