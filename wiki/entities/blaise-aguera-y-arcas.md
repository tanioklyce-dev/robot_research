**Blaise Agüera y Arcas** — Google VP/Fellow who leads the **Paradigms of Intelligence Team**, an internal group pursuing **non-LLM-centric views of intelligence** (intelligence and life as **emergent from simple interacting computation**). In this wiki he enters as lead author of **["Computational Life" (2024)](../sources/computational-life-self-replicating-programs-paper.md)** — the demonstration that self-replicating programs spontaneously arise from random interacting code with no fitness function.

## Role in this wiki
- **Lead author, "Computational Life"** ([source](../sources/computational-life-self-replicating-programs-paper.md)): self-replicators emerge from random, interacting, self-modifying programs across multiple substrates (BFF/Brainfuck, Forth, Z80, Intel 8080), with SUBLEQ as a counterexample. See [Artificial life and the emergence of self-replication](../concepts/alife/artificial-life-and-self-replication.md).
- His **"Paradigms of Intelligence"** banner is the unifying thread: intelligence is better understood as **emergent / collective / computational-life-like** than as a property scaled into a single large model.

## Position in the broader debate
A distinct voice in the wiki's running **"is LLM-scaling the right north star?"** thread, alongside:
- [Yann LeCun](yann-lecun.md) — world models / JEPA as the substrate of intelligence.
- [Michael I. Jordan](michael-i-jordan.md) — intelligence as a property of **collectives and economic mechanisms**, not individual agents.
- **Agüera y Arcas** — intelligence/life as **emergent from simple interaction** (artificial life, self-organization).

See [critiques of the intelligence north star](../syntheses/society/critiques-of-the-intelligence-north-star.md).
