# TechCrunch — Amazon retires Astro for Business

## Summary

Report, read on 2026-11-08 and referenced by URL, on Amazon ending its business version of [Astro](../entities/amazon-astro.md), based on an email to customers and an Amazon statement. Its lasting value is as a dated example of a purchased robot that stopped working when the vendor withdrew the service.

## Key claims

- **Short life.** Discontinued seven months after launch. Amazon said it wanted to focus on the home model.
- **Robots stopped working.** Astro for Business units "will no longer function" from 25 September 2024.
- **Customer remedy.** A $300 credit, and refunds of unused prepaid Astro Secure fees. Amazon recommended recycling the robots at its expense.
- **Pricing.** $2,350, with subscriptions of $60, $20 and $99 a month.
- **Home version.** Has a cargo bin rated for up to 4.4 pounds.
