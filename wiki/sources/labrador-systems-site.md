# Labrador Systems — company site

## Summary

The company's own product, availability and reservation pages, read on 2026-11-08 and referenced by URL. They describe a self-driving, two-deck cart that fetches special trays, with no arm. **The specifications are labelled targets, and the availability page still gives a past date: production of the Retriever Pro in Q2 2024.** The site's copyright says 2026, but the newest dated item on its press page is from January 2024.

## Key claims

- **Two models.** The Caddie has a fixed height. The Retriever adjusts its upper deck from 25 to 38 inches and can fetch Labrador trays, each holding up to 10 pounds, from pallets placed on counters or shelves.
- **Shared specification.** 18 by 23 inches, up to 25 pounds on two decks, a day of normal use per charge, one level only, indoors only. Navigates spaces as narrow as 28 inches.
- **How it is commanded.** App, Alexa-enabled device, Bluetooth button or schedule, to pre-mapped "bus stops."
- **Safety sensors.** Depth, bumper, cliff and load sensors. "Our robots should always be used under the supervision of an adult," and a detailed safety guide was promised closer to production.
- **Privacy.** Autonomy runs on the robot. Once trained, it can be taken off Wi-Fi and commanded by local Bluetooth. A Labrador technician remotely trains the map, and video streaming in such modes is "controlled by you." More detail on data was promised before production.
- **Rollout.** Care-provider Retriever Pro comes first, with pilots planned in 2023. Consumer models depend on how fast the Pro ramps.
- **Early reservation terms (closed).** $1,499 upfront and $99 a month for the Caddie, $1,499 and $149 a month for the Retriever, for 36 months.

> [!note] What this page does not show
> Nothing here confirms that production began, or says how many robots exist. The site's privacy policy covers the website and does not describe robot data.
