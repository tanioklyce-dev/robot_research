## Summary
Overview article from the 2023 AI for Good Global Summit covering seven assistive technologies. Useful as a landscape survey of assistive robotics at that moment; includes the RELab Tenoexo (ETH Zurich), which has its own source page.

## Seven showcased technologies

1. **RELab Tenoexo** (ETH Zurich) — lightweight hand orthosis; see [RELab tenoexo](relab-ethz-tenoexo.md)
2. **ReHandyBot** — table-top robotic hand function recovery device post-neurological injury
3. **iReCHeCk** (EPFL CHILI Lab) — robot teaching handwriting to children
4. **Social Robot Haru** (Honda Research Institute) — expressive robot for emotional connection
5. **I.R.I.S. / QT Robot** — platform for older adults' social connection and purpose
6. **Buddy** (Blue Frog Robotics) — emotional companion; thousands deployed in French schools
7. **Furhat** (Furhat Robotics) — conversational humanoid with SDK

## SDG alignment
- SDG 3 (Health): accelerates recovery, reduces healthcare burden
- SDG 4 (Education): enhances interactive learning
- SDG 10 (Reduced Inequalities): promotes inclusive participation
