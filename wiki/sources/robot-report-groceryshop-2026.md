# The Robot Report on Groceryshop 2026

## Summary

Trade-show report showing where deployed retail robots actually are in 2026. The robots at scale scan shelves and do not touch products. Physical picking is the harder and much younger segment.

## Key claims

- **Simbe Tally.** Simbe says it has completed the rollout of **3,000 Tally robots** globally. The robot scans shelves, and Simbe is building analytics on that data.
- **Badger Technologies.** Its new CEO describes it as evolving from a robotics company into a "retail intelligence company," using robot-collected shelf data.
- **[Blue Collar Robotics](../entities/blue-collar-robotics.md).** A startup-pitch finalist "taking on a harder but potentially more transformative retail use case: physically picking products in the store."
