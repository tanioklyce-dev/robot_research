# New center to improve robot dexterity selected to receive up to $52 million

## Summary

Northwestern's announcement of the [HAND ERC](../entities/hand-erc.md) award, dated 21 August 2024. It gives the facts the center's own website leaves out: the award's start, how the "$52 million" is structured, and who directs the center. It is a university press piece, so the framing is promotional, but the funding terms and names are specific.

## Key claims

- **Funding is $26 million over five years, renewable.** The collaboration *"has received $26 million from the National Science Foundation"*; the grant funds the center *"across five years, with the ability to renew for another $26 million for an additional five years."* The headline "up to $52 million" is the sum of both terms.
- **Announced 21 August 2024.** It is the first Engineering Research Center that Northwestern has led.
- **Director: J. Edward Colgate**, a professor of mechanical engineering described as an expert in robots and haptics. **Research director: Kevin Lynch**, director of Northwestern's Center for Robotics and Biosystems.
- **Northwestern collaborators named:** Brenna Argall, Jian Cao, Matthew Elwin, Elizabeth Gerber, Todd Murphey, Ryan Truby and Lois Trautvetter.
- **Partners:** Carnegie Mellon, Florida A&M and Texas A&M as core partners, with faculty from Syracuse, Wisconsin–Madison and MIT.
- **The stated problem** (Colgate): AI has made manipulators potentially accessible to small manufacturers and people with motor impairments, but *"a huge challenge... is what we put at the end of the robot's arm. Today's two-jaw grippers are far too limited."*
- **Requirements beyond dexterity:** hands that are *"inexpensive, easy to operate without expertise, robust, durable and mass-manufacturable."*
- **Applications named:** manufacturing, caregiving, and handling precious or dangerous materials.

## Analysis

- **This corrects how the award should be quoted.** "$52 million over ten years" on the center's site is a ceiling. The committed amount is $26 million through about 2029, with the second half dependent on renewal. Pages in this wiki now state it that way.
- **The gripper claim is a direct disagreement with much of this wiki.** Most learned-policy results here, including the household ones, come from two-jaw grippers. The center's premise is that the end-effector is the limit. Neither side's evidence is in this article.
- **Evidence weight.** A press release. Reliable for dates, amounts and names; not evidence for any technical claim.
