# Blue Collar Robotics — company site

## Summary

Vendor pages, read on 2026-11-08, for [Blue Collar Robotics](../entities/blue-collar-robotics.md), a Utah startup building remotely operated robots that pick online grocery orders inside existing supermarkets. It deliberately does not make full autonomy a prerequisite for deploying. All customer and market figures here are the company's own.

## Key claims

- **Model.** "Human-in-the-Loop teleoperation," sold as Robotic Labor-as-a-Service, with e-grocery order fulfillment as the entry market.
- **Timeline.** Kickoff 10 August 2025; first robot ready to demonstrate by 10 November 2025.
- **Addressable assortment.** The company analyzed more than 40,000 supermarket SKUs and says about 65% suit its approach.
- **Next step (as of 10 August 2026).** "The first Gen 2 units are in production now for paid customer trials in the fourth quarter," with batch picking and improved mobility and remote operation. This supersedes the September pilot date reported by [Utah Business](utah-business-blue-collar-robotics.md).
- **Stated intent.** Robots "pick online grocery orders inside live supermarkets, alongside shoppers, during store hours."
- **Customers.** "Active engagements with several of the largest grocery retailers," unnamed.
