# Team 254 Website

## Summary

The official website of [Team 254 (The Cheesy Poofs)](../entities/team-254.md), a [FIRST Robotics Competition](../entities/first-robotics-competition.md) team based at Bellarmine College Preparatory in San Jose, California. Founded in 1998 at Broadway High School by NASA Ames Research Center. The site showcases their robot history, technical resources, and community programs.

## Key claims

- **Founded**: Fall 1998 at Broadway High School; relocated to Bellarmine College Preparatory in 2000.
- **Founded by**: NASA Ames Research Center.
- **Mission**: "We give students opportunities to experience real world engineering through competitive robotics."
- **2022 FRC World Champions**.
- **2026 season**: 65-6-0 record; ranked #2 in FIRST California district (364 points). 2026 robot named "Overload."
