# NVIDIA Blackwell-Powered Jetson Thor Now Available

Official NVIDIA press release marking the Jetson Thor availability date and confirming the developer-kit price point and named adopter list.

## Summary

On **August 25, 2025**, NVIDIA announced that **Jetson AGX Thor developer kits and Jetson T5000 production modules are now available**. The dev kit starts at **$3,499**. The release names 12 partner robotics, autonomy, and AI organizations adopting Thor and frames the chip as "the ultimate supercomputer" for physical AI.

## Key claims

- **Launch date**: 2025-08-25.
- **Developer kit price**: $3,499 (starting).
- **Production modules**: Jetson T5000 available from distribution partners on the same date.
- **Named adopters** (verbatim list from release): Agility Robotics, Amazon Robotics, [Boston Dynamics](../entities/boston-dynamics.md), Caterpillar, Figure, Hexagon, Medtronic, Meta, 1X, John Deere, OpenAI, Physical Intelligence.
- **Software stack** explicitly called out: NVIDIA Isaac (robotics simulation), [Isaac GR00T N1.5](../entities/nvidia-groot.md) (humanoid robot foundation models), NVIDIA Metropolis (vision AI), NVIDIA Holoscan (real-time sensor processing).
- **Jensen Huang quote**: "With unmatched performance and energy efficiency, and the ability to run multiple generative AI models at the edge, Jetson Thor is the ultimate supercomputer" for physical AI and robotics.

The release does not list JetPack 7 / Isaac ROS / Cosmos with version numbers — see [JetPack 7.0 for Jetson Thor white paper](nvidia-jetpack-7-thor-whitepaper.md) for the software-stack specifics.
