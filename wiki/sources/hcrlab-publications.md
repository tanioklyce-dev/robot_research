## Summary

Complete publication record (2016–2025) of the Human-Centered Robotics Lab (HCR Lab) at the University of Washington, directed by Maya Cakmak. The lab's two main research tracks are (1) **physically assistive robots** — feeding assistance, teleoperation for users with motor impairments, robot-assisted daily activities — and (2) **end-user robot programming (EUP)** — enabling non-programmers to customize and program robots for their specific needs. Both tracks are grounded in inclusive design: users with disabilities are research participants, not just subjects. Research platform of choice is [Hello Robot Stretch](../entities/stretch.md).

## Key claims

- **Sense of agency**: 2025 RO-MAN paper examines user preferences for robot autonomy vs. user control across household tasks — not a simple "more autonomy is better" story.
- **Autonomy preferences**: HRI 2020 study found that people with severe motor impairments do NOT always prefer more autonomous feeding robots. Autonomy–control tradeoff is user-specific and context-specific.
- **Henry Evans long-term deployments**: Stretch used by quadriplegic user Henry Evans across summers 2021–2023; tasks achieved include self-feeding, face wiping, scratching, lotion application, operating medical devices, playing card games, handing objects to family.
- **Feeding system deployed out-of-lab** (HRI 2025): Lessons Learned paper from real-world deployment of feeding robot — Best Systems Paper Award Finalist.
- **Handover work**: ICRA 2021 Best HRI Paper Award for reactive handovers. Recent work: affordance-aware handover poses accounting for human arm mobility constraints (RA-L).
- **EUP tools transferred to Hello Robot Stretch SE2**: programming tools have been deployed on commercial hardware.
- **Systematic review** (2024, Annual Review of Control, Robotics, and Autonomous Systems): "Physically assistive robots: A systematic review of mobile and manipulator robots that physically assist people with disabilities."
- Award-winning papers: Best HRI Paper ICRA 2021; Best Design Paper HRI 2023; Best Systems Paper Finalist HRI 2025; Best Short Contribution HRI 2024; multiple best paper finalists.
