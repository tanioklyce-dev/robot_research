## Summary

The **Ultimate Robot Knockout Legend (URKL / "UKRL")** — billed as the world's first humanoid-robot **free-combat league** — kicked off its 2026 season in **Shenzhen** in February 2026, organized by **[EngineAI](../entities/engineai.md)**. All entrants fight with identical **[EngineAI T800](../entities/engineai-t800.md)** humanoids provided free of charge; the winner takes a gold championship belt valued at **10 million yuan (~$1.44 million)**. The league's stated rationale is as much **engineering testbed as spectacle**: real-world combat stress-tests balance, motion control, dexterous-hand tendons, reducers, and lead screws, and (per organizers) shortens technology-iteration cycles by **30%+** while lowering R&D barriers for smaller teams that couldn't otherwise afford the hardware.

## Key claims

- **Name**: Ultimate Robot Knockout Legend (URKL), 2026 season; tiered competition schedule **running through December 2026**.
- **Launch**: **February 9–10, 2026**, Shenzhen (Guangdong). Venue for the finals shown as the **Nanshan Cultural and Sports Center** ([Shenzhen Story video](shenzhen-story-urkl-youtube.md)).
- **Organizer**: [EngineAI](../entities/engineai.md); **T800 robots provided free** to participating teams.
- **Prize**: gold championship belt worth **10M yuan (~$1.44M)**.
- **Scale**: **200+ teams from 10+ countries**; months of **online qualifiers → top 32 to the finals** ([video](shenzhen-story-urkl-youtube.md)).
- **T800 capability on display**: martial-arts moves incl. side kicks, aerial kicks, **360-degree aerial rotation**, capoeira-inspired spins, quick directional changes (full specs on the [T800 entity](../entities/engineai-t800.md)).
- **Framed as a real-world benchmark**: "Every punch, every fall, every recovery is a real-world test of humanoid robotics." Combat tests reducers, lead screws, and dexterous-hand tendons under load.
- **Quotes**: Pan Helin (Beijing analyst) — competitions raise public awareness and expand application scenarios; Tian Feng (former SenseTime dean) — "such real-world combat can shorten technology iteration cycles by over 30 percent," and free T800s lower R&D barriers for smaller companies.
- **Market context**: China's humanoid-robot market projected to reach **870 billion yuan by 2030**.
- **July 2026 expansion**: a **6-meter T800 mech suit** appeared at Futian COCO Park (2026-07-11), launching a URKL theme park.
