# Starship Technologies — grocery and scale press releases

## Summary

Three company releases, read on 2026-11-08 and referenced by URL: the [June 2026 grocery refocus](https://www.starship.xyz/press/starship-technologies-doubles-down-on-grocery/), the [April 2026 ten-million milestone](https://www.starship.xyz/press/autonomous-delivery-moves-into-the-mainstream-as-starship-technologies-passes-10-million-deliveries/) and the [December 2025 Finland milestone](https://www.starship.xyz/press/one-million-deliveries-finland/). They show sidewalk delivery as the grocery robotics with the largest real volume. **All figures are the company's own. The robots bring goods to the door and do no in-store picking.**

## Key claims

- **Scale.** More than 10 million deliveries, over 3,000 robots, 300+ locations and eight countries as of April 2026.
- **Grocery refocus.** It is winding down US university campuses and redeploying over 1,200 robots to grocery retailers in Europe and the US. It projects tenfold grocery growth in two years.
- **Finland.** One million S Group grocery deliveries in just under three years, and "over 800" robots in the country. More than 170 stores offer robot delivery.
- **Cost.** $3–4 less per grocery delivery than a courier, by the company's account. The long-term target is about $1 per drop.
- **Autonomy.** Described as Level 4 "without active human supervision" in the April release, while its website says "99% autonomous." Neither defines how that is measured.

> [!warning] Contradiction — what 20% in Finland measures
> S Group says robots account for "as much as 20 percent" of online orders in its own S-kaupat service. Six months later Starship said "roughly one in five grocery deliveries" in "Finland alone." The first is one retailer's online orders. The second reads like a national figure. Use the S Group version.

> [!note] Fleet numbers in the same period differ
> The December 2025 release says both "over 650" robots in the subheading and "over 800" in the body, for Finland. These may count different fleets.
