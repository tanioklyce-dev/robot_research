## Summary
Product page for myBuddy 280, Elephant Robotics' dual-arm collaborative robot with 13 DOF. Positioned for research, education, and dual-arm manipulation tasks at ~$1,619.

## Key specs
- DOF: 13 (6 per arm + 1 torso)
- Working radius: 280mm per arm
- Payload: 250g per arm
- Positioning accuracy: ±0.5mm
- Weight: 2.75 kg
- Joint speed: 160°/s max
- Compute: Raspberry Pi 4B (master) + ESP32 (auxiliary)
- Display: 7-inch touchscreen
- Cameras: dual 2MP HD
- Price: $1,619 (standard); $1,678 (with G-Shape base)

## Software
- Python; Windows/Linux/macOS
- ROS1 + RViz + MoveIt
- myBlockly, myStudio
- Serial + TCP/IP
- Open-source URDF

## End effectors
Suction pumps, grippers (multiple options)
