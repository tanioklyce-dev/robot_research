# Roborock Saros Z70 product page and TechRadar review

## Summary

The [Saros Z70](../entities/roborock-saros-z70.md) is a shipping robot vacuum with a folding 5-axis arm. It is the only arm-equipped home robot here at mass-market scale. Reviewers find it a capable vacuum, but its autonomous arm is limited and unreliable.

## Key claims

- **Price.** Roborock's US store showed **$999.99 against a $2,599.99 list** on 2026-11-08, during a promotion.
- **Arm (vendor).** A 5-axis retractable arm picks up items "up to 300g," such as slippers, socks, small towels and crumpled tissues. It can also be remote-controlled.
- **Review (TechRadar, two weeks of testing).** The arm is "thoughtfully designed, intuitive to control, and works well when controlled remotely," but "currently limited in what it can achieve autonomously," and "Automated arm function is buggy." The reviewer "couldn't get it to automatically tidy away anything at all," and notes potential "for users with mobility issues."

> [!note] Reviews date from 2025
> The reviews predate later firmware. [Gizmodo](https://gizmodo.com/roborock-saros-z70-review-this-robovacs-robotic-arm-is-a-swing-and-a-miss-2000607909) and [RTINGS](https://rtings.com/robot-vacuum/reviews/roborock/saros-z70) reach similar conclusions on the arm. No 2026 re-test was found.
