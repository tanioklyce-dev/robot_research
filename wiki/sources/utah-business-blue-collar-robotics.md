# Utah Business on Blue Collar Robotics

## Summary

Profile of [Blue Collar Robotics](../entities/blue-collar-robotics.md) with the only hardware and throughput figures found for its Robot 1.0 prototype. The figures come from the company and have not been independently measured.

## Key claims

- **Prototype.** A basket on wheels with a single arm, using suction cups that pull items weighing up to 10 pounds off a shelf.
- **Throughput.** About 120 items per hour, managed by a remote human.
- **Staffing.** Currently one person per robot. The goal is one person managing several.
- **Economics.** The pitch relies on lower overseas wages for remote operators, plus low-cost, off-the-shelf hardware.
- **Stage.** Still "in the garage phase" at publication, with a major chain under NDA interested in a September pilot. The company later gave [paid Q4 trials](blue-collar-robotics-site.md) as its next step.
