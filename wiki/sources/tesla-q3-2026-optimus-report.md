# Tesla starts building production-quality Optimus bots in Fremont

> [!note] Secondary source
> Tesla's Q3 2026 Update could not be fetched directly. This page rests on Tesla North's direct quotations from it.

## Summary

Tesla's Q3 2026 Update, as quoted here, says [Optimus](../entities/tesla-optimus.md) production has begun on high-volume lines at Fremont. It gives **no production rate, unit count, price or date for outside sales**, and does not say which version is being built.

## Key claims

- "Optimus production began in Fremont on our high-volume lines." The robots are "the first production-quality bots that we have manufactured."
- "We have proven that the current manufacturing process for Optimus is suitable for a commercial product."
- The capacity table lists California as "Early Ramp" and Texas as "Construction," with no capacity figure for either.
