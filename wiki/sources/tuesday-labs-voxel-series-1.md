# Tuesday Labs — Voxel Series-1

## Summary

Vendor pages, read on 2026-11-08, for [Voxel Series-1](../entities/tuesday-labs.md), a legged home robot taking reservations for delivery in 2028. The reservation agreement is unusually candid: what is shown is "a concept and development prototype," and the company expects to redesign the hardware substantially before delivery.

## Key claims

- **Advertised jobs.** Home Reset (tidying), Find, Fetch and Tend (watering plants).
- **Hardware named.** 7-DOF arms with fully actuated wrists, 1-DOF grippers, Z-shaped legs with impact-aware feet, stereo RGB plus wide-field vision. The robot page as captured gives no height, weight, payload or battery figure.
- **Reservation.** $200, fully refundable until you order, US delivery addresses only. It is "not a sales contract." The money is not held in escrow.
- **Price.** Estimated $20,000, "not binding," with a monthly option expected.
- **Delivery.** "Expected to begin in 2028 and may be later," possibly in select areas first.
- **Prototype.** Tuesday Labs expects the "appearance, design, materials, components, features, capabilities and specifications" may differ significantly.
- **Regulatory.** Voxel has not yet received FCC equipment authorization, and delivery depends on it.
- **Liability.** Limited to the $200 reservation payment. Disputes go to individual arbitration unless you opt out within 30 days.
