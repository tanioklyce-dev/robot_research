# Sunday Robotics — Memo site and technology page

## Summary

Vendor site for [Memo](../entities/sunday-robotics.md), a wheeled two-arm home robot trained from a wearable glove instead of in-home teleoperation. It is the only vendor in this wiki's home-robot tier that shows **clearing a table and loading a dishwasher**, labelled autonomous. **It is not for sale**: a beta is "launching late 2026," and no price is set.

## Key claims

- **Availability.** "Memo will not be available for purchase until after we complete our beta testing in 2026."
- **Cost.** A single hand-built Memo "costs approximately $20,000." The company does not expect that to be the retail price, and says scale will cut costs "by at least 50%," with pricing "announced closer to launch."
- **Listed skills.** Collecting utensils, plates, cups and "delicate wine glasses," dumping food scraps, loading and running a dishwasher; pulling an espresso shot; handling piles of socks. The FAQ adds: "We are still actively improving the reliability and generalization of these skills."
- **Data.** A Skill Capture Glove is worn by "Memory Developers" in their homes. Memo "does not need to learn through human tele-operation in your home."
- **Hardware.** Technology page: 1.7 m, 170 lb, arms 2×7, hands 2×4, 4 h run-time, 1 h charge. Rests at 4 ft and reaches from the floor to 7 ft.
- **Speed and safety.** Trained for "50% of a human's natural pace." Compliant control. Passive stability, so that with power cut it "will not suddenly drop its weight or fall down."
