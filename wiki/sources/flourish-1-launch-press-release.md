# Flourish Robots Launches Flourish 1 (press release)

## Summary

The company's own launch announcement. Flourish Robots *"emerged from stealth"* on **2026-09-29** in San Francisco, opened pre-orders for the first **50 units at $3,555** with deliveries from **December 2026**, and named its investor: **Families Fund** (founder Kartik Sathappan) **led the pre-seed round**. The release is the origin of the funding and founder-background claims that the trade press repeats.

## Key claims
- *"emerged from stealth"*, dateline **San Francisco, 2026-09-29**. Pre-orders open the same day.
- Teachable in 30 minutes *"by physically demonstrating the task with their phone"*. Tasks: shoes, clothes and toys; **loading the dishwasher**; wiping tables; watering plants; fetching; clearing a cat litter box.
- Founder and CEO **Antoine Marcel**: *"a repeat founder with a prior exit and previously received funding from a Xavier Niel-backed fund."*
- **Families Fund led the pre-seed.** No amount, no other investors, no team size.
- Sathappan: *"Flourish does the chores so parents can play with their kids."*

- The release, the [site](flourish-robots-website.md) and [Clubic](clubic-flourish-1.md) all say "50 units". The release adds no hardware detail.
