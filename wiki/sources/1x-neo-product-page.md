## Summary
Primary product page for [1X NEO](../entities/1x-neo.md) — the first primary-source data for this entity. Consumer household humanoid with a $200 deposit, Redwood AI (1X's proprietary VLM), and 22 hand joints per side.

## Key specs
- Height: 5'6" (~168 cm); Weight: 66 lbs (~30 kg)
- Lift: 154 lbs (~70 kg); Carry: 55 lbs (~25 kg)
- Battery: 842 Wh, 4-hour runtime
- Walking speed: 1.4 m/s; Running: up to 6.2 m/s
- DOF: 22 hand joints per side; 7 arm joints per side; spine + neck
- Noise: 22 dB (quieter than a refrigerator)
- Safety: HIC <250, no pinch points

## Capabilities
Autonomous household tasks (dishes, cleaning), voice interaction, mobile app, VR remote piloting, self-charging, Bluetooth speaker, personalized spatial memory.

## AI stack
- **Redwood AI** — 1X's proprietary vision-language model
- Built-in LLM
- **Expert Mode**: 1X staff remotely supervise to teach novel tasks
- Autonomy expands over time with updates

## Pricing
- Deposit: $200
- No mandatory subscription
- NEO Beta (2024) and NEO Gamma (2025) variants exist
