# PAL Robotics TIAGo Pro product page

## Summary

Vendor page for TIAGo Pro, the current generation of [TIAGo](../entities/tiago.md): a research mobile manipulator with two 7-DoF arms on ROS 2. No price is published on the page.

## Key claims

- 2×7 DoF arms, with a single-arm version also available.
- Payload of 3 kg per arm, excluding end-effector.
- ROS 2 software.
