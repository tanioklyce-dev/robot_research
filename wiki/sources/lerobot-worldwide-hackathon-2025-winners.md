## Summary

A Hugging Face Space hosting **all 30 ranked winning teams** from the **LeRobot Worldwide Hackathon**, held **June 14–15, 2025** as a hybrid online + local event organized by Hugging Face. The Space displays each team's 1-minute demo video pulled from `maringetxway/all-winners` (a HF dataset). The event drew ~400 submissions (last submission ID is 397) across 916 registered team members. Theme: **"The ChatGPT moment of robotics"** — accessible, open robotics built on the [LeRobot](../entities/lerobot.md) framework.

This is the cleanest single-source view of **the LeRobot ecosystem's community scale** as of mid-2025.

## Key claims

### Event
- **Dates**: June 14–15, 2025 (48-hour hybrid format).
- **Format**: online + local hackathons worldwide.
- **Organizing org**: [Hugging Face](../entities/hugging-face.md) — Marine Caous (`maringetxway`), Thomas Wolf (`thomwolf`), [Remi Cadene](../entities/remi-cadene.md) (`cadene`).
- **Community size at registration**: 916 team members, 1,150+ followers of the org.
- **Sponsors visible in the Space**: Hugging Face, [Seeed Studio](../entities/seeed-studio.md), and additional sponsors (sponsors.png aggregate).
- **Org assets created during the event** (per [LeRobot-worldwide-hackathon HF page](https://huggingface.co/LeRobot-worldwide-hackathon)): **189 datasets**, **12 models**, **11 Spaces**. The `submissions` dataset alone has 11.3k downloads.

### Prize structure
| Rank | Hardware prize per team |
|---|---|
| 🥇 1st–3rd | 1 [Hope Jr Arm](../entities/hope-jr-arm.md) + 1 [LeKiwi](../entities/lekiwi.md) |
| 🥈 4th–5th | 1 [Hope Jr Arm](../entities/hope-jr-arm.md) |
| 🥉 6th–24th | 1 [LeKiwi](../entities/lekiwi.md) |
| 🥉 25th–30th | 1 [SO-101](../entities/so-arm101.md) |

A useful market signal: **LeKiwi is the prize hardware in 22 of 30 ranked positions** (1st–3rd + 6th–24th), and SO-101 anchors the bottom tier. The Hope Jr Arm — a higher-tier arm awarded only to the top 5 — appears to be the premium prize.

### Notable patterns
- **Geographic spread implied by team names**: "great-akihabara" (Tokyo), "West Indian Style" (Caribbean), "ML6" (Belgian AI consultancy), "LAPESP" (likely Brazil — University of São Paulo lab acronym pattern), "TeamSuzetteArtilect" (Artilect is a Toulouse, France FabLab), "IlliniRoboticsCrashDummies" (Illinois / UIUC).
- **UIUC presence**: Team #24 `IlliniRoboticsCrashDummies` is the visible UIUC entry; appears unrelated to the [SIGRobotics-UIUC](../entities/sigrobotics-uiuc.md) group that designed [LeKiwi](../entities/lekiwi.md).
- **Submission requirements (per HF page)**: each team submits LeRobot-format datasets + a <1-minute demo video. Naming convention: `[TEAM-N°]-[TEAM NAME]-[DATASET-NAME]` for datasets.
