# UniX AI introduces Panther (press release and product page)

## Summary

Vendor-issued release for [Panther](../entities/unix-ai-panther.md), a full-size wheeled dual-arm robot from Suzhou-based UniX AI. It claims global deliveries have begun and calls Panther the first service humanoid "to enter real household deployment." That claim rests on one vendor video. No price is given and no independent home deployment was found.

## Key claims

- **Hardware.** 34 joints; 8-DoF arms; adaptive grippers; four-wheel-steer, four-wheel-drive base; **80 cm upper-body lift** that reaches ground level; 48 V platform. Product page, for the Panther Pro Max Mini: dual-arm load capacity 12 kg, battery 4–8 h.
- **Deliveries.** UniX AI "has also commenced global deliveries of Panther." It says it has delivered "over 100 units per month" since 2025, across its products.
- **Target uses.** Hotels, retail and guided tours; household tasks, elderly care and companionship; security, research and education.
- **Demonstration.** A "fully autonomous tea-making process" live at a Shenzhen investor event.
