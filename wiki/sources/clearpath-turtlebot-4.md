## Summary
Product page for TurtleBot 4, "the next-generation of the world's most popular open source robotics platform for education and research." Made by [Clearpath Robotics](https://clearpathrobotics.com) (now a Rockwell Automation company) on top of the [iRobot Create 3](../entities/irobot-create-3.md) base. First primary-source data for the [TurtleBot](../entities/turtlebot.md) entity.

## Key specs

| | Standard | Lite |
|---|---|---|
| Dimensions | 341 × 339 × 351 mm | 341 × 339 × 192 mm |
| Weight | 3.9 kg | 3.3 kg |
| Camera | OAK-D-PRO | OAK-D-LITE |
| Display | OLED screen | None |

**Shared specs:**
- Max speed: 0.31 m/s
- Payload: 9 kg (default); 15 kg (custom)
- Runtime: 2.5–4.0 hours
- Computer: Raspberry Pi 4B (4 GB)
- Sensors: 2D LiDAR (RPLIDAR-A1), IMU, wheel encoders, infrared, cliff/bump/slip detection
- Software: ROS 2 pre-installed + Ignition Gazebo simulation model + micro-ROS support
- Base: [iRobot Create 3](../entities/irobot-create-3.md)
