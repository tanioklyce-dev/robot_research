# IEEE Spectrum — Labrador Systems' Retriever robot

## Summary

Independent launch coverage, read on 2026-11-08 and referenced by URL. Its useful argument is about design: rather than solving general grasping, the Retriever changes the environment slightly with trays and pallets, so that fetching becomes a tractable job.

## Key claims

- **Environment instead of manipulation.** The robot "can only fetch what's on the tray," in exchange for avoiding a problem Spectrum called years from being solved in the home.
- **Autonomy.** 3D visual SLAM, depth sensors and bumpers. A Labrador representative remotely drives the robot to build the map.
- **Privacy.** CEO Mike Dooley said that apart from setup and troubleshooting the robots are intended to run without a human in the loop, and that a switch for remote connectivity was planned.
- **Cost framing.** Spectrum compares the price with human care rather than with other gadgets, and says subsidies by health systems or insurers had not yet happened.
