# Walden Robotics — Launch from Stealth

## Summary

**Walden Robotics** launched out of stealth on **2026-07-15**, revealing itself as the physical-AI startup the wiki had been tracking as [Russ Tedrake](../entities/russ-tedrake.md)'s "still-stealth" venture ([Automated Podcast, 2026-07](automated-podcast-tedrake-rocket-ship.md)). The company **spun out of [Toyota Research Institute](../entities/tri.md) in January 2026** and builds general-purpose robots for **manufacturing and logistics** that "continuously learn and improve while performing real work." It raised **$300M in seed funding at a $1.1B valuation** and is headquartered in **Cambridge, Massachusetts**. Its technical foundation is the exact TRI lineage this wiki already documents: **[Large Behavior Models](../concepts/learning/large-behavior-models.md)** and **[Diffusion Policy](../entities/diffusion-policy.md)**. The founding team is a near-wholesale transplant of TRI's robot-learning leadership. Positioning quote: *"Robots ready to work. With you. Today."*

## Key claims

**Founding & structure**
- Spun out of [TRI](../entities/tri.md) in **January 2026**; launched publicly **2026-07-15**.
- HQ: **Cambridge, MA** (co-located with the MIT/TRI-Cambridge robotics cluster).
- Full-stack thesis: builds "hardware, software, frontier-class AI, and applications."

**Funding**
- **$300M seed** at a **$1.1B valuation**.
- **Co-leads**: Toyota Motor Corp, Toyota Invention Partners, Toyota Ventures, and **Deviation Capital**.
- **Participating investors**: NVIDIA, Boeing, AE Ventures, Samsung Ventures, Prologis Ventures, CoreWeave Ventures, Calibrate Ventures, Colle Capital, Shine Capital, NextView Ventures, Squarepoint Capital, One Madison Group, KAS Venture Partners, Menlo Ventures.

**Technology**
- Robots leverage **[Large Behavior Models (LBMs)](../concepts/learning/large-behavior-models.md)** and **[Diffusion Policy](../entities/diffusion-policy.md)** — "frontier model classes that let them quickly learn new tasks and continuously improve through real-world practice."
- Builds on "a decade of foundational AI and robotics research" (i.e. the TRI Diffusion-Policy → LBM program).
- Deployment model: **"powerful autonomy combined with a human available for remote assistance"** — the hybrid pattern that lets them field capability immediately while the policy improves.

**Deployment / traction**
- Robots have been doing **"useful work in production at a Toyota plant in North America since February 2026"** (North Carolina facility) — "from first pilot to real work in under two months."
- Current task capabilities advertised: **machine tending, tool setting, parts kitting, assembly.**
- Target verticals: **automotive, aerospace, semiconductors, electronics, logistics, life sciences.**

**Philosophy / naming**
- Name references **Thoreau's *Walden*** and deliberate living. Essay line: *"We must think now about how robots will help us find more purpose in our work and in our lives."*
- Robots-**augment**-not-replace framing: *"a future where robots are working alongside people"* / *"side-by-side with people from day one."* This is the same **"amplify, not replace"** thesis the wiki already tied to Tedrake and TRI.
- Tedrake quote: *"Core advances in Physical AI have made disruptive change possible. But providing real value to customers and building a robust business requires deep understanding of how manufacturing is done today."*

**Founding team** (all co-founders; see [Walden Robotics](../entities/walden-robotics.md) for the full roster)
- **[Russ Tedrake](../entities/russ-tedrake.md)** — CEO. MIT Toyota Professor; former SVP of Large Behavior Models at TRI; led Team MIT in the DARPA Robotics Challenge; author of Drake + the Underactuated Robotics course.
- **[Ben Burchfiel](../entities/ben-burchfiel.md)** — CTO. Ex-Aurora Innovation production ML; at TRI co-led a 60+ person robot-learning team; **Diffusion Policy / OpenVLA / LBM 1.0** contributor.
- **[Siyuan Feng](../entities/siyuan-feng.md)** — Principal Architect. Full-stack roboticist; DARPA Robotics Challenge bipedal locomotion; **Diffusion Policy** and co-led TRI's LBM effort.
- **Adrien Gaidon** — CSO. Adjunct Prof. at Stanford, former Calibrate Ventures Partner, former ML division exec director at TRI; 100+ patents, 16,000+ citations.
- **Rares Ambrus** — Head of AI. Former head of computer vision / ML at TRI; 70+ papers, 50+ patents.
- **Kerri Fetzer-Borelli** — COO. 10+ years building operating systems for R&D orgs; scaled automated-driving and robotics operations.
- **David Johnson** — CPO. Founder (and seller) of **Dexai Robotics** (kitchen automation); ex-Director of Sony's Robotic Gastronomy; PhD Physics (Stanford).

## Open questions

- **Robot hardware** — the site shows no specific robot form factor or arm/base vendor. Is Walden building its own hardware, or integrating existing platforms (Franka-class arms, as the TRI LBM work used)? "Builds hardware" is claimed but unspecified.
- **Naming tension** — the [Tedrake podcast](automated-podcast-tedrake-rocket-ship.md) strongly hinted the startup name *"references LBMs"* ("it's in the company name," unchallenged) and a Robotics Summit keynote was titled *"Building Great Behavior Models for Industry."* The actual name, **"Walden,"** references **Thoreau**, not LBMs. Either the earlier hint was misdirection/playful, or "Behavior Models" lived in the keynote/tagline rather than the corporate name. See [contradiction note on the company page](../entities/walden-robotics.md).
- **Headcount** — not disclosed.
- **Relationship to TRI going forward** — Tedrake's TRI SVP-of-LBMs role vs. Walden CEO role: clean departure or dual affiliation? The spin-out framing + Toyota's investor co-lead suggests TRI remains a close partner, not a severed tie.
