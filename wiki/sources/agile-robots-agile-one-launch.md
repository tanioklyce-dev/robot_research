## Summary

Munich-based **[Agile Robots SE](../entities/agile-robots.md)** — a DLR (German Aerospace Center) spinout that had built its business on force-sensitive robotic arms and AMRs — announced its first humanoid robot, **[Agile ONE](../entities/agile-one.md)**, for industrial use. The strategic thesis distinguishing it from most 2026 humanoid entrants: Agile ONE is positioned **not as a standalone product but as one component of a fully integrated automation stack** (humanoid + AMRs + robotic arms + AI vision, all managed by the [AgileCore](../entities/agile-robots.md) software platform). Series production begins **early 2026 at a new Bavarian facility** — a deliberate "made in Germany, not China" manufacturing stance.

## Key claims

- **Agile ONE specs** (triangulated with [heise online](https://www.heise.de/en/news/Munich-robotics-company-Agile-Robots-showcases-its-first-humanoid-robot-11087280.html) and DirectIndustry coverage): **174 cm tall, 69 kg, 20 kg payload, 2 m/s (7.2 km/h) walking speed, hands with 21 degrees of freedom** approaching human-hand dexterity.
- **"World-leading dexterous hands"** with fingertip sensors and **force-torque sensors in every joint** — leveraging the company's arm-side force-sensing heritage.
- AI trained on **"one of Europe's largest real-world industrial datasets."** Layered AI architecture with specialized cognition levels.
- **Human-centric design**: responsive eyes and proximity sensors for safe co-working.
- **Manufacturing**: in-house production from **early 2026** at a new **Bavarian (Germany)** facility, with full control over the hardware process. Series production timeline confirmed by [industrial-production.de](https://www.industrial-production.de/).
- **Strategic partnerships**: **Google DeepMind** (AI) and **Deutsche Telekom's Industrial AI Cloud** (Europe's first industrial-AI cloud infrastructure).
- **Company scale**: revenue doubled YoY to **€200 million**; ~2,000+ employees across 15 global sites; founded 2018 by DLR experts.
- **Product portfolio it slots into**: [Agile Hand](../entities/agile-robots.md), FR3 force-sensitive arm, Diana 7 arm, Thor Series, AMRs/AGVs, all under **AgileCore**.
- **Target applications**: material gathering/transport, pick-and-place, machine tending, tool use, fine manipulation.
