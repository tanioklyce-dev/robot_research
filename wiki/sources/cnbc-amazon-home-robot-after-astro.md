# CNBC — Amazon's Astro and the next home robot

## Summary

The most recent reporting found on [Astro](../entities/amazon-astro.md), read on 2026-11-08 and referenced by URL. It says Astro never left its invite-only stage, reached only a few thousand customers, and that Amazon has moved much of the team to a new home-robot effort that includes [Fauna Robotics](../entities/fauna-robotics.md). The new product's form and tasks are unknown; CNBC inferred the effort from job posts and LinkedIn profiles.

## Key claims

- **Astro's reach.** "Shipped to just a few thousand customers" and "never evolved beyond an invite-only product."
- **One owner's use.** An owner since 2023 still uses it to check pets and appliances remotely, but calls it a party trick and a "trip hazard." A single owner is an anecdote.
- **An arm was discussed.** Former consumer-robotics VP Ken Washington says executives discussed adding one, and that he pushed for a biped. He recalls Jeff Bezos being disappointed with the functionality but impressed by mapping and navigation.
- **New effort.** Job posts describe a "new-to-world robot product" for "dynamic home environments." Amazon redeployed much of the Astro team, and acquired Fauna in March 2026 for a new Personal Robotics Group.
- **Amazon's caution.** A spokesperson said there remains a substantial gap between the lab and settings such as a living room.
- **Support unclear.** Some owners believe Astro's days are numbered. The article quotes no Amazon statement about ending home Astro.
