# Weave Robotics — Isaac 1 product and pre-order pages

## Summary

Vendor pages for [Isaac 1](../entities/weave-robotics.md), a wheeled two-arm home robot aimed at laundry and tidying. It costs **$7,999 or $449 a month with a $250 refundable deposit**, and the first shipments are planned for fall 2026, California first. The pages disclose teleoperation assistance. Dishes and cooking are not among the listed features.

## Key claims

- **Price.** $449 a month or $7,999 up front, plus an "optional $99/mo premium membership." The deposit is $250, refundable until the unit ships.
- **Availability.** "First shipments begin in fall of 2026. Deliveries to California come first, with broader U.S. availability following through 2027."
- **Features.** Laundry Flow finds and picks up dirty clothes, handles loaded hampers, and folds and puts clothes away. Daily Reset makes beds and returns toys, shoes and clutter.
- **Autonomy.** "Autonomous for Laundry Flow and Daily Reset by default, with teleoperation assistance when needed to guarantee we complete tasks."
- **Hardware.** Footprint 20.5" × 22"; height 3' to 5'9"; vertical reach 80"; horizontal reach 38". DOF: neck 2, arms 2×6, hands 2×1, torso 2, base 3. Battery 8 h. Wi-Fi.
- **Isaac 0.** The [company site](https://www.weaverobotics.com/isaac-0) describes a stationary laundry-folding "early-release prototype" shipping in limited quantities across California, with "2,000+ hours run in the field."
