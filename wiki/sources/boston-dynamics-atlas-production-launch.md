# Boston Dynamics unveils the product version of Atlas (CES 2026) and Atlas product page

## Summary

Boston Dynamics announced a production [Atlas](../entities/atlas.md) for industrial work on 2026-01-05. Production started immediately, **2026 output is fully committed to Hyundai's RMAC and Google DeepMind**, and more customers are planned for early 2027. No price is given.

## Key claims

- **Specs.** 1.9 m, 90 kg, 56 DoF, 2.3 m reach, 50 kg instant and 30 kg sustained capacity, 4 h battery with self-swapping, IP67, -20 to 40 °C.
- **Control.** Autonomous, teleoperated, or tablet steering.
- **Safety.** Human detection and fenceless guarding.
- **Availability.** "All Atlas deployments are already fully committed for 2026." Additional customers "in early 2027."
