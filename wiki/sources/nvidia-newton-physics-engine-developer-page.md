## Summary
NVIDIA's developer landing page for the Newton physics engine, an open-source GPU-accelerated physics simulator co-developed by NVIDIA, Google DeepMind, and Disney Research, and managed by the Linux Foundation.

## Key claims
- Newton is built on NVIDIA Warp and OpenUSD.
- Optimized for robotics: targets dexterous manipulation and locomotion.
- Designed as a pluggable backend for both [NVIDIA Isaac Lab](../entities/nvidia-isaac-lab.md) and [MuJoCo Playground](../entities/mujoco-playground.md).
- Linux Foundation governance gives vendor-neutral oversight despite heavy NVIDIA contribution.
