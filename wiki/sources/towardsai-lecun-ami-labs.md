## Summary
Secondary journalism (blog post) reporting that Yann LeCun departed Meta ~5 months before April 2026 (i.e., ~Nov/Dec 2025) and founded **AMI Labs** with a $1.03B seed round. Claims three world-model papers shipped within 60 days. **Treat as provisional — not corroborated by a primary source in this wiki.**

- LeCun described as founder of **[AMI Labs](../entities/ami-labs.md)**, no longer VP/Chief AI Scientist at [Meta](../entities/meta-fair.md).

- Three models shipped (within 60 days):
  1. [V-JEPA 2.1](v-jepa-2-1-paper.md)
