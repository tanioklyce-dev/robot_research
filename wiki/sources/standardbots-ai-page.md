## Summary

Product/marketing page for **[Standard Bots](../entities/standard-bots.md)'** AI offering — positioned as *"the first self-serve AI platform for industrial robots — train by demonstration and automate the impossible."* A **commercial learn-from-demonstration** play for factory automation: teach a skill by teleoperation, capture it with onboard vision, train a model in the cloud, deploy autonomous self-correcting execution — *"~1 task demo, 0 code."* Notable in this wiki as the **industrial / commercial** end of the imitation-learning spectrum, contrasting with the research-VLA line ([π0](../entities/pi-zero.md), [GR00T](../entities/nvidia-groot.md)).

## Key claims
- **Products**: Spark (7 kg / 900 mm / $29,500), **Core (RO1)** (18 kg / 1,300 mm / $37,000), Thor (30 kg / 2,000 mm / $49,500), Bolt (mobile droid, 14 kg / 900 mm, beta).
- **AI = learn-from-demonstration**: teach via teleoperator (gamepad / anti-gravity assist / jogging); onboard vision captures actions; **cloud-based model training**; deployed models run autonomously with self-correction. Claim: ~1 demo, no code.
- **Target**: industrial manufacturing — machine tending, palletizing, welding, pick & place, assembly, inspection, material handling.
- **No disclosed model name, architecture, benchmark, or NVIDIA partnership.** Customers listed: NASA, Amazon, Lockheed Martin, Verizon, Timken, Sonoco. CEO Evan Beard; $63M raised; Glen Cove, NY.

- Contrast with the research-VLA line — no foundation-model / cross-embodiment framing; single-vendor arm + per-customer skills.
