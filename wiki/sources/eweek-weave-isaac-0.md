# Weave's $8K Laundry Robot Still Needs Human Help (eWeek)

## Summary

Trade coverage of [Isaac 0](../entities/weave-robotics.md), the stationary laundry folder, read against Weave's current product page. It folds common garments in **30 to 90 minutes**, with remote people correcting mistakes. It is the only product found in this review that sells a manipulation chore to households today.

## Key claims

- **Performance (eWeek).** Folds common garments "in 30 to 90 minutes." Plugs into a standard outlet.
- **Remote help.** A Weave specialist can step in "for a 5- to 10-second correction." Weave's page says T-shirts, shorts and long-sleeves are "often" folded "almost entirely autonomously," while others need more help.
- **Camera access.** eWeek, citing The Register, says teleoperators can see the robot's head and wrist camera feeds.
- **Availability (Weave, 2026-11-08).** Shipping to California residents in limited quantities.

> [!warning] Contradiction — price and bedding
> eWeek reported a **$7,999** list price or about $450 a month in February. Weave's current page gives **$249 a month, or $3,999 up front plus $49 a month**. The current page is used here. eWeek, citing TechBuzz, says it does not fold large blankets or bed sheets, while Weave's page lists "bed sheets" among real loads.
