# LG CLOiD home robot at CES 2026 (press release)

## Summary

LG's announcement of [CLOiD](../entities/lg-cloid.md), a wheeled two-arm home robot shown as a demonstration of its "Zero Labor Home" vision. The release gives no price, date or market. The only forward statement is that LG "plans to continue developing home robots."

## Key claims

- **Hardware.** Head unit, torso with two arms, wheeled base with autonomous navigation. Each arm has 7 DOF and each hand five independently actuated fingers. The torso tilts so it can pick up objects "from knee level and above."
- **Demonstrated scenarios.** Taking milk from a refrigerator, placing a croissant in an oven, starting laundry cycles, and folding and stacking garments.
- **Integration.** Coordinates with LG's ThinQ appliances.
