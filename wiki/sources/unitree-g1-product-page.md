# Unitree G1 and G1-D product pages and store listing

## Summary

Unitree's own G1 specification table and store listing, retrieved 2026-11-08. They settle what the wiki's two G1 prices refer to. **The $13.5K G1 has no secondary-development access**; the G1 EDU that research requires is **"Contact sales."** The store also lists a **G1 Pro at $21,500**. The separate [G1-D page](https://www.unitree.com/G1-D/) describes a wheeled data-collection platform with 7-DOF arms and no published price.

## Key claims

- **G1 vs G1 EDU (spec table).** Price: "US $13.5K" vs "Contact sales." Secondary Development: "/" vs "YES." Joints: 23 vs 23–43. Arm maximum load: about 2 kg vs about 3 kg. Single-arm DOF: 5 on both. Hands: none listed vs optional force-controlled three-finger hand. Warranty: 8 vs 18 months.
- **Store.** Variants "G1" at $13,500 and "G1 Pro" at $21,500, both listed as available. The listing does not say what Pro adds.
- **G1-D.** Robot DOF excluding end-effector 19: arms 7×2, waist 2, column 1, base 2. Marketed with data-acquisition and training tooling that supports "PI and GROOT."

> [!note] Third-party EDU prices are not primary
> Reseller guides quote EDU from about $43,900 and G1-D from about $38,800. Those numbers come from dealers, not Unitree. Get a quote before relying on them.
