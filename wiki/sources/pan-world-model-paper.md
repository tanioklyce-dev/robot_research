# PAN: A World Model for General, Interactable, and Long-Horizon World Simulation

The results paper the [Critique of World Model](critique-of-world-model-paper.md) deferred to. **What it delivers is narrower than what the Critique promised, and the paper says so**: a video-conditioned, language-actioned world model with a frozen VLM as encoder and latent backbone and a 14 B video-diffusion decoder trained by flow matching — *"a more streamlined and unified autoregressive language backbone for clarity and scalability"* in place of the Critique's mixed discrete/continuous backbone with a learned switch. It is evaluated on a benchmark of the authors' own design against video generators and one JEPA.

## Summary

PAN instantiates [GLP](../concepts/world-models/generative-latent-prediction.md) as `p(o_{t+1}|o_t,a_t) = Σ p_h(ŝ_t|o_t) p_f(ŝ_{t+1}|ŝ_t,a_t) p_g(o_{t+1}|ŝ_{t+1})`: the **Qwen2.5-VL-7B-Instruct** vision tower is `h`, its language model is `f` (run as a multi-turn chat in which each assistant turn emits **256 learnable query tokens** that become the next latent state), and **Wan2.1-T2V-14B** adapted with **Causal Swin-DPM** is `g`. Training is generative only: the flow-matching discrepancy between the decoded next chunk and the real one; the VLM is frozen throughout and only the query embeddings and decoder are trained in the joint stage. Actions are **natural-language captions** of what changes in a clip, generated by a VLM over filtered public video. On the authors' three-part benchmark PAN reports the best open-source scores on action fidelity, long-horizon stability and step-wise simulation, and lifts an o3 planning agent's success by 23–27 points where video-generator baselines sometimes lower it.

## Key claims

### Architecture (§2–3)

- **Encoder** — Qwen2.5-VL-7B vision tower: ViT, 14×14 patches, windowed attention, 2-D RoPE; 3-D patching over consecutive frames so short-term motion is in the tokens.
- **Backbone** — Qwen2.5-VL-7B language model in a conversational template (`<user> <video state> <action>` / `<assistant> <256 query tokens>` …), predicting the next latent from the full history of states and actions. Teacher-forced in training; closed-loop at inference. The 256-token output is described as *"a form of associative memory."* **The latent is continuous**; the Critique's discrete-token backbone, learned switch, and diffusion embedding predictor are all named as *"a promising direction for future work"* (§3.2).
- **Decoder** — Wan2.1-T2V-14B DiT (rectified-flow / flow-matching objective, 1000 steps, shifted schedule) with (a) a **new zero-initialised cross-attention stream** for the latent world state, summed with the umT5 text stream carrying the action; (b) **Causal Swin-DPM**: a sliding window holding two 10-latent-frame chunks at noise levels K/2 and K, the earlier one dequeued when fully denoised and a fresh noisy chunk enqueued, with a **chunk-wise causal attention mask** so a chunk cannot see the next action; (c) Wan-VAE 3-D causal compression (1+T frames → 1+T/4; window 21 latent = 81 real frames), random 0–122-frame padding at training to mimic the sliding context; (d) a conditioning frame carried from the last chunk with **noise augmentation at k = 0.055** because a clean one accumulates error.
- **The paper's answer to "pixels are unpredictable"** (§3.3.3): the decoder conditions on *fuzzy, partially-noised* history, so it *"is not 'unfairly' penalized for details that are inherently unknowable"* — stochastic detail is delegated to the diffusion process, structured dynamics to the backbone.

### Training and inference (§4–5)

- **Stage 1** — adapt Wan2.1 to Causal Swin-DPM: 5 epochs on **960 H200s**, HSDP + activation checkpointing, FlashAttention-3 / FlexAttention, AdamW 1e-5, grad-clip 0.05. **Stage 2** — joint training under the GLP loss with the **VLM frozen**; only query embeddings + decoder learn; history capped at 10 rounds; early-stopped after **1 of 5 planned epochs**.
- **Inference** — the predicted state is augmented with the encoding of its *own* decoded observation, `ŝ′_k = [ŝ_k, h(ô_k)]`, and the decoder also sees `ô_t`; CFG scale 4; SageAttention2++ (8-bit/4-bit) gives 30.3 % speed-up; sequence parallel over 8 GPUs per sample.

### Data (§6)

Public long-form video → shot segmentation → rule filters (static / erratic motion, pure pan-zoom, fade frames) → pretrained aesthetic and scene-text detectors → a custom VLM filter (lectures, screen recordings, heavy edits, residual cuts) → **dense re-captioning by a VLM prompted for temporal dynamics, not scene description**. Those captions are the "actions." **No dataset size, hour count, or source list is given.**

### Benchmark and results (§7) — the authors' own protocol

| Dimension | Protocol | PAN | Baselines |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Action Simulation Fidelity** | GPT-4o proposes action sequences; VLM judge (after WorldModelBench) scores faithfulness. Agent-simulation and environment-simulation splits | **70.3 % / 47.0 % → 58.6 %** overall | *"surpasses all open-source baselines and most commercial"* — numbers only in Fig. 5 (a dashboard screenshot) |
| **Long-horizon Forecast** | Transition Smoothness = inverse-exp of optical-flow acceleration at step boundaries; Simulation Consistency = WorldScore consistency metrics with late-step penalties | **53.6 % / 64.1 %** | *"substantially surpassing all baselines, including KLING and MiniMax"* |
| **Step-wise Simulation** | WM-ABench-style 4-way choice on AgiBot manipulation; all models fine-tuned on AgiBot; generators judged by humans, V-JEPA 2 by latent similarity | **56.1 %** | highest among open-source |
| **Open-ended planning** | 15 AgiBot scenarios; o3 proposes, WM simulates, o3 picks; blinded human assessment | **+26.7 pts** over o3 alone | baselines *"improve in some scenarios but degrade in others"* |
| **Structured planning** | 46 Language Table rearrangements, same loop | **+23.4 pts** | same |

Baselines: Wan 2.1 / 2.2 I2V-14B, Cosmos 1 / Cosmos 2 (14 B), **[V-JEPA 2](../entities/v-jepa-2.md)** (extended with the umT5 encoder so it can take language actions, then fine-tuned), KLING, MiniMax Hailuo, Runway Gen-3.

### Qualitative (§8; image-only pages)

Interactive long rollouts under real-time interventions; the same action across drastically different worlds; **rare-case simulation** (*"tail events and counterfactuals on demand … safety-focused augmentation"*) — the crash-data claim Xing makes in the [debate](lecun-xing-jepa-glp-debate-2026.md); multi-step planning trees.

## The wiki's read

> [!warning] What this paper does and does not test
> The Critique's argument for GLP was **statefulness via discrete tokens**, a **decoder as diagnostic** for a lossy encoder, and **RL on simulated experience**. PAN implements none of the three: the state is 256 continuous VLM tokens, the encoder is *frozen* so the decoder cannot pressure it, and usage is per-step planning with an external VLM. What PAN tests is a narrower and still interesting claim — that a frozen VLM's latent, rolled forward autoregressively and grounded by a video decoder, makes a better *action-conditioned simulator* than video generators trained end-to-end. On its own benchmark, it does.

- **This is a generative-video world model with a VLM latent, not a third paradigm.** Architecturally its nearest kin are its own baselines — [Cosmos](../entities/nvidia-cosmos.md) and Genie-class models — with the change that the state passed between steps is a language-model embedding rather than frames. On the wiki's [paradigm table](../syntheses/world-models/generative-video-vs-jepa-world-models.md) it sits firmly on the generative side, with LeCun's live objection ([debate](lecun-xing-jepa-glp-debate-2026.md), 76:52) applying in full: it renders pixels to learn.
- **The V-JEPA 2 comparison is not the test the Critique promised.** V-JEPA 2 is scored by *latent similarity* while generators are scored by *human judgement of decoded frames*; the two columns are not the same measurement. The one number that would decide the Critique's Theorem 2 in practice — a decision-relevant error a latent world model misses and a generative one catches — is not reported.
- **The planning result is the strongest thing here, and it is the one most worth replicating.** +23 to +27 points for an o3 agent, with baselines sometimes *hurting* the agent, is the same finding the wiki records for VLA supervision ([supervising a VLA hurts in-distribution, helps on novel tasks](../concepts/agents/llm-agent-architecture.md)): a simulator only helps a planner if its errors are smaller than the planner's. But 15 + 46 cases, blinded human scoring of unspecified size, and no baseline numbers in the text.
- **Compute and data are asymmetric with everything on the JEPA side.** 960 H200s for the decoder alone, against [LeWorldModel](../entities/leworldmodel.md) on one GPU. The training set is described by its filters and never by its size. Fig. 5 is a screenshot of a local web dashboard (the page header carries a `127.0.0.1` URL), so baseline scores are unrecoverable from the PDF text.
- **Causal Swin-DPM is the reusable engineering.** Two-chunk sliding window at staggered noise levels, causal mask over chunks, noised conditioning frame — a concrete recipe for the compounding-error problem the wiki's [WorldTrace](../entities/worldtrace.md) and DreamDojo pages approach from other directions. It is also, read carefully, the paper's real reply to LeCun: don't predict from sharp history.
- **Early stopping after one epoch** of a five-epoch joint schedule, with the VLM frozen, means the "unification of imagination and reality" is mostly a decoder learning to read a fixed latent. Whether joint training of the encoder would help or collapse is the experiment the GLP thesis actually needs, and it is not run.

## Open questions

- Training-set size and composition. Not stated anywhere in 33 pages.
- Baseline numbers for Fig. 5, and the size of the human-evaluation pool.
- Joint training with an *unfrozen* encoder: the decoder-as-diagnostic argument requires it and the paper does not try it.
- A like-for-like V-JEPA 2 comparison on the planning loop, both scored by task success.
- Xing's *"10× faster than playout"* generation claim in the debate has no counterpart in this paper (30.3 % from SageAttention2++ is the only inference figure).
