## Summary
Project page for the tenoexo, RELab ETH Zurich's wearable robotic hand orthosis for sensorimotor hand impairment. <150g on the hand, remote actuation in a backpack/wheelchair-mounted module. Demonstrated functional benefit in spinal cord injury adults and children.

## Key specs
- Hand module: <150g
- Back module: 720g (motors, power, controllers; backpack or wheelchair mount)
- Fingertip force: 5N per finger
- Lift capability: objects up to 0.5-liter water bottles
- Fabrication: 3D-printed + laser cutting
- Actuation: remote with 3-layered compliant sliding spring mechanism
- Thumb: actuated for opposition and lateral grasping

## Research directions
- Unsupervised home-based therapy via mobile apps
- Intention detection for user control
- Pediatric optimization (PEXO variant)
- Multi-population clinical evaluation

## Results
"Immediate functional benefit in adults after spinal cord injury" and children with severe hand impairment.
