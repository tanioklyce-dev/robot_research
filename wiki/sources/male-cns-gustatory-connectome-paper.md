# The complete gustatory connectome of adult Drosophila reveals how taste guides feeding, foraging, and social behavior (Tastekin et al.; Cell 2026)

## Summary

**A complete sensory-to-motor system in one map.** The first full wiring diagram of the adult male gustatory system: gustatory receptor neurons reconstructed *"from peripheral organs in a contiguous electron microscopy volume spanning brain, cervical connective, and ventral nerve cord,"* integrated with existing datasets into a *"pan-CNS, cross-sex connectome."* The authors *"mapped all feeding motor neurons and traced complete sensory-to-motor pathways to feeding, foraging, endocrine, and social behavior circuits,"* finding distinct circuits for nutrient assessment, motor control, neuroendocrine regulation and courtship. Of the three companions this is the one closest to the wiki's interest: it is a worked example of the *"eyes to legs in one go"* promise of an intact neck connective — here tongue to pharynx — the loop a brain-to-body [fly simulation](../concepts/bio/biomechanical-simulation.md) would have to close.
