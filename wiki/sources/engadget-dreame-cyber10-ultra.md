# Engadget on the Dreame Cyber 10 Ultra

## Summary

CES 2026 hands-on coverage of the [Dreame Cyber 10 Ultra](../entities/dreame-cyber10-ultra.md), a robot vacuum with an extendable arm. **This is a secondary source, because no Dreame primary could be found.** Guessed product URLs returned 404, and the first 250 listings of Dreame's US store on 2026-11-08 contained no Cyber product. Whether it has gone on sale is unconfirmed.

## Key claims

- **Arm.** Lifts up to 500 grams (Engadget, Tom's Guide) and extends about 33 cm (Tom's Guide). It can also take vacuum nozzles and brushes from the base station.
- **Demonstration.** At CES it picked up balls and placed them in a basket. Neither outlet saw heavier objects lifted.
- **Timing and price.** Dreame was targeting August 2026 at about €1,799, or about $2,100 (Engadget). Tom's Guide reported late summer and about $2,000. Neither is an announced final price.
