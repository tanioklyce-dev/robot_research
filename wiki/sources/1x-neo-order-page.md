# 1X NEO order page

## Summary

1X's checkout page for [NEO](../entities/1x-neo.md), retrieved 2026-11-08. It is the primary for price and delivery wording: **$20,000 to own or $499 a month, a $200 refundable deposit, and "US Deliveries start 2026."** It also says plainly that early owners get limited autonomy, and that complex tasks use a remote 1X operator at scheduled times.

## Key claims

- **Price.** Early Access: $20,000, with a 3-year warranty, premium support and priority delivery. Standard: $499 a month, a starter productivity package and standard delivery.
- **Deposit and timing.** $200, "fully refundable." "US Deliveries start 2026." No date is given for any individual order.
- **Autonomy.** NEO "arrives with basic autonomy for early owners and grows in capability overtime." The [product page](1x-neo-product-page.md) likewise says Early Access "offers foundational autonomy."
- **Scheduled Expert Mode.** "For complex tasks NEO doesn't know, an Expert from 1X can remotely supervise its actions at scheduled times." The page's own example is laundry.
- **Hardware.** 5'6", 66 lb, lift 154 lb, carry 55 lb, **arm payload 18 lb**. DOF: hands 22×2, arms 7×2, neck 3, spine 2, legs 6×2. 842 Wh, 4 h run-time.
