## Summary
Product page for Sprout Creator Edition, Fauna Robotics' humanoid developer platform. 107 cm, 22.7 kg, 29 DOF, powered by NVIDIA Jetson AGX Orin 64GB. NYC-based, American-assembled. Has a linked arXiv technical report.

## Key specs
- Height: 107 cm; Weight: 22.7 kg
- DOF: 29 (6 DOF arms, 5 DOF legs, 2 DOF neck)
- Compute: NVIDIA Jetson AGX Orin 64GB + 1 TB SSD
- Battery: 3–3.5 hours, swappable
- Vision: ZED 2i stereoscopic cameras + 4 time-of-flight sensors

## Capabilities
- Compliant locomotion with balanced movement
- Whole-body teleoperation; floor-to-countertop reach; integrated grippers
- Modular autonomy: navigation, mapping, human awareness
- Social engagement: interactive behaviors, conversational interaction, dynamic facial expressions
- Safety: soft materials, no pinch points, compliant motor control

## Pricing
Not listed. "Request Robot" form.

## Company
Fauna Robotics — designed in NYC; assembled in America. Mission: "Building robots that operate safely alongside people." Has arXiv technical report available.
