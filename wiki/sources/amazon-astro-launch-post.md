# Amazon — Meet Astro, a home robot unlike any other

## Summary

Amazon's launch post for [Astro](../entities/amazon-astro.md), read on 2026-11-08 and referenced by URL. It describes a wheeled home-monitoring and Alexa robot, with no arm, and sets out Amazon's privacy design. These are the company's descriptions, not test results. The date comes from CNBC's account of the September 2021 launch event.

## Key claims

- **Jobs.** Remote home checks through a live view and periscope camera, alerts about unrecognized people, reminders and calls for relatives, and Alexa that follows you around the home.
- **Ring.** With a Ring Protect Pro subscription, Astro can patrol, investigate events and save clips to Ring cloud storage.
- **Autonomy.** SLAM-based navigation around changing homes, and "visual ID" so it can find a specific person.
- **Safety.** Sensors and a dedicated safety processor detect stairs and obstacles.
- **Privacy design.** Much processing is on the device, and visual ID is stored there. Owners can mark out-of-bounds zones. One button turns off cameras, microphones and motion. An indicator shows when video or audio is streaming to the cloud.
- **Price.** $1,449.99, or $999.99 as an introductory Day 1 Editions price with six months of Ring Protect Pro. Sold in limited quantities by invitation, in the US.
- **Testing.** "Hundreds of thousands of hours" of internal testing.
