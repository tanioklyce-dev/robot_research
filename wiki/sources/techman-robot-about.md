## Summary

**[Techman Robot](../entities/techman-robot.md)** is a Taiwan-based collaborative-robot (cobot) manufacturer, a subsidiary of **Quanta** (the world's largest contract laptop maker). Its defining product distinction: it was the **first cobot line on the market with a built-in vision system** — Techman markets itself as the only robotics company to natively integrate **AI + vision + hardware** in one arm ("Native AI Engine + Robotic Arm + Vision System"). By 2018 it was the **world's second-largest cobot brand**, a position it still held in 2021 with ~10% market share behind [Universal Robots](../concepts/robotics/collaborative-robots.md)' ~50%.

## Key claims

- **Founded 2015** (the predecessor robotics lab launched 2012 inside Quanta); HQ in **Taoyuan, Taiwan** (Hwa Ya Technology Park). The **only cobot manufacturer based in Taiwan**.
- **Parent company Quanta** — established as a subsidiary of **Quanta Storage Inc.**, operating under Quanta Computer.
- **First commercial product, the TM5, shipped end of 2016.** TM5 won Red Dot Design Award (2018), iF Design Award (2018), and Taiwan Excellence recognition.
- **Market position**: world's #2 cobot brand by 2018; **~10% market share vs. Universal Robots' ~50%** as of 2021.
- **Product lines**: TM Robot Series (built-in vision), TM Operator Series, TM Smart Factory Series, TM Xplore.
- **Omron partnership**: Japanese automation firm **Omron acquired a 10% stake in 2021** (building on a 2018 MoU to co-promote cobots).
- **Distribution**: 100+ distributors across China, Europe, Japan, South Korea, and Southeast Asia; overseas branches in Shanghai, Shenzhen, Chongqing, Busan, and Alblasserdam (Netherlands).
