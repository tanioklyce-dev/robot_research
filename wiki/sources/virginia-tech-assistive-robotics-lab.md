## Summary
Lab profile for the Assistive Robotics Lab at Virginia Tech, directed by Prof. Alan Asbeck. Focuses on exoskeletons, soft robotics, haptics, and human motion sensing for disabled and rehabilitation populations.

## Research focus
1. Human interfaces for mechanical/electrical systems
2. High-efficiency actuators and transmissions
3. Human biomechanics and body control
4. Sensors and control for assistive device integration

## Domains
Exoskeletons, human motion sensing, haptic feedback, soft robotics.
