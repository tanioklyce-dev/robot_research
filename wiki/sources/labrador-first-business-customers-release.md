# Labrador Systems announces first business customers

## Summary

Company press release, read on 2026-11-08 and referenced by URL. It announces **reservations**, not deliveries, for the Retriever Pro, a care-provider version sold as a service. Later coverage turned this into completed deployments.

## Key claims

- **Named organizations.** "Set to receive the first deliveries": On Lok PACE, Nationwide Insurance, Masonic Homes of California, Western Homes Communities, Eskaton, The Perfect Companion, Presbyterian Villages of Michigan, University of Michigan Flint and Graceworks Lutheran Services.
- **Earlier pilots.** Pilot robots had been placed in the homes of a mix of individuals. The release gives no count or measured outcomes.
- **Model.** Fleets offered as a service, with features for caregivers and staff.
- **Backers.** SOSV/HAX, Amazon's Alexa Fund, iRobot Ventures, Grep VC and SkyRiver Ventures.

> [!warning] Contradiction — reserved or delivered?
> [TechCrunch's report](https://techcrunch.com/2022/11/03/labrador-systems-deploys-its-first-assistive-eldercare-robots/) said Labrador had begun deploying the Pro, and a [May 2026 roundup](https://the-gadgeteer.com/2026/05/20/home-robots-available-2026/) says first fleet deliveries "have gone to care providers." This release says only reservations and organizations "set to receive." [Labrador's availability page](labrador-systems-site.md) later set production for Q2 2024. No primary source found here confirms delivery.
